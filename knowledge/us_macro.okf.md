# US Macro Trading Rules

## Rule 1: Fed Rate Hikes
- **Condition:** Federal Reserve increases interest rates.
- **Action:** Bearish on Equities (SPY), Bullish on Yields.

## Rule 2: Inflation Data (CPI)
- **Condition:** CPI prints above expectations.
- **Action:** Bearish on Equities, Bullish on USD.

## Rule 3: Non-Farm Payrolls (NFP)
- **Condition:** NFP prints significantly above expectations.
- **Action:** Bearish on Bonds, Bullish on USD.
