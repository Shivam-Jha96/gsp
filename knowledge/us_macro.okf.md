# United States Macro Trading Rules

## Rule 1: Federal Reserve Rate Hikes Amid Stubborn Inflation
- **Condition:** Federal Reserve increases interest rates in response to persistent or accelerating consumer price inflation.
- **Action:** Bearish on Equities (SPY), Bullish on Yields (UST), and Bullish on USD.

## Rule 2: Above-Expectations Consumer Price Index (CPI) Prints
- **Condition:** Monthly or annual CPI prints above consensus expectations, signaling entrenched inflation pressures.
- **Action:** Bearish on Equities (SPY), Bullish on the US Dollar (USD), and Bearish on long-duration Fixed Income (TLT).

## Rule 3: Robust Non-Farm Payrolls (NFP) and Labor Market Resilience
- **Condition:** NFP prints significantly above expectations or unemployment remains persistently low, fueling wage pressures.
- **Action:** Bearish on Bonds (Yields rise on anticipated Fed tightening), and Bullish on USD.

## Rule 4: Structural Economic Shocks from Trade and Tariffs
- **Condition:** Implementation or escalation of broad US tariffs (e.g., auto sector or clean energy policy rollbacks) impacting domestic supply chains and regional GDP.
- **Action:** Bearish on cyclical Equities and industrial sectors, Bullish on defensive assets and USD safe-haven flows.

## Rule 5: Productivity-Driven Growth Divergence
- **Condition:** Technological shifts, such as sustained private-sector AI investments, offset traditional monetary policy drags to keep baseline US economic growth in gear.
- **Action:** Bullish on secular growth and tech-heavy Equities (QQQ) despite restrictive Fed policy rates.
