# United States Macro Trading Rules

## Rule 1: Federal Reserve Rate Lift-Off and Policy Re-Engagement
- **Condition:** The Federal Reserve approves a quarter-point interest rate hike—its first in three years—and signals further tightening ahead as officials monitor persistent inflation, resisting political pressure for easing.
- **Action:** Bullish on the US Dollar (USD), Bullish on Short-End Treasury Yields, Bearish on Broad Equities (SPY), and Bearish on Long-Duration Fixed Income (TLT).

## Rule 2: Persistent Consumer Price Acceleration and Three-Year Highs
- **Condition:** U.S. consumer prices and headline CPI accelerate toward or above 3-year highs exceeding 4%, driven by persistent inflation prints that lock in aggressive Federal Reserve rate hikes.
- **Action:** Bullish on USD, Bullish on Treasury Inflation-Protected Securities (TIPS), Bearish on Long-Duration Treasuries (TLT), and Bearish on High-Multiple Growth Equities.

## Rule 3: Yield Curve Pressures and Sovereign Fiscal Deficit Focus
- **Condition:** Sovereign debt market dynamics and surging fiscal funding requirements eclipse standard inflation data, causing sharp repricing across the yield curve and duration sell-offs.
- **Action:** Bearish on Core Aggregate Fixed Income (AGG), Bearish on Long-Duration Yield Spreads, and Bullish on Cash and Ultra-Short T-Bills (BIL).

## Rule 4: Productivity Divergence via AI and Digital Infrastructure Capex
- **Condition:** Sustained private-sector capital expenditures in artificial intelligence compute and enterprise tech platforms successfully buoy baseline U.S. economic growth despite broader monetary tightening.
- **Action:** Bullish on Large-Cap Tech Equities (QQQ) and Semiconductor Equities (SMH), Bearish on Capital-Intensive Small-Caps (IWM) and Traditional Non-Tech Defensive Value.

## Rule 5: Protectionist Trade Tariffs and North American Supply Chain Friction
- **Condition:** Imposition or escalation of U.S. tariffs on integrated manufacturing sectors (such as the Canadian automotive industry) raise import costs and threaten regional industrial output and employment.
- **Action:** Bearish on Cyclical Industrials (XLI) and Automotive Equities, Bearish on Trade-Partner Currencies (CAD), and Bullish on the US Dollar (USD).
