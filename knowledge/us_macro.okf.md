# United States Macro Trading Rules

## Rule 1: Federal Reserve Monetary Tightening and Rate Lift-Off
- **Condition:** The Federal Reserve approves policy rate hikes and signals additional tightening to combat persistent inflation, resisting political pressure for monetary easing.
- **Action:** Bullish on the US Dollar (USD), Bullish on Short-End Treasury Yields (2Y UST), Bearish on Broad Equities (SPY), and Bearish on Long-Duration Fixed Income (TLT).

## Rule 2: Persistent Consumer Price Pressures and Elevated Inflation
- **Condition:** Headline and core CPI prints accelerate or hold stubbornly above target (~3.4% annualized), driven by persistent service costs and elevated energy inputs that lock in hawkish central bank policy.
- **Action:** Bullish on USD, Bullish on Treasury Inflation-Protected Securities (TIPS), Bearish on Long-Duration Treasuries (TLT), and Bearish on High-Multiple Growth Equities.

## Rule 3: Yield Curve Inversion and Sovereign Debt Repricing
- **Condition:** Short-term benchmark yields surge faster than long-term yields as the terminal policy rate is repriced upward, driving deep yield curve inversion and sovereign debt duration sell-offs.
- **Action:** Bearish on Curve Steepener Spreads (2Y/10Y Inversion bias), Bearish on Core Aggregate Fixed Income (AGG), and Bullish on Cash and Ultra-Short T-Bills (BIL).

## Rule 4: Productivity Divergence via AI and Enterprise Technology Capex
- **Condition:** Aggressive private-sector capital expenditure in artificial intelligence compute, software, and semiconductor infrastructure buoys baseline GDP growth independently of broader monetary tightening.
- **Action:** Bullish on Large-Cap Tech Equities (QQQ) and Semiconductor Equities (SMH), Bearish on Capital-Intensive Small-Caps (IWM) and Traditional Non-Tech Defensive Value.

## Rule 5: Elevated Energy Prices and Upstream Commodity Pressures
- **Condition:** Crude oil and refined product prices hold elevated due to structural supply constraints or geopolitical friction, sustaining upstream input costs and pass-through headline inflation.
- **Action:** Bullish on Energy Equities (XLE) and Energy Commodities (WTI/Brent Crude), Bearish on Consumer Discretionary (XLY) and Transportation Equities.

## Rule 6: Protectionist Trade Tariffs and Cross-Border Supply Friction
- **Condition:** Imposition or escalation of cross-border trade tariffs on integrated manufacturing sectors (such as North American automotive supply chains), raising import costs and threatening industrial output.
- **Action:** Bearish on Cyclical Industrials (XLI) and Automotive Equities, Bearish on Trade-Partner Currencies (CAD, MXN), and Bullish on the US Dollar (USD).

## Rule 7: Discretionary Margin Compression and Consumer Strain
- **Condition:** Cumulative inflation and high borrowing costs outpace real disposable income gains, constraining consumer expenditure in retail, dining, and non-essential services.
- **Action:** Bearish on Consumer Discretionary (XLY) and Restaurant/Hospitality Equities, Bullish on Consumer Staples (XLP) and Healthcare (XLV).
