# United Kingdom Macro Trading Rules

## Rule 1: Conditional Hawkish Hold and Front-End Rate Repricing
- **Condition:** The Bank of England holds the Bank Rate at 3.75% while explicitly conditioning a late-cycle Q4 rate hike on sustained energy import inflation and Middle East geopolitical escalation.
- **Action:** Bearish on 2Y UK Gilts and short-dated SONIA futures; bullish on front-month SONIA implied volatility and 1Y forward rate agreements (FRAs).

## Rule 2: Disinflationary Print Relief and Tactical Dovish Retracement
- **Condition:** Monthly headline CPI decelerates to 3.1%, offering transient disinflation relief that fractures MPC consensus for an immediate rate hike despite elevated household cost-of-living metrics.
- **Action:** Bullish on short-dated UK Gilts and SONIA futures contracts; bearish on GBP short-term yield spreads against EUR and USD.

## Rule 3: Energy Pass-Through and Gilt Breakeven Widening
- **Condition:** Global supply chain disruptions sustain an imported energy price shock, driving inflation expectations higher while real household disposable income contracts.
- **Action:** Bullish on UK 5Y and 10Y inflation-linked Gilts (breakevens) and FTSE 100 integrated energy producers; bearish on FTSE 250 Consumer Discretionary equities.

## Rule 4: Sovereign Debt Supply and Maturity Shortening Premia
- **Condition:** Persistent public sector net borrowing warnings from fiscal authorities coincide with the UK Debt Management Office (DMO) shortening issuance maturity to absorb deficit supply.
- **Action:** Bearish on 10Y and 30Y nominal Gilts; bullish on UK Gilt yield curve steepeners (2s10s and 5s30s) and long-end asset swap spreads.

## Rule 5: Cyclical Growth Resilience and Delay in Rate Cut Discounting
- **Condition:** Monthly or quarterly UK GDP prints deliver surprise expansions, reflecting underlying domestic resilience that forces money markets to price out near-term monetary easing.
- **Action:** Bullish on UK front-end real yields and GBP/EUR; bearish on duration-sensitive long-dated sovereign bonds.

## Rule 6: Mortgage Refinancing Cliff and Household Credit Impairment
- **Condition:** The persistence of the 3.75% Bank Rate transmits into maturing fixed-rate mortgages, driving borrowing costs higher and accelerating debt-service ratios for households.
- **Action:** Bearish on UK residential homebuilders, domestic retail banks, and commercial property REITs; bullish on defensive UK regulated utilities and non-discretionary consumer staples.

## Rule 7: Cabinet Resignations and Political Risk Discounting
- **Condition:** High-profile cabinet resignations inject domestic political volatility into fiscal policy expectations while the Bank Rate remains elevated.
- **Action:** Bearish on GBP/USD and domestic UK corporate credit spreads; bullish on USD-earning multinational FTSE 100 exporters.

## Rule 8: Resource-Exporting Multinationals vs. Domestic Mid-Cap Divergence
- **Condition:** Global commodity benchmarks stay supported by geopolitical supply risk while domestic UK economic activity is constrained by elevated credit costs and tax burdens.
- **Action:** Bullish on the FTSE 100 / FTSE 250 equity spread (long FTSE 100, short FTSE 250); bearish on UK small-cap domestic industrials.
