# Japan Macro Trading Rules

## Rule 1: BOJ Yield Curve Control (YCC)
- **Condition:** Bank of Japan tightens or removes yield curve control.
- **Action:** Bullish on JPY, Bearish on Nikkei 225.

## Rule 2: JPY Intervention
- **Condition:** Ministry of Finance intervenes to support the Yen.
- **Action:** Bullish on JPY, Bearish on exporters.

## Rule 3: BOJ Rate Decision
- **Condition:** BOJ raises interest rates from ultra-low levels.
- **Action:** Bearish on Nikkei 225, Bullish on JPY.
