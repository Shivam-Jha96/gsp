# Japan Macro Trading Rules

## Rule 1: BOJ Monetary Normalization, Ultra-Long Yield Spikes, and Fiscal Backstop Friction
- **Condition:** The Bank of Japan advances policy rate hikes and quantitative tightening while ultra-long JGB yields test multi-decade highs, stoking political friction over sovereign debt-servicing costs and sparking market speculation of emergency central bank bond-buying operations.
- **Action:** Bearish on 20Y-40Y JGBs, Bullish on Japanese mega-cap banking equities, Bullish on JGB 10Y-30Y yield-curve steepeners.

## Rule 2: Persistent FX Carry Divergence and Asymmetric Intervention Catalysts
- **Condition:** Widened global interest rate differentials keep the yen suppressed near multi-decade lows despite firmer underlying Japanese inflation prints, intensifying Ministry of Finance (MoF) verbal and physical currency intervention risk alongside disorderly carry-trade unwind potential.
- **Action:** Bullish on tactical spot JPY and long JPY volatility (gamma), Bearish on unhedged export-heavy Nikkei momentum in the immediate window of official FX intervention.

## Rule 3: Subsidy-Distorted Headline CPI vs. Imported Commodity Cost-Push
- **Condition:** Headline inflation cools due to state utility and fuel subsidies, while underlying core CPI re-accelerates under the pressure of higher imported crude oil prices, geopolitical shipping shocks, and persistent weak-yen pass-through.
- **Action:** Bullish on general trading companies (*sogo shosha*) and resource-linked value equities, Bearish on import-reliant domestic consumer staples and margin-squeezed small-and-medium enterprises.

## Rule 4: Capex-Led Industrial Modernization vs. Stagnant Real Consumption
- **Condition:** Headline real GDP growth misses consensus due to sluggish household real purchasing power and weak private consumption, while corporate capital expenditure (capex) demonstrates upward revisions driven by domestic reshoring, automation, and capacity upgrades.
- **Action:** Bullish on domestic factory automation, industrial machinery, and capex-beneficiary equities, Bearish on domestic mass-market retail and consumer discretionary cyclicals.

## Rule 5: TSE Governance Mandates and Structural AI/Semiconductor Inflows
- **Condition:** Domestic equities benefit from structural foreign and institutional capital inflows propelled by Tokyo Stock Exchange governance directives (cross-shareholding unwinds, record share buybacks, and ROE optimization) alongside direct capital deployment into Japan's semiconductor supply chain and AI infrastructure ecosystem.
- **Action:** Bullish on semiconductor manufacturing equipment (SME) leaders, advanced silicon materials suppliers, and high-ROE TOPIX quality leaders, Bearish on low-margin governance laggards with persistent price-to-book ratios below 1.0x.
