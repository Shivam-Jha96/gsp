# Japan Macro Trading Rules

## Rule 1: BOJ Monetary Normalization, Ultra-Long Yield Spikes, and Fiscal Backstop Friction
- **Condition:** The Bank of Japan proceeds with policy rate hikes and quantitative tightening while ultra-long JGB yields surge to multi-decade highs, stoking Ministry of Finance concerns over sovereign debt-servicing costs and triggering speculation of emergency central bank purchase operations.
- **Action:** Bullish on 10Y-30Y JGB yield-curve steepeners and Japanese mega-cap banking equities, Bearish on 20Y-40Y JGB paper and rate-sensitive J-REITs.

## Rule 2: FX Carry Divergence, Inflation Disconnect, and MoF Intervention Asymmetry
- **Condition:** The yen remains anchored near multi-decade lows despite domestic policy rates reaching multi-decade highs and core inflation prints firming, driven by wide absolute cross-border rate differentials and sparking asymmetric Ministry of Finance currency intervention risk.
- **Action:** Bullish on tactical spot JPY, long JPY implied volatility (gamma), and short USD/JPY risk reversals, Bearish on unhedged export-heavy Nikkei momentum and yen-funded G10 carry trades.

## Rule 3: Subsidy-Masked Headline CPI vs. Imported Energy Cost-Push
- **Condition:** State energy and utility subsidies artificially compress headline CPI prints while underlying core inflation re-accelerates due to imported crude oil shocks, geopolitical freight disruptions, and weak-currency import pass-through.
- **Action:** Bullish on general trading companies (*sogo shosha*), domestic inflation-linked bonds (JGBi), and upstream commodity plays, Bearish on import-dependent consumer staples and utilities lacking automatic fuel-cost pass-through provisions.

## Rule 4: Domestic Automation and Reshoring Capex vs. Real Household Consumption Drag
- **Condition:** Real GDP growth prints miss expectations due to persistent declines in real wages and sluggish private household consumption, while corporate capital expenditure accelerates on factory automation, domestic supply-chain reshoring, and facility upgrades.
- **Action:** Bullish on factory automation equipment, specialized industrial machinery, and domestic engineering contractors, Bearish on domestic mass-market retail and consumer discretionary cyclicals.

## Rule 5: TSE Governance Mandates and Structural Semiconductor Supply-Chain Inflows
- **Condition:** The Tokyo Stock Exchange enforces capital efficiency directives (PBR < 1.0x remediations, cross-shareholding unwinds, and ROE targets) concurrently with institutional capital deployment into Japan's semiconductor manufacturing equipment and AI infrastructure ecosystem.
- **Action:** Bullish on semiconductor manufacturing equipment (SME) leaders, advanced silicon materials suppliers, and high-ROE TOPIX quality leaders, Bearish on low-governance corporate holdouts with stagnant cash balances and sub-1.0x book values.

## Rule 6: Hawkish Policy Overhang, Rate-Hike Fears, and Growth Factor De-Rating
- **Condition:** Resilience in underlying GDP or hawkish BOJ forward guidance heightens expectations of accelerated monetary tightening, prompting valuation compression on high-multiple equities and triggering violent intraday selloffs in growth indices despite positive economic headlines.
- **Action:** Bullish on TOPIX Value relative to TOPIX Growth and short-duration cash-rich balance sheets, Bearish on high-PE Nikkei 225 growth equities and long-duration speculative technology.
