# Japan Macro Trading Rules

## Rule 1: BOJ Monetary Normalization, Ultra-Long Yield Spikes, and Emergency Backstop Friction
- **Condition:** The Bank of Japan pursues policy rate hikes and quantitative tightening while ultra-long JGB yields reach multi-decade highs amid fiscal tensions, political resistance over rising debt-servicing costs, and heightened market speculation of emergency central bank bond-buying operations.
- **Action:** Bearish on 20Y-40Y JGBs, Bullish on Japanese mega-cap banking equities, Bullish on JGB 10Y-30Y yield-curve steepeners.

## Rule 2: Persistent FX Carry Divergence and Asymmetric Intervention Catalysts
- **Condition:** JPY remains pinned near multi-decade lows (USD/JPY elevated near multi-decade highs) as wide global interest rate differentials cause firmer core inflation prints to fail to lift the yen, triggering sharp Ministry of Finance (MoF) verbal or direct currency intervention and rapid carry-trade unwinds.
- **Action:** Bullish on tactical spot JPY and long JPY volatility (gamma), Bearish on unhedged export-heavy Nikkei momentum in the immediate window of official FX intervention.

## Rule 3: Subsidy-Distorted Headline CPI vs. Imported Energy Cost-Push
- **Condition:** Headline inflation cools due to government utility and energy tariff subsidies, while underlying core CPI re-accelerates from imported crude oil price spikes, geopolitical freight disruptions, and persistent currency depreciation pass-through.
- **Action:** Bullish on general trading companies (*sogo shosha*) and resource-linked value equities, Bearish on import-reliant domestic consumer staples and margin-squeezed small-and-medium enterprises.

## Rule 4: Capex-Driven Output Expansion vs. Household Consumption Fragility
- **Condition:** Real GDP prints miss consensus expectations due to stagnant household real purchasing power, while corporate fixed investment (capex) and industrial modernization remain resilient.
- **Action:** Bullish on domestic factory automation, industrial machinery, and capex-beneficiary equities, Bearish on domestic mass-market retail and consumer discretionary cyclicals.

## Rule 5: TSE Governance Mandates and Structural AI/Semiconductor Inflows
- **Condition:** Domestic equity benchmarks sustain long-term structural tailwinds driven by Tokyo Stock Exchange governance directives (cross-shareholding liquidations, accelerated share buybacks, ROE optimization) alongside foreign institutional capital allocations into Japan's semiconductor supply chain and AI infrastructure ecosystem.
- **Action:** Bullish on semiconductor manufacturing equipment (SME) leaders, advanced materials/silicon wafer suppliers, and high-ROE TOPIX quality leaders, Bearish on low-margin governance laggards with persistent price-to-book ratios below 1.0x.
