# India Macro Trading Rules

## Rule 1: RBI Repo Rate
- **Condition:** RBI increases repo rate.
- **Action:** Bearish on NIFTY 50, Bullish on INR.

## Rule 2: Monsoon Output
- **Condition:** Below average monsoon rainfall.
- **Action:** Bearish on FMCG and Auto sectors, Bullish on inflation expectations.

## Rule 3: FPI Outflows
- **Condition:** Sustained Foreign Portfolio Investment outflows.
- **Action:** Bearish on Indian Equities, Bearish on INR.
