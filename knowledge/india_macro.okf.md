# India Macro Trading Rules

## Rule 1: RBI Monetary Tightening & Yield Curve Bear Flattening
- **Condition:** The RBI MPC initiates its first interest rate-tightening cycle in 44 months, raising the policy repo rate by 25 basis points to 5.50% and shifting its stance to "calibrated tightening" as retail inflation breaches the 4.0% statutory target.
- **Action:** Bullish on short-term money market instruments (91-day/364-day T-Bills), 1-year Overnight Index Swaps (OIS) paying rates, bank term deposits, and large commercial bank Net Interest Margins (NIMs); Bearish on long-duration sovereign bonds (10-year G-Secs), duration-sensitive NBFC balance sheets, and high-multiple growth equities.

## Rule 2: Food & Semiconductor Intermediate Supply Shock Inflation
- **Condition:** Headline retail CPI accelerates above the RBI's 4.0% midpoint target (printing at 4.45% in July with food inflation at 5.52%, and August tracking 20-month highs), driven by price surges in agricultural staples (sugar) and industrial components (semiconductor chips).
- **Action:** Bullish on domestic sugar processors/ethanol distillers, agricultural input suppliers (fertilizers, agrochemicals), and upstream electronic component distributors with pricing power; Bearish on volume-sensitive mass FMCG, entry-level two-wheelers, and margin-compressed consumer durable assemblers.

## Rule 3: Structural FX Depreciation & Capital Account Divergence
- **Condition:** Spot USD/INR experiences persistent upward drift (rupee depreciation) driven by widening cross-border interest rate differentials and Foreign Direct Investment (FDI) falling to multi-year lows, decoupling from strong headline GDP growth.
- **Action:** Bullish on foreign-currency-earning exporters (Tier-1 IT services, generic pharmaceutical exporters, auto ancillaries); Bearish on import-intensive domestic manufacturers, margin-squeezed oil marketing companies (OMCs), and corporates with unhedged External Commercial Borrowings (ECBs).

## Rule 4: Tactical FPI Equity Inflows & Institutional Large-Cap Momentum
- **Condition:** Foreign Portfolio Investors (FPIs) maintain sustained net monthly equity buying across July, August, and September, compounding structural domestic Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) inflows despite broader foreign direct investment stagnation.
- **Action:** Bullish on institutional foreign-flow-sensitive benchmark indices (Nifty 50, Sensex), large-cap private sector banks, and index heavyweights; Bearish on illiquid micro-caps, speculative small-caps, and equities vulnerable to retail liquidity exhaustion.

## Rule 5: Global Supply Chain Realignment & Bilateral Trade Formalization
- **Condition:** Upgraded sovereign trade credentials, high-level bilateral trade negotiations (such as India-US trade talks), and multinational supply chain diversification accelerate formal manufacturing integration.
- **Action:** Bullish on Electronics Manufacturing Services (EMS), contract industrial engineering exporters, multi-modal logistics providers, and modern port operators; Bearish on unorganized low-value textile producers and uncompetitive secondary steel and metal fabricators.
