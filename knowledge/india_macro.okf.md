# India Macro Trading Rules

## Rule 1: RBI Monetary Tightening & Yield Curve Bear Flattening
- **Condition:** The RBI MPC delivers its first repo rate hike in 44 months, raising the policy rate by 25 basis points to 5.50% and shifting its stance to "calibrated tightening" as headline CPI breaches the 4.0% statutory target.
- **Action:** Bullish on short-term money market instruments (91-day/364-day T-Bills), 1-year Overnight Index Swaps (OIS) paying rates, short-tenor commercial paper, and large commercial bank Net Interest Margins (NIMs); Bearish on long-duration sovereign bonds (10-year G-Secs), duration-sensitive NBFCs, and rate-sensitive consumer auto/housing credit volumes.

## Rule 2: Food & Intermediate Supply Shock Inflation Dynamics
- **Condition:** Retail CPI prints above the RBI’s 4.0% midpoint target (July printing at 4.45% with food inflation at 5.52%, and August tracking 20-month highs under the updated index base), driven by cost surges in staple agricultural items, fuel inputs, and industrial intermediate components.
- **Action:** Bullish on domestic agrochemical producers, fertilizer distributors, ethanol distillers, and upstream component distributors with direct pass-through pricing power; Bearish on volume-sensitive mass FMCG manufacturers, margin-compressed consumer durable assemblers, and entry-level two-wheeler manufacturers.

## Rule 3: Structural FX Depreciation & Capital Account Divergence
- **Condition:** Spot USD/INR exhibits persistent upward drift (rupee weakness) as wide cross-border interest rate differentials and multi-year lows in Foreign Direct Investment (FDI) decouple external capital account dynamics from robust domestic GDP growth.
- **Action:** Bullish on USD-earning export sectors (Tier-1 IT services, specialized contract pharmaceutical exporters, export-oriented auto ancillaries); Bearish on import-dependent manufacturers, state-owned Oil Marketing Companies (OMCs) bearing FX and procurement spread compression, and corporates with high unhedged External Commercial Borrowings (ECBs).

## Rule 4: Tactical FPI Momentum & Large-Cap Index Polarization
- **Condition:** Foreign Portfolio Investors (FPIs) maintain sustained net equity purchases across July, August, and September, compounding structural domestic retail SIP flows while long-term foreign direct investment remains depressed.
- **Action:** Bullish on institutional large-cap benchmark indices (Nifty 50, Sensex), blue-chip private sector banks, and liquid index heavyweights; Bearish on illiquid micro-caps, frothy small-cap equities vulnerable to retail liquidity exhaustion, and low-governance speculative counters.

## Rule 5: Global Supply Chain Realignment & Industrial Export Formalization
- **Condition:** Formalized bilateral trade negotiations, validated sovereign trade credentials, and multinational supply-chain de-risking accelerate capital expenditure into domestic assembly ecosystems and modern freight networks.
- **Action:** Bullish on Electronics Manufacturing Services (EMS), precision industrial engineering exporters, multi-modal logistics networks, and commercial port terminal operators; Bearish on low-value unorganized textile weavers, traditional leather goods processors, and secondary metal fabricators facing overseas price competition.
