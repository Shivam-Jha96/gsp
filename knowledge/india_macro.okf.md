# India Macro Trading Rules

## Rule 1: RBI Rate Tightening Cycle & Yield Curve Bear Flattening
- **Condition:** The RBI MPC initiates its first interest rate-tightening cycle in 44 months, raising the policy repo rate as retail CPI persistently breaches the 4.0% statutory medium-term target amid global monetary tightening.
- **Action:** Bearish on long-duration sovereign bonds (G-Secs), duration-sensitive NBFC balance sheets, and high-multiple growth equities; Bullish on short-term money market instruments (91-day/364-day T-Bills), bank term deposits, 1-year Overnight Index Swaps (OIS) paying rates, and large commercial bank Net Interest Margins (NIMs).

## Rule 2: Food & Intermediate Goods Supply Shock Inflation
- **Condition:** Retail CPI inflation accelerates above the RBI's 4.0% median midpoint (printing at 4.45% with food inflation breaching 5.5%), driven by supply-side cost pressures in agricultural staples (sugar) and core industrial components (semiconductor chips).
- **Action:** Bearish on volume-sensitive mass FMCG, entry-level two-wheelers, and margin-compressed consumer durable assemblers; Bullish on domestic sugar processors/distillers, agricultural input suppliers (fertilizers, agrochemicals), and industrial firms with upstream pricing power.

## Rule 3: High-Growth FX Decoupling & Rupee Depreciation Pressure
- **Condition:** Spot USD/INR experiences structural upward drift (INR depreciation) driven by cross-border interest rate differentials and import demand, despite exceptional domestic macroeconomic expansion (real GDP growth holding above 8.2%).
- **Action:** Bullish on foreign-currency-earning exporters (Tier-1 IT services, generic pharmaceutical exporters, auto ancillaries); Bearish on import-intensive domestic manufacturers, margin-squeezed oil marketing companies, and corporates with unhedged External Commercial Borrowings (ECBs).

## Rule 4: Balance of Payments Capital Influx & RBI Reserve Sterilization
- **Condition:** Massive cross-border capital inflows drive the Balance of Payments toward a multi-billion-dollar surplus ($50B+ trajectory), prompting aggressive RBI FX intervention and liquidity sterilization to prevent disorderly currency appreciation.
- **Action:** Bullish on onshore short-dated USD/INR forward premia and sovereign FX reserve assets; Bearish on systemic banking liquidity surpluses and short-tenor commercial paper yields.

## Rule 5: Sustained FPI Equity Inflows & Institutional Large-Cap Momentum
- **Condition:** Foreign Portfolio Investors (FPIs) maintain sustained net monthly equity buying through consecutive quarters (positive net flows across July, August, and September), complementing resilient domestic Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) inflows.
- **Action:** Bullish on foreign-flow-sensitive benchmark indices (Nifty 50, Sensex large-cap constituents) and private banking heavyweights; Bearish on illiquid micro-caps and speculative small-cap equities vulnerable to liquidity withdrawal.

## Rule 6: Global Supply Chain Realignment & Export Manufacturing Formalization
- **Condition:** Institutional trade credential upgrades, bilateral commercial partnerships, and global supply chain diversification away from regional manufacturing hubs drive foreign direct investment into Indian export-oriented production.
- **Action:** Bullish on Electronics Manufacturing Services (EMS), contract industrial engineering exporters, multi-modal logistics providers, and modern port operators; Bearish on low-value unorganized textile producers and uncompetitive secondary steel/metal fabricators.
