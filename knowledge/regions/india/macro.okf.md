# India Macro Trading Rules

## Rule 1: Calibrated Policy Rate Tightening & Systemic Liquidity Drainage
- **Condition:** The RBI enters a calibrated rate-hiking cycle to defend its inflation target and rebalance real policy rates, narrowing systemic liquidity surplus toward neutral or deficit while raising the policy repo rate.
- **Action:** Bullish on 1-year Overnight Index Swaps (OIS) paying positions, ultra-short duration liquidity funds, and high-CASA Tier-1 commercial banks; Bearish on benchmark 10-year G-Secs, corporate bond duration funds, and wholesale-funded non-banking financial companies (NBFCs).

## Rule 2: CPI Target Breaches & New Series Basket Re-Weighting
- **Condition:** Headline retail CPI accelerates toward or breaches the RBI's 6.0% upper tolerance ceiling driven by perishable food supply shocks and domestic fuel costs, while the rollout of revised CPI series weighting shifts the baseline trajectory of core vs. food inflation.
- **Action:** Bullish on domestic agrochemical producers, fertilizer distributors, gold loan NBFCs, and short-dated sovereign T-bills; Bearish on mass-market volume-sensitive FMCG manufacturers, entry-level two-wheeler/PV OEMs, and price-sensitive consumer durable fabricators.

## Rule 3: EBLR Transmission & Bank Deposit Repricing Lag
- **Condition:** Policy rate increases transmit rapidly to floating-rate retail, MSME, and corporate loans pegged to External Benchmark Lending Rates (EBLRs), while term deposit and fixed deposit (FD) costs reprice with a multi-quarter lag, temporarily widening net interest margins (NIMs).
- **Action:** Bullish on Tier-1 mega-cap commercial banks with dominant low-cost retail deposit franchises, asset management companies (AMCs), and wealth platforms; Bearish on standalone retail housing finance companies (HFCs), unhedged vehicle financiers, and liability-constrained mid-tier private banks.

## Rule 4: Sovereign Yield Curve Bear Steepening & Fiscal Supply Pressure
- **Condition:** Sustained sovereign borrowing programs, heavy weekly G-Sec auction supply, and policy rate uncertainty push 10-year yields higher, expanding term premiums and bear-steepening the sovereign yield curve.
- **Action:** Bullish on floating-rate government bonds (FRBs), primary dealer desks with strong market-making spreads, and short-term debt mutual funds; Bearish on long-duration sovereign bond exchange-traded funds (ETFs), 30-year/40-year sovereign paper, and long-dated fixed-rate corporate infrastructure bonds.

## Rule 5: Balance of Payments Strain & Multi-Market FX Interventions
- **Condition:** Persistent merchandise trade deficits, elevated crude import bills, and broad US dollar strength drive USD/INR toward record resistance, triggering RBI spot sales, offshore NDF intervention, and forward book adjustments to manage volatility.
- **Action:** Bullish on USD/INR carry trades via short offshore forward points, Tier-1 IT services exporters, and contract pharmaceutical manufacturers; Bearish on corporate borrowers with unhedged External Commercial Borrowings (ECBs) and import-dependent electronics hardware assemblers.

## Rule 6: Geopolitical Energy Volatility & Regulated Fuel Margin Compression
- **Condition:** Geopolitical disruption in West Asia elevates imported Brent crude oil and LNG prices while domestic retail fuel prices face administrative freezes or excise duty adjustments that compress marketing margins.
- **Action:** Bullish on upstream domestic oil and gas exploration and production (E&P) firms with unhedged crude realization upside, domestic coal extractors, and utility-scale renewable independent power producers (IPPs); Bearish on downstream Oil Marketing Companies (OMCs), commercial aviation carriers, and synthetic specialty chemical and paint manufacturers.

## Rule 7: FPI Capital Flow Volatility vs. Domestic Institutional SIP Absorption
- **Condition:** Foreign Portfolio Investors (FPIs) trigger net equity outflows amid global risk-off sentiment and stretched domestic equity valuations, while domestic mutual fund Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) inflows provide consistent counter-cyclical liquidity absorption.
- **Action:** Bullish on Nifty 50 large-cap bellwethers, domestic equity exchange operators, central securities depositories, and high-dividend public sector enterprises (PSEs); Bearish on richly valued small-and-mid-cap (SMID) equities trading at historical valuation premiums and unprofitable growth technology firms.

## Rule 8: "China+1" Supply-Chain Relocation & PLI Capex Execution
- **Condition:** Multilateral supply-chain reorientation and sovereign disbursements under the Production Linked Incentive (PLI) schemes accelerate greenfield manufacturing capacity, technology transfers, and industrial exports across India.
- **Action:** Bullish on Electronics Manufacturing Services (EMS) providers, precision heavy engineering exporters, multi-modal container rail logistics operators, and private port operators; Bearish on unorganized low-tech textile fabricators, sub-scale bulk commodity chemical producers, and secondary steel producers facing regional dumping.

## Rule 9: Sovereign Capex Push & Resilient Real GDP Formation
- **Condition:** Central and state capital expenditure budgets expand to sustain real GDP growth near 6.9%, driving order book accretion across public infrastructure, railway modernization, and power transmission despite elevated nominal interest rates.
- **Action:** Bullish on domestic EPC infrastructure contractors, electrical transmission and substation equipment manufacturers, and commercial vehicle OEMs; Bearish on low-margin sub-contractors facing delayed municipal payments and non-infrastructure private real estate developers with high working-capital leverage.
