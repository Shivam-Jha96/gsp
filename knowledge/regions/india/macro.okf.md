# India Macro Trading Rules

## Rule 1: Calibrated Policy Rate Tightening & Systemic Liquidity Drainage
- **Condition:** The RBI enters or accelerates a rate-hiking cycle to defend its inflation target and anchor inflation expectations, raising the policy repo rate while draining durable liquidity surpluses via variable rate reverse repo (VRRR) operations and regulatory cash reserve ratio adjustments.
- **Action:** Bullish on 1-year Overnight Index Swaps (OIS) paying positions, ultra-short duration liquidity funds, and high-CASA Tier-1 commercial banks; Bearish on benchmark 10-year G-Secs, long-duration corporate bond funds, and wholesale-funded non-banking financial companies (NBFCs).

## Rule 2: CPI Tolerance Ceiling Breaches Driven by Food & Energy Shocks
- **Condition:** Headline retail CPI breaches or sustains runs near the RBI's 6.0% upper tolerance band, driven by volatile food baskets (vegetables, edible oils, cereals) and imported energy pass-through, forcing the Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) to adopt a decisively hawkish posture.
- **Action:** Bullish on domestic agrochemical producers, fertilizer distributors, gold loan NBFCs, and 91-day sovereign T-bills; Bearish on mass-market volume-sensitive FMCG manufacturers, entry-level two-wheeler OEMs, and discretionary consumer durable fabricators.

## Rule 3: EBLR Transmission & Bank Deposit Repricing Lag
- **Condition:** Policy rate increases transmit immediately to floating-rate retail and MSME loans pegged to External Benchmark Lending Rates (EBLRs), while term deposit and fixed deposit (FD) costs reprice with a multi-quarter lag before triggering aggressive deposit mobilization wars.
- **Action:** Bullish on Tier-1 mega-cap commercial banks with dominant low-cost retail deposit franchises, asset management companies (AMCs), and wealth platforms; Bearish on standalone retail housing finance companies (HFCs), unhedged vehicle financiers, and liability-constrained mid-tier private banks.

## Rule 4: Sovereign Yield Curve Bear Steepening & Fiscal Supply Pressure
- **Condition:** Elevated gross central and state government (SDO) market borrowing programs collide with RBI policy rate tightening and diminishing bank statutory liquidity ratio (SLR) appetite, expanding term premiums across benchmark tenors.
- **Action:** Bullish on floating-rate government bonds (FRBs), primary dealer desks with wide bid-ask market-making spreads, and short-term debt mutual funds; Bearish on long-duration sovereign bond exchange-traded funds (ETFs), 30-year/40-year sovereign paper, and long-dated fixed-rate corporate infrastructure bonds.

## Rule 5: Balance of Payments Strain & Multi-Market FX Interventions
- **Condition:** Widening merchandise trade deficits, elevated imported energy bills, and broad US dollar strength drive USD/INR toward record resistance levels, prompting aggressive RBI spot dollar sales, offshore non-deliverable forward (NDF) interventions, and forward-book rollovers.
- **Action:** Bullish on USD/INR carry trades via short offshore forward points, Tier-1 IT services exporters, and contract pharmaceutical manufacturers; Bearish on corporate borrowers with unhedged External Commercial Borrowings (ECBs) and import-dependent consumer electronics assemblers.

## Rule 6: Geopolitical Energy Volatility & Regulated Fuel Margin Compression
- **Condition:** Heightened geopolitical risks in global energy transit corridors drive international Brent crude and LNG spot prices higher, while domestic retail auto-fuel prices face administrative intervention or excise duty compression.
- **Action:** Bullish on upstream domestic oil and gas exploration and production (E&P) firms with unhedged crude realization upside, domestic coal extractors, and utility-scale renewable independent power producers (IPPs); Bearish on downstream Oil Marketing Companies (OMCs), commercial aviation carriers, and synthetic specialty chemical and paint manufacturers.

## Rule 7: FPI Capital Flow Volatility vs. Domestic Institutional SIP Absorption
- **Condition:** Foreign Portfolio Investors (FPIs) trigger periodic equity outflows driven by global risk-off impulses and valuation premiums, while domestic institutional mutual fund Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) inflows provide continuous counter-cyclical liquidity absorption.
- **Action:** Bullish on Nifty 50 large-cap bellwethers, domestic equity exchange operators, central securities depositories, and high-dividend public sector enterprises (PSEs); Bearish on richly valued small-and-mid-cap (SMID) equities trading at historical valuation premiums and unprofitable growth technology firms.

## Rule 8: "China+1" Supply-Chain Relocation & PLI Capex Execution
- **Condition:** Multilateral supply-chain de-risking and sovereign disbursements under Production Linked Incentive (PLI) schemes accelerate greenfield manufacturing capacity, technology transfers, and industrial goods exports across India.
- **Action:** Bullish on Electronics Manufacturing Services (EMS) providers, precision heavy engineering exporters, multi-modal container rail logistics operators, and private port operators; Bearish on unorganized low-tech textile fabricators, sub-scale bulk commodity chemical producers, and secondary steel producers facing regional dumping.

## Rule 9: Sovereign Capex Push & Industrial GDP Formation
- **Condition:** Central and state capital expenditure budgets expand to anchor real GDP growth above broad emerging market peers, driving order book accretion across public infrastructure, railway modernization, and power transmission corridors despite elevated nominal interest rates.
- **Action:** Bullish on domestic EPC infrastructure contractors, electrical transmission and substation equipment manufacturers, and commercial vehicle OEMs; Bearish on low-margin civil sub-contractors facing delayed municipal disbursements and highly leveraged urban private real estate developers.
