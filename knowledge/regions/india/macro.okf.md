# India Macro Trading Rules

## Rule 1: Calibrated Monetary Tightening & Two-Way Interbank Liquidity Operations
- **Condition:** The RBI MPC executes an initial 25 bps repo rate hike with guidance toward further calibrated tightening (targeting additional hikes toward 5.75%), while deploying active two-way liquidity operations (VRR/VRRR churning) to keep weighted average call money rates aligned strictly with the elevated policy corridor.
- **Action:** Bullish on 1-year and 2-year Overnight Index Swaps (OIS) paying positions, short-tenor Treasury Bills (91-day/182-day), and high-CASA Tier-1 commercial banks; Bearish on benchmark 10-year G-Secs, long-duration bond mutual funds, and wholesale-funded non-banking financial companies (NBFCs).

## Rule 2: CPI Target Overshoot Under Revised 2024=100 Base Series
- **Condition:** Retail headline inflation calculated under the revised 2024=100 base series consistently breaches the RBI's 4.00% midpoint target (accelerating above 4.45%), driven by food basket volatility and sticky service price components.
- **Action:** Bullish on domestic agrochemical producers, fertilizer manufacturers, upstream extractors with contractual cost pass-through capabilities, and inflation-hedged defensive sectors; Bearish on volume-sensitive mass-market FMCG manufacturers, entry-level auto OEMs, and margin-compressed consumer durable fabricators.

## Rule 3: Instantaneous EBLR Transmission & Retail Liability Competition
- **Condition:** Policy rate increases transmit rapidly through External Benchmark Lending Rates (EBLRs) to retail credit and mortgages, lengthening loan tenures and compelling scheduled commercial banks to aggressively bid up fixed deposit rates above 8.00% to fund loan expansion.
- **Action:** Bullish on Tier-1 mega-cap banks with superior low-cost CASA franchises, wealth management platforms, and Asset Management Companies (AMCs) capturing household savings disintermediation; Bearish on standalone retail housing finance companies (HFCs), unhedged vehicle financiers, and liability-constrained mid-tier private lenders.

## Rule 4: Balance of Payments Expansion & Rupee Reserve Cushion
- **Condition:** Heavy net external capital inflows exceeding $50 billion expand sovereign foreign exchange reserves toward the $800 billion threshold, creating a substantial BoP surplus and insulating the Rupee from broad US Dollar appreciation shocks.
- **Action:** Bullish on USD/INR carry trades via short offshore forward points, Indian sovereign credit default swap (CDS) tightening, Tier-1 IT services exporters, and contract research/pharma exporters; Bearish on corporates carrying high unhedged External Commercial Borrowings (ECBs) and import-reliant consumer electronics assemblers.

## Rule 5: Crude Volatility & Energy Import Margin Compression
- **Condition:** Periodic spikes in imported crude oil prices and global energy volatility threaten external stability while domestic pump prices remain politically capped or regulated, widening input cost differentials across the energy complex.
- **Action:** Bullish on upstream domestic oil and gas producers with unhedged realization upside and utility-scale renewable power producers; Bearish on state-owned Oil Marketing Companies (OMCs) facing severe retail marketing margin compression, commercial aviation carriers, and synthetic chemical/paint manufacturers.

## Rule 6: Dual Institutional Flow Divergence & Large-Cap Valuation Primacy
- **Condition:** Positive Foreign Portfolio Investor (FPI) inflows combine with sticky domestic Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) contributions, directing the bulk of institutional liquidity into liquid index bellwethers while SMID valuations face scrutiny under higher cost of capital.
- **Action:** Bullish on Nifty 50 large-cap bellwethers, equity exchange operators, custody banks, and dividend-rich cash-generative PSUs; Bearish on richly valued small-and-mid-cap (SMID) equities trading at historical valuation premiums and unprofitable tech platform startups.

## Rule 7: Sovereign Trade Credential Upgrades & "China+1" Capex Acceleration
- **Condition:** Multilateral upgrades of sovereign trade credentials, expanding bilateral free trade agreements, and supply-chain relocation programs accelerate private greenfield manufacturing investment and export industrial park development.
- **Action:** Bullish on Electronics Manufacturing Services (EMS) providers, precision heavy engineering exporters, multi-modal container rail freight operators, and private deep-water port operators; Bearish on unorganized low-value textile mills, sub-scale bulk commodity chemical producers, and secondary metal fabricators vulnerable to regional import dumping.
