# India Macro Trading Rules

## Rule 1: RBI Calibrated Tightening & Yield Curve Bear-Flattening
- **Condition:** The RBI MPC initiates a rate-tightening cycle by raising the repo rate by 25 bps to 5.50% and shifting stance to "calibrated tightening" to anchor inflation expectations against external volatility and domestic price pressures.
- **Action:** Bullish on 1-year and 2-year Overnight Index Swaps (OIS) paying positions, short-duration Treasury Bills (91-day/182-day), and Tier-1 commercial banks with dominant low-cost CASA ratios; Bearish on benchmark 10-year G-Secs, long-duration debt mutual funds, and wholesale-funded non-banking financial companies (NBFCs).

## Rule 2: Food Basket Volatility & Headline CPI Re-Acceleration
- **Condition:** Headline retail CPI accelerates sharply to 5.54%, driven by food price shocks and vegetable basket volatility, approaching the RBI’s 6.00% upper tolerance threshold and solidifying hawkish policy expectations.
- **Action:** Bullish on domestic agrochemical producers, fertilizer manufacturers, upstream oil & gas exploration entities, and primary commodity producers with contractual cost pass-through capabilities; Bearish on volume-sensitive mass-market FMCG manufacturers, entry-level automotive OEMs, and margin-compressed consumer durable fabricators.

## Rule 3: Commercial Bank Deposit War & Liability Spread Compression
- **Condition:** Systemic liquidity drainage and policy transmission drive commercial bank fixed deposit (FD) rates up to 8.25%, intensifying retail liability competition and triggering Net Interest Margin (NIM) compression across the banking sector.
- **Action:** Bullish on Tier-1 private mega-cap banks possessing structural CASA advantages, wealth management distribution platforms, and Asset Management Companies (AMCs) capturing deposit disintermediation; Bearish on wholesale liability-dependent mid-sized private banks, regional non-bank lenders (NBFCs), and retail mortgage financiers facing asset-liability duration mismatches.

## Rule 4: Sovereign FX Reserve Expansion Toward $800B & Rupee Policy Defense
- **Condition:** The RBI broadens rupee defense mechanisms to include calibrated rate policy alongside spot market interventions, while massive capital inflows and FCNR deposits push sovereign foreign exchange reserves toward $800 billion.
- **Action:** Bullish on Tier-1 IT services exporters, contract pharmaceutical manufacturers, USD/INR carry trades via short offshore forward points, and sovereign credit default swap (CDS) tightening; Bearish on state-owned Oil Marketing Companies (OMCs) subject to retail fuel margin freezes, import-reliant electronics assemblers, and corporates carrying heavy unhedged External Commercial Borrowings (ECBs).

## Rule 5: Dual Institutional Liquidity Surge & Large-Cap Market Hegemony
- **Condition:** Resurgent Foreign Portfolio Investor (FPI) equity inflows converge with resilient, programmatic domestic Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) inflows and above-consensus GDP growth.
- **Action:** Bullish on Nifty 50 mega-cap index bellwethers, domestic investment brokerages and custody banks, and cash-rich public sector undertakings (PSUs) offering high dividend yields; Bearish on high-beta mid-and-small-cap (SMID) growth equities trading at extreme valuation multiples, unprofitable tech platforms, and illiquid thematic equities.

## Rule 6: Trade Credential Upgrades & "China+1" Industrial Capex
- **Condition:** Sovereign trade credential upgrades, bilateral corridor expansion, and multinational supply-chain diversification drive sustained capital expenditure into domestic manufacturing and trade logistics infrastructure.
- **Action:** Bullish on Electronics Manufacturing Services (EMS) providers, specialized heavy engineering exporters, multi-modal rail freight operators, and private commercial port operators; Bearish on unorganized low-value textile producers, sub-scale commodity chemical producers, and secondary metal fabricators vulnerable to low-cost regional dumping.
