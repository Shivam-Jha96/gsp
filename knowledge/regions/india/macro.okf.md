# India Macro Trading Rules

## Rule 1: Calibrated Policy Rate Hikes & Two-Way Interbank Liquidity Steering
- **Condition:** The RBI MPC executes calibrated 25 bps repo rate hikes amid persistent inflation risks, while utilizing alternating fine-tuning operations (simultaneous VRR injections and VRRR absorptions) to anchor the Weighted Average Call Rate (WACR) strictly within the operating policy corridor.
- **Action:** Bullish on 1-year Overnight Index Swaps (OIS) paying positions, ultra-short duration liquidity funds, and high-CASA Tier-1 commercial banks; Bearish on benchmark 10-year G-Secs, corporate bond duration funds, and wholesale-funded non-banking financial companies (NBFCs).

## Rule 2: Food Basket and Precious Metal Shocks Driving CPI Acceleration
- **Condition:** Headline retail CPI accelerates toward the RBI’s 6.0% upper tolerance band (printing between 5.50% and 5.54%, a multi-month high), driven by sharp domestic food supply disruptions (e.g., doubling vegetable prices) alongside elevated imported precious metal costs.
- **Action:** Bullish on domestic agrochemical producers, fertilizer distributors, gold loan NBFCs, and short-duration sovereign treasury bills; Bearish on volume-sensitive mass-market FMCG manufacturers, entry-level passenger vehicle OEMs, and price-sensitive consumer durable fabricators.

## Rule 3: EBLR Transmission Disparity & Deposit Repricing Lag
- **Condition:** Successive repo rate hikes transmit immediately to floating-rate retail and MSME loans via External Benchmark Lending Rates (EBLRs), while commercial bank term deposit rate revisions lag asset yields, temporarily widening net interest margins (NIMs).
- **Action:** Bullish on Tier-1 mega-cap commercial banks with superior low-cost retail deposit franchises, asset management companies (AMCs), and wealth platforms; Bearish on standalone retail housing finance companies (HFCs), unhedged vehicle financiers, and liability-constrained mid-tier private banks.

## Rule 4: Imported Energy Shocks & Downstream Margin Compression
- **Condition:** Elevated global crude oil prices, maritime freight disruptions, and currency pass-through increase imported feedstock costs while domestic retail auto fuel prices remain frozen or heavily regulated by the government.
- **Action:** Bullish on upstream domestic oil and gas exploration and production (E&P) firms with unhedged realization upside, domestic coal extractors, and commercial renewable independent power producers (IPPs); Bearish on state-owned downstream Oil Marketing Companies (OMCs), commercial aviation carriers, and synthetic specialty chemical and paint manufacturers.

## Rule 5: Foreign Portfolio Rebalancing & Domestic Institutional Absorption
- **Condition:** Episodic foreign portfolio investor (FPI) equity rotations and global risk-off liquidations clash with structural, counter-cyclical domestic retail Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) and mutual fund capital inflows.
- **Action:** Bullish on Nifty 50 large-cap bellwethers, domestic equity exchange operators, central securities depositories, and high-dividend public sector enterprises; Bearish on richly valued small-and-mid-cap (SMID) equities trading at historical valuation premiums and unprofitable growth technology firms.

## Rule 6: FX Depreciation Pressure & Active Sovereign Reserve Defense
- **Condition:** Balance of payments (BoP) pressures and broad USD strength push USD/INR toward historical resistance levels, triggering aggressive RBI two-way interventions across onshore spot and offshore forward markets to contain exchange rate volatility.
- **Action:** Bullish on USD/INR carry trades via short offshore forward points, Tier-1 IT services exporters, contract pharmaceutical exporters, and Indian sovereign credit default swap (CDS) tightening; Bearish on corporates carrying unhedged External Commercial Borrowings (ECBs) and import-reliant consumer electronics assemblers.

## Rule 7: Global Supply Chain Reconfiguration & "China+1" Capex Expansion
- **Condition:** Sovereign trade credential validations, multilateral industrial partnerships, and performance-linked incentive (PLI) rollouts accelerate private greenfield manufacturing investment and global supply-chain relocation into India.
- **Action:** Bullish on Electronics Manufacturing Services (EMS) providers, precision heavy engineering exporters, multi-modal container rail logistics operators, and private deep-water port operators; Bearish on unorganized low-value textile mills, sub-scale bulk commodity chemical producers, and secondary metal fabricators facing regional dumping.
