# India Macro Trading Rules

## Rule 1: Calibrated Policy Rate Tightening & Multi-Year Repo Escalation
- **Condition:** The RBI initiates its first interest rate hike in nearly four years, entering a global tightening cycle aimed at taking the repo rate toward 6.25% as quarterly inflation forecasts threaten to breach the 6.0% upper tolerance band and reach 6.3%.
- **Action:** Bullish on 1-year Overnight Index Swaps (OIS) paying positions, ultra-short duration liquidity funds, and high-CASA Tier-1 commercial banks; Bearish on benchmark 10-year G-Secs, corporate bond duration funds, and wholesale-funded non-banking financial companies (NBFCs).

## Rule 2: Food Basket Inflation Surge & Tolerance Band Breaches
- **Condition:** Headline retail CPI accelerates for multiple consecutive months—surging through 5.54%–5.60% toward a projected 6.3% peak—propelled by doubling vegetable and onion prices alongside domestic agricultural supply bottlenecks.
- **Action:** Bullish on domestic agrochemical producers, fertilizer distributors, gold loan NBFCs, and short-duration sovereign treasury bills; Bearish on volume-sensitive mass-market FMCG manufacturers, entry-level passenger vehicle OEMs, and price-sensitive consumer durable fabricators.

## Rule 3: EBLR Rate Reset Transmission & Deposit Repricing Lag
- **Condition:** Sovereign policy rate hikes transmit immediately to floating-rate retail and MSME loans benchmarked to External Benchmark Lending Rates (EBLRs), while commercial bank term deposit costs reprice with a multi-quarter lag, expanding net interest margins (NIMs).
- **Action:** Bullish on Tier-1 mega-cap commercial banks with dominant low-cost retail deposit franchises, asset management companies (AMCs), and wealth management platforms; Bearish on standalone retail housing finance companies (HFCs), unhedged vehicle financiers, and liability-constrained mid-tier private banks.

## Rule 4: Geopolitical Energy Turmoil & Downstream Margin Compression
- **Condition:** Escalating West Asia conflict elevates imported Brent crude oil and refinery feedstock costs while retail auto fuel prices face administrative caps or regulated margin absorption.
- **Action:** Bullish on upstream domestic oil and gas exploration and production (E&P) firms with unhedged realization upside, domestic coal extractors, and commercial renewable independent power producers (IPPs); Bearish on state-owned downstream Oil Marketing Companies (OMCs), commercial aviation carriers, and synthetic specialty chemical and paint manufacturers.

## Rule 5: FPI Multi-Year Flow Fatigue vs. Domestic SIP Absorption
- **Condition:** Foreign Portfolio Investors (FPIs) reduce equity allocations toward multi-year lows driven by valuation fatigue and global risk premia, while domestic systemic investment plan (SIP) inflows and institutional capital provide continuous counter-cyclical liquidity.
- **Action:** Bullish on Nifty 50 large-cap bellwethers, domestic equity exchange operators, central securities depositories, and high-dividend public sector enterprises (PSEs); Bearish on richly valued small-and-mid-cap (SMID) equities trading at extreme valuation multiples and unprofitable growth technology firms.

## Rule 6: Currency Depreciation Pressure & FX Reserve Interventions
- **Condition:** Balance of payments (BoP) strains, elevated energy import bills, and broad US dollar resilience push USD/INR toward historic resistance levels, prompting aggressive RBI two-way interventions across onshore spot and offshore non-deliverable forward (NDF) markets.
- **Action:** Bullish on USD/INR carry trades via short offshore forward points, Tier-1 IT services exporters, contract pharmaceutical exporters, and Indian sovereign credit default swap (CDS) tightening; Bearish on corporate borrowers with unhedged External Commercial Borrowings (ECBs) and import-reliant consumer electronics assemblers.

## Rule 7: "China+1" Greenfield Capex & Institutional Trade Credentialing
- **Condition:** Multilateral industrial trade credentialing and performance-linked incentive (PLI) disbursements accelerate structural supply-chain relocation, cross-border joint ventures, and greenfield manufacturing capacity across India.
- **Action:** Bullish on Electronics Manufacturing Services (EMS) providers, precision heavy engineering exporters, multi-modal container rail logistics operators, and private deep-water port operators; Bearish on unorganized low-value textile mills, sub-scale bulk commodity chemical producers, and secondary metal fabricators facing regional dumping.
