# India Macro Trading Rules

## Rule 1: Policy Rate Tightening & Systemic Liquidity Absorption
- **Condition:** Headline retail CPI breaches the RBI's 6.0% statutory upper tolerance band on the rebased (2024=100) series, prompting the Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) to execute consecutive 25–50 bps repo rate hikes toward a terminal rate of 6.25% or higher while draining banking system liquidity via Variable Rate Reverse Repo (VRRR) auctions.
- **Action:** Bullish on 1-year and 2-year Overnight Index Swaps (OIS) paying positions, 91-day sovereign T-bills, and ultra-short duration liquidity funds; Bearish on 10-year benchmark G-Secs, long-duration debt mutual funds, and wholesale credit-dependent Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs).

## Rule 2: Food Inflation Spikes & Core "Shrinkflation" Propagation
- **Condition:** Perishable agricultural supply shocks drive headline inflation above 6.0%, accelerating corporate "shrinkflation" and structural price pass-through into packaged consumer goods, personal care, and core services.
- **Action:** Bullish on domestic agrochemical producers, fertilizer distributors, gold-loan NBFCs, and large-cap FMCG manufacturers with dominant rural distribution and inelastic pricing power; Bearish on mass-market volume-sensitive consumer durables, entry-level two-wheeler OEMs, and sub-scale quick-service restaurant (QSR) chains.

## Rule 3: Credit-Deposit Imbalance & Commercial Bank NIM Compression
- **Condition:** Commercial bank loan growth structurally outpaces domestic deposit accretion, forcing lenders to aggressively raise retail term deposit rates by 25–50 bps to bridge the funding deficit as marginal cost of funds catches up with EBLR-linked floating asset yields.
- **Action:** Bullish on low Credit-to-Deposit Ratio (CDR) public sector banks with deep low-cost CASA franchises, institutional wealth management platforms, and short-tenor commercial paper; Bearish on high-CDR private commercial banks, standalone housing finance companies (HFCs), and vehicle finance NBFCs.

## Rule 4: Sovereign Yield Curve Bear Flattening & Supply Indigestion
- **Condition:** Record central fiscal borrowing targets converge with front-loaded State Development Loan (SDL) issuances and muted commercial bank Statutory Liquidity Ratio (SLR) absorption amidst monetary policy rate tightening.
- **Action:** Bullish on sovereign floating-rate bonds (FRBs), primary dealer short-end trading books, and low-duration liquid debt instruments; Bearish on 10-year, 30-year, and 40-year nominal sovereign paper, long-dated corporate infrastructure bonds, and duration bond ETFs.

## Rule 5: Balance of Payments Imbalance & Rupee Defense Interventions
- **Condition:** Merchandise trade deficits widen and external portfolio flows wobble, pushing the Indian Rupee toward historical lows despite structural private remittance inflows exceeding USD 135 billion, triggering multi-pronged RBI interventions across spot, onshore forward, and offshore non-deliverable forward (NDF) markets.
- **Action:** Bullish on Tier-1 IT services exporters, contract Active Pharmaceutical Ingredient (API) manufacturers, and USD/INR long carry positions via offshore forward points; Bearish on domestic commercial aviation carriers, corporate issuers with unhedged External Commercial Borrowings (ECBs), and import-dependent consumer electronics assemblers.

## Rule 6: FPI Capital Volatility vs. Counter-Cyclical Domestic SIP Inflows
- **Condition:** Elevated domestic valuation premia or global risk-off events trigger intermittent Foreign Portfolio Investor (FPI) equity liquidations, counterbalanced by persistent, monthly retail Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) mutual fund inflows absorbing secondary market supply.
- **Action:** Bullish on defensive Nifty 50 mega-cap bellwethers, domestic stock exchange operators, central depository service providers (CDSL), and asset management companies (AMCs); Bearish on high-beta small-and-mid-cap (SMID) equities trading at extreme forward P/E multiples and cash-burning consumer internet platforms.

## Rule 7: Imported Hydrocarbon Shocks & Regulated Downstream Margin Squeeze
- **Condition:** Global Brent crude and imported LNG spot prices surge while retail transport fuel pump prices are administratively capped or constrained by state directives to cushion consumer inflation.
- **Action:** Bullish on upstream domestic oil and gas exploration and production (E&P) firms, domestic coal miners, and renewable independent power producers (IPPs); Bearish on downstream Oil Marketing Companies (OMCs), synthetic chemical producers, and industrial paint manufacturers.

## Rule 8: "China+1" Supply Chain Re-Shoring & PLI Greenfield Capex
- **Condition:** Global manufacturing supply chains diversify into India, accelerated by operational central Production Linked Incentive (PLI) disbursements and fast-tracked state industrial infrastructure development.
- **Action:** Bullish on Electronics Manufacturing Services (EMS) contractors, precision engineering exporters, industrial automation providers, and private commercial port operators; Bearish on unorganized low-tech textile fabricators and secondary commoditized steel producers exposed to regional import dumping.

## Rule 9: Sovereign Capital Outlay Expansion & Infrastructure Fixed Capital Formation
- **Condition:** Central and state capital expenditure budgets expand at double-digit annual rates to finance dedicated freight corridors, high-speed rail, power grid modernization, and defense indigenization programs.
- **Action:** Bullish on domestic EPC infrastructure conglomerates, heavy electrical transmission equipment manufacturers, commercial vehicle (HCV) makers, and defense electronics contractors; Bearish on sub-scale municipal subcontractors burdened by elongated working-capital cycles and over-leveraged commercial real estate developers.
