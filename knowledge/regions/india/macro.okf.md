# India Macro Trading Rules

## Rule 1: RBI Calibrated Tightening & Yield Curve Bear-Flattening
- **Condition:** The RBI MPC initiates a rate-tightening cycle by raising the repo rate by 25 bps to 5.50% and shifting its stance to "calibrated tightening" to anchor inflation expectations against global volatility and domestic price pressures.
- **Action:** Bullish on 1-year and 2-year Overnight Index Swaps (OIS) paying positions, short-duration Treasury Bills (91-day/182-day), and Tier-1 private commercial banks with superior low-cost CASA ratios; Bearish on benchmark 10-year G-Secs, long-duration debt mutual funds, and wholesale-funded non-banking financial companies (NBFCs).

## Rule 2: Food Basket Shock & Headline CPI Drift Toward Tolerance Ceiling
- **Condition:** Headline retail CPI accelerates sharply toward the RBI's 6.00% upper tolerance threshold (reaching 5.54%), driven by severe food price shocks, including surging vegetable and onion prices.
- **Action:** Bullish on domestic agrochemical producers, fertilizer manufacturers, upstream oil & gas exploration entities, and primary commodity producers with contractual cost pass-through capabilities; Bearish on volume-sensitive mass-market FMCG manufacturers, entry-level automotive OEMs, and margin-compressed consumer durable fabricators.

## Rule 3: Rupee Depreciation & External Account Friction
- **Condition:** Spot USD/INR exhibits sustained depreciation pressure toward all-time highs amid geopolitical instability in West Asia, elevated crude import bills, and foreign investor caution regarding domestic growth moderation.
- **Action:** Bullish on Tier-1 IT services exporters, US-FDA compliant contract pharmaceutical manufacturers, and export-oriented auto ancillary producers; Bearish on state-owned Oil Marketing Companies (OMCs) burdened by retail fuel margin freezes, import-reliant electronics assemblers, and corporates carrying heavy unhedged External Commercial Borrowings (ECBs).

## Rule 4: Institutional FPI Volatility & Domestic Liquidity Divergence
- **Condition:** Foreign Portfolio Investor (FPI) equity flows remain erratic around multi-year structural lows, while resilient domestic Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) equity inflows provide programmatic downside support.
- **Action:** Bullish on index-heavy mega-cap private banks, domestic Asset Management Companies (AMCs) capturing retail inflows, and cash-rich defensive Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs) offering high dividend yields; Bearish on high-beta mid-and-small-cap growth equities, stretched-multiple consumer discretionary names, and illiquid thematic equities.

## Rule 5: Trade Credential Upgrades & "China+1" Industrial Capex
- **Condition:** Sovereign trade credential upgrades, bilateral corridor expansion, and multinational supply-chain diversification drive sustained capital expenditure into domestic manufacturing and trade logistics infrastructure.
- **Action:** Bullish on Electronics Manufacturing Services (EMS) providers, specialized heavy engineering exporters, multi-modal rail freight operators, and private commercial port operators; Bearish on unorganized low-value textile producers, commodity chemical producers lacking scale, and secondary metal fabricators vulnerable to low-cost regional dumping.
