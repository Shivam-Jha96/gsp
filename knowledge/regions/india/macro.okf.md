# India Macro Trading Rules

## Rule 1: Policy Rate Tightening & Systemic Liquidity Absorption
- **Condition:** The RBI accelerates repo rate hikes by 50–75 basis points toward an elevated terminal rate as retail CPI breaches the 6.0% statutory upper tolerance band, actively mopping up banking liquidity via Variable Rate Reverse Repo (VRRR) operations and regulatory cash reserve mandates.
- **Action:** Bullish on 1-year and 2-year Overnight Index Swaps (OIS) paying positions, 91-day sovereign T-bills, and ultra-short duration liquidity funds; Bearish on 10-year benchmark G-Secs, long-duration debt mutual funds, and wholesale credit-dependent Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs).

## Rule 2: Food Inflation Spikes & Core Basket "Shrinkflation" Propagation
- **Condition:** Perishable food shocks drive headline CPI above the 6.0% upper tolerance ceiling (on the rebasing 2024=100 series), triggering widespread corporate "shrinkflation" and structural price pass-through into core manufacturing, personal care, and consumer services.
- **Action:** Bullish on agrochemical producers, domestic fertilizer distributors, gold-loan NBFCs, and FMCG manufacturers with dominant rural penetration and inelastic pricing power; Bearish on mass-market volume-sensitive consumer durables, entry-level two-wheeler OEMs, and sub-scale quick-service restaurant (QSR) chains.

## Rule 3: Credit-Deposit Imbalance & Commercial Bank NIM Compression
- **Condition:** Commercial bank loan growth outpaces domestic deposit accretion, forcing lenders to aggressively raise retail term deposit rates by 25–50 bps to bridge the funding deficit as marginal cost of funds catches up with EBLR-linked floating asset yields.
- **Action:** Bullish on low Credit-to-Deposit Ratio (CDR) public sector banks with deep low-cost CASA franchises, institutional wealth management platforms, and short-tenor commercial paper; Bearish on high-CDR private commercial banks, standalone housing finance companies (HFCs), and vehicle finance NBFCs.

## Rule 4: Sovereign Yield Curve Bear Flattening & Supply Absorption Strain
- **Condition:** Heavy central fiscal borrowing targets converge with front-loaded State Development Loan (SDL) issuances and muted statutory liquidity ratio (SLR) absorption by commercial banks amid monetary tightening.
- **Action:** Bullish on floating-rate government bonds (FRBs), primary dealer short-end trading books, and low-duration liquid debt instruments; Bearish on 10-year, 30-year, and 40-year nominal sovereign paper, long-dated corporate infrastructure bonds, and duration bond ETFs.

## Rule 5: Balance of Payments Deficit & Rupee Depreciation Interventions
- **Condition:** Widening merchandise trade deficits and lumpy external portfolio outflows weaken the Indian Rupee toward record support levels, prompting multi-pronged RBI intervention across spot, onshore forward, and offshore non-deliverable forward (NDF) markets.
- **Action:** Bullish on Tier-1 IT services exporters, contract Active Pharmaceutical Ingredient (API) manufacturers, and USD/INR long carry positions via offshore forward points; Bearish on domestic commercial aviation carriers, corporate issuers with unhedged External Commercial Borrowings (ECBs), and import-dependent electronics assemblers.

## Rule 6: FPI Capital Rotations vs. Counter-Cyclical Domestic SIP Inflows
- **Condition:** Heightened global risk aversion or domestic valuation premia trigger heavy Foreign Portfolio Investor (FPI) equity liquidations, counterbalanced by persistent retail Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) mutual fund inflows absorbing secondary market supply.
- **Action:** Bullish on defensive Nifty 50 mega-cap bellwethers, domestic stock exchange operators, central depository service providers (CDSL), and asset management companies (AMCs); Bearish on high-beta small-and-mid-cap (SMID) equities trading at excessive earnings multiples and unprofitable consumer internet platforms.

## Rule 7: Imported Hydrocarbon Shocks & Regulated Downstream Margin Squeeze
- **Condition:** Global Brent crude and imported LNG spot prices surge while retail transport fuel pump prices are administratively capped or constrained by state directives to cushion consumer inflation.
- **Action:** Bullish on upstream domestic oil and gas exploration and production (E&P) firms, state coal producers, and renewable independent power producers (IPPs); Bearish on downstream Oil Marketing Companies (OMCs), synthetic chemical producers, and industrial paint manufacturers.

## Rule 8: "China+1" Supply Chain Re-Shoring & PLI Greenfield Capex
- **Condition:** Multinationals accelerate supply chain diversification into South Asia, supported by central Production Linked Incentive (PLI) operational disbursements and state-level industrial park fast-tracking.
- **Action:** Bullish on Electronics Manufacturing Services (EMS) contractors, precision engineering exporters, industrial automation providers, and private commercial port operators; Bearish on unorganized low-tech textile fabricators and secondary commoditized steel producers exposed to regional dumping.

## Rule 9: Sovereign Capital Outlay Expansion & Infrastructure Fixed Capital Formation
- **Condition:** Central and state capital expenditure budgets expand at double-digit annual rates to finance dedicated freight corridors, high-speed rail, power grid modernization, and defense indigenization.
- **Action:** Bullish on domestic EPC infrastructure conglomerates, heavy electrical transmission equipment manufacturers, commercial vehicle (HCV) makers, and defense electronics contractors; Bearish on sub-scale municipal subcontractors burdened by elongated working-capital cycles and over-leveraged commercial real estate developers.
