# India Macro Trading Rules

## Rule 1: Calibrated Policy Rate Tightening & Operating Corridor Anchoring
- **Condition:** The RBI MPC initiates a policy rate-tightening cycle with a 25 bps hike to the repo rate (reaching 5.50%) and explicitly takes near-term rate cuts off the table amid persistent inflation risks, while using fine-tuning liquidity operations (VRR/VRRR) to anchor the Weighted Average Call Rate (WACR) strictly within the corridor.
- **Action:** Bullish on 1-year Overnight Index Swaps (OIS) paying positions, ultra-short duration liquidity funds, and high-CASA Tier-1 commercial banks; Bearish on benchmark 10-year G-Secs, corporate bond duration funds, and wholesale-funded non-banking financial companies (NBFCs).

## Rule 2: Food and Energy Price Shocks Pushing CPI Toward Upper Tolerance Band
- **Condition:** Headline retail CPI accelerates toward the RBI’s 6.0% upper tolerance ceiling, driven by compounded domestic food basket supply shocks alongside imported energy and freight cost escalation.
- **Action:** Bullish on domestic agrochemical producers, fertilizer distributors, gold loan NBFCs, and short-duration sovereign treasury bills; Bearish on volume-sensitive mass-market FMCG manufacturers, entry-level passenger vehicle OEMs, and price-sensitive consumer durable fabricators.

## Rule 3: EBLR Rate Reset Transmission & Deposit Repricing Lag
- **Condition:** Repo rate increases transmit instantaneously to floating-rate retail and MSME credit via External Benchmark Lending Rates (EBLRs), while commercial bank term deposit repricing lags credit yields, temporarily widening net interest margins (NIMs).
- **Action:** Bullish on Tier-1 mega-cap commercial banks with dominant low-cost retail deposit franchises, asset management companies (AMCs), and wealth management platforms; Bearish on standalone retail housing finance companies (HFCs), unhedged vehicle financiers, and liability-constrained mid-tier private banks.

## Rule 4: Geopolitical Energy Disruptions & Regulated Downstream Margins
- **Condition:** Escalating West Asia conflict and maritime supply bottlenecks elevate imported crude oil and feedstock prices while domestic retail auto fuel prices remain frozen or subject to administrative margin caps.
- **Action:** Bullish on upstream domestic oil and gas exploration and production (E&P) firms with unhedged realization upside, domestic coal extractors, and commercial renewable independent power producers (IPPs); Bearish on state-owned downstream Oil Marketing Companies (OMCs), commercial aviation carriers, and synthetic specialty chemical and paint manufacturers.

## Rule 5: FPI Valuation Fatigue & Counter-Cyclical Domestic SIP Absorption
- **Condition:** Foreign Portfolio Investors (FPIs) rotate out of domestic equities due to rich relative valuations and rising global sovereign yields, while domestic retail Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) inflows and institutional capital provide continuous absorption.
- **Action:** Bullish on Nifty 50 large-cap bellwethers, domestic equity exchange operators, central securities depositories, and high-dividend public sector enterprises (PSEs); Bearish on richly valued small-and-mid-cap (SMID) equities trading at historical valuation premiums and unprofitable growth technology firms.

## Rule 6: FX Depreciation Pressure & Active Sovereign Reserve Defense
- **Condition:** Balance of payments (BoP) pressures, imported energy bills, and broad US dollar strength push USD/INR toward historical resistance, triggering active RBI two-way interventions across onshore spot and offshore non-deliverable forward (NDF) markets.
- **Action:** Bullish on USD/INR carry trades via short offshore forward points, Tier-1 IT services exporters, contract pharmaceutical exporters, and Indian sovereign credit default swap (CDS) tightening; Bearish on corporates carrying unhedged External Commercial Borrowings (ECBs) and import-reliant consumer electronics assemblers.

## Rule 7: Global Trade Credentialing & "China+1" Greenfield Capex
- **Condition:** Multilateral industrial trade validations and performance-linked incentive (PLI) rollouts accelerate domestic greenfield manufacturing investment and structural supply-chain relocation into India.
- **Action:** Bullish on Electronics Manufacturing Services (EMS) providers, precision heavy engineering exporters, multi-modal container rail logistics operators, and private deep-water port operators; Bearish on unorganized low-value textile mills, sub-scale bulk commodity chemical producers, and secondary metal fabricators facing regional dumping.
