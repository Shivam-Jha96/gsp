# United States Macro Trading Rules

## Rule 1: Federal Reserve Hawkish Skip-and-Hike Terminal Trajectory
- **Condition:** The Federal Reserve pauses policy rate hikes for a single meeting while explicitly signaling subsequent tightening in upcoming meetings and a prolonged higher-for-longer stance due to resilient economic growth and elevated inflation prints.
- **Action:** Bullish on the US Dollar (USD), Bullish on Front-End Treasury Yields (2Y Yield), Bearish on Long-Duration Treasuries (TLT), and Bearish on Rate-Sensitive Small-Cap Equities (IWM).

## Rule 2: Energy-Driven Headline CPI Surge and Sticky Breakevens
- **Condition:** Headline Consumer Price Index (CPI) prints at or above 3.7% YoY, driven primarily by retail gasoline and global crude oil price surges that lift near-term breakeven inflation rates and extinguish expectations of near-term monetary easing.
- **Action:** Bullish on Treasury Inflation-Protected Securities (TIPS), Bullish on the US Dollar (USD), Bearish on Intermediate Treasuries (IEF), and Bearish on Gold (XAU/USD).

## Rule 3: Sovereign Deficit Financing and Term Premium Expansion
- **Condition:** Massive federal fiscal deficits and expanded Treasury coupon auction sizes collide with ongoing Federal Reserve quantitative tightening (QT), driving structural term premium bear-steepening across the long end of the yield curve.
- **Action:** Bearish on Core Aggregate Fixed Income (AGG), Bearish on 30-Year Treasuries, Bullish on 10Y/2Y Yield Curve Steepener Trades, and Bullish on Ultra-Short Treasury Bills (BIL).

## Rule 4: Geopolitical Flashpoints and Crude Oil Supply Premium
- **Condition:** Military conflict or shipping bottlenecks across critical maritime choke points threaten global crude transit and export capacity, sustaining a structural geopolitical risk premium in global energy benchmarks.
- **Action:** Bullish on WTI and Brent Crude Oil Futures, Bullish on Energy Exploration & Production Equities (XOP), Bearish on Passenger Airlines and Transportation Equities (JETS), and Bearish on Consumer Discretionary Equities (XLY).

## Rule 5: Concentrated AI Infrastructure Capex Driving Asymmetric Tech Growth
- **Condition:** Hyperscaler and enterprise capital expenditures concentrate heavily into artificial intelligence compute hardware, data centers, and advanced power infrastructure, driving mega-cap technology earnings outperformance while broad industrial capex lags.
- **Action:** Bullish on Mega-Cap Technology Equities (QQQ), Bullish on Semiconductor Equities (SMH), Bullish on Data Center Real Estate Investment Trusts, and Bearish on Broad Industrial Small-Caps (IWM).

## Rule 6: Real Disposable Income Compression and Consumer Credit Strain
- **Condition:** Headline inflation at or above 3.7% and cumulative interest rate hikes elevate debt service burdens on credit cards and variable-rate mortgages, depressing real disposable income and increasing default risks for lower- and middle-income borrowers.
- **Action:** Bullish on Consumer Staples (XLP), Bullish on Quality Factor Equities (QUAL), Bearish on Subprime Consumer Credit and Auto Lenders, and Bearish on Cyclical Consumer Discretionary Equities (XLY).

## Rule 7: Clean Energy Project Cancellations and Baseload Power Pivot
- **Condition:** High financing costs, supply chain bottlenecks, and grid interconnection delays lead to widespread cancellations of utility-scale renewable energy projects, redirecting institutional capital toward regulated baseload utilities, nuclear power, and midstream natural gas.
- **Action:** Bearish on Global Clean Energy Equities (ICLN), Bearish on Solar and Wind Component Manufacturers (TAN), Bullish on Midstream Energy Infrastructure (AMLP), and Bullish on Regulated Baseload Utilities (XLU).

## Rule 8: Cross-Border Industrial Tariffs and North American Supply Chain Friction
- **Condition:** Trade tariffs, protectionist policy shifts, or non-tariff barriers threaten deeply integrated North American value chains (e.g., automotive and agricultural flows between the US and Mexico), driving up domestic manufacturing input costs.
- **Action:** Bullish on the US Dollar against the Canadian Dollar (USD/CAD) and Mexican Peso (USD/MXN), Bearish on Automotive Original Equipment Manufacturers (OEMs), and Bearish on Broad Cyclical Industrials (XLI).

## Rule 9: Clinical Wage Inflation and Healthcare Provider Margin Squeeze
- **Condition:** Structural shortages of specialized medical labor sustain elevated clinical wage pressures that outpace rigid Medicare and commercial insurer reimbursement rates, compressing operating margins for hospital networks and inpatient operators.
- **Action:** Bullish on Large-Cap Pharmaceuticals and Medical Technology (XLV), Bearish on Healthcare Facility and Hospital Operators, and Bearish on Labor-Intensive Consumer Services (XLY).
