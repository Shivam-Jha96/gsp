# United States Macro Trading Rules

## Rule 1: Federal Reserve Late-Cycle Tightening and Hawkish Terminal Policy Path
- **Condition:** The Federal Reserve delivers an interest rate hike and signals additional tightening or a prolonged pause at peak terminal rates due to resilient economic growth and re-accelerating inflation prints.
- **Action:** Bullish on the US Dollar (USD), Bullish on Front-End Treasury Yields (2Y Yield), Bearish on Long-Duration Treasuries (TLT), and Bearish on Rate-Sensitive Small-Cap Equities (IWM).

## Rule 2: Energy-Driven Headline CPI Re-Acceleration
- **Condition:** Headline Consumer Price Index (CPI) prints at or above 3.7% YoY, driven primarily by retail gasoline and global crude oil price surges that push short-to-medium-term inflation expectations higher and foreclose near-term monetary easing.
- **Action:** Bullish on Treasury Inflation-Protected Securities (TIPS), Bullish on the US Dollar (USD), Bearish on Intermediate Treasuries (IEF), and Bearish on Gold (XAU/USD).

## Rule 3: Sovereign Deficit Financing and Term Premium Bear Steepening
- **Condition:** Massive federal fiscal deficits and expanded Treasury coupon auction sizes collide with ongoing Federal Reserve quantitative tightening (QT), driving structural term premium expansion across the long end of the curve.
- **Action:** Bearish on Core Aggregate Fixed Income (AGG), Bearish on 30-Year Treasuries, Bullish on 10Y/2Y Yield Curve Steepener Trades, and Bullish on Ultra-Short Treasury Bills (BIL).

## Rule 4: Geopolitical Flashpoints and Crude Oil Supply Premium
- **Condition:** Military conflict or shipping bottlenecks across critical Middle Eastern and global maritime corridors threaten crude export volumes, sustaining a structural geopolitical risk premium in global energy benchmarks.
- **Action:** Bullish on WTI and Brent Crude Oil Futures, Bullish on Energy Exploration & Production Equities (XOP), Bearish on Passenger Airlines and Transportation Equities (JETS), and Bearish on Consumer Discretionary Equities (XLY).

## Rule 5: Concentrated AI Infrastructure Capex Driving Asymmetric Tech Growth
- **Condition:** Hyperscaler and enterprise capital expenditures concentrate aggressively into artificial intelligence compute hardware, data center construction, and power infrastructure, driving mega-cap tech outperformance while non-tech corporate capex lags.
- **Action:** Bullish on Mega-Cap Technology Equities (QQQ), Bullish on Semiconductor Equities (SMH), Bullish on Data Center Real Estate Investment Trusts, and Bearish on Broad Industrial Small-Caps (IWM).

## Rule 6: Real Disposable Income Compression and Consumer Stagflation
- **Condition:** Headline inflation and urban wage-earner inflation remain stubbornly elevated near 3.7%–3.9% while real economic growth slows, eroding real disposable income and triggering trading-down behavior across middle- and lower-income cohorts.
- **Action:** Bullish on Consumer Staples (XLP), Bullish on Quality Factor Equities (QUAL), Bearish on Subprime Consumer Credit and Auto Lenders, and Bearish on Cyclical Consumer Discretionary Equities (XLY).

## Rule 7: Clean Energy Capital Retrenchment and Baseload Power Pivot
- **Condition:** High interest rates, grid interconnection delays, and cost inflation cause widespread cancellations of utility-scale renewable energy installations, shifting institutional capital toward regulated baseload power, nuclear generation, and midstream natural gas.
- **Action:** Bearish on Global Clean Energy Equities (ICLN), Bearish on Solar and Wind Component Manufacturers (TAN), Bullish on Midstream Energy Infrastructure (AMLP), and Bullish on Regulated Baseload Utilities (XLU).

## Rule 8: Cross-Border Industrial Tariffs and North American Supply Chain Friction
- **Condition:** Proposed or implemented trade tariffs and non-tariff barriers on intermediate manufactured goods, steel, and automotive components disrupt integrated cross-border North American value chains and elevate domestic manufacturing input costs.
- **Action:** Bullish on the US Dollar against the Canadian Dollar (USD/CAD) and Mexican Peso (USD/MXN), Bearish on Automotive Original Equipment Manufacturers (OEMs), and Bearish on Broad Cyclical Industrials (XLI).

## Rule 9: Clinical Wage Inflation and Healthcare Facility Margin Compression
- **Condition:** Structural shortages in specialized medical labor maintain elevated clinical wage pressures that outstrip contracted insurer and Medicare reimbursement rates, compressing operating margins for hospital networks and inpatient care operators.
- **Action:** Bullish on Large-Cap Pharmaceuticals and Medical Technology (XLV), Bearish on Healthcare Facility and Hospital Operators, and Bearish on Labor-Intensive Consumer Services (XLY).
