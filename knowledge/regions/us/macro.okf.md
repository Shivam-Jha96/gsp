# United States Macro Trading Rules

## Rule 1: Federal Reserve Late-Cycle Tightening and Hawkish Terminal Policy Path
- **Condition:** The Federal Reserve executes an interest rate hike and signals further policy tightening in response to stubborn, re-accelerating inflation data, keeping terminal rate expectations elevated and pushing back rate-cut horizons.
- **Action:** Bullish on the US Dollar (USD), Bullish on Front-End Treasury Yields (2Y Yield), Bearish on Long-Duration Treasuries (TLT), and Bearish on Rate-Sensitive Small-Cap Equities (IWM).

## Rule 2: Energy-Driven Headline CPI Re-Acceleration
- **Condition:** Headline Consumer Price Index (CPI) prints at or above 3.7% YoY, driven primarily by retail gasoline and global crude oil price spikes that elevate short-to-medium-term inflation expectations and complicate policy easing timelines.
- **Action:** Bullish on Treasury Inflation-Protected Securities (TIPS), Bullish on the US Dollar (USD), Bearish on Intermediate Treasuries (IEF), and Bearish on Gold (XAU/USD).

## Rule 3: Sovereign Deficit Financing and Term Premium Bear Steepening
- **Condition:** Substantial federal fiscal deficits and expanded Treasury coupon issuance collide with ongoing Federal Reserve quantitative tightening (QT), driving structural term premium expansion across the long end of the yield curve.
- **Action:** Bearish on Core Aggregate Fixed Income (AGG), Bearish on 30-Year Treasuries, Bullish on 10Y/2Y Yield Curve Steepener Trades, and Bullish on Ultra-Short Treasury Bills (BIL).

## Rule 4: Middle East Geopolitical Conflict and Crude Oil Supply Shocks
- **Condition:** Military conflict and geopolitical escalations involving key Middle Eastern oil producers threaten critical shipping lanes and regional output, injecting a sustained geopolitical risk premium into global crude benchmarks.
- **Action:** Bullish on WTI and Brent Crude Oil Futures, Bullish on Energy Exploration & Production Equities (XOP), Bearish on Passenger Airlines and Transportation Equities (JETS), and Bearish on Consumer Discretionary Equities (XLY).

## Rule 5: Concentrated AI Infrastructure Capex Driving Asymmetric Tech Growth
- **Condition:** Hyperscaler and enterprise capital expenditures concentrate heavily in artificial intelligence compute hardware, data center construction, and semiconductor capacity, driving mega-cap tech earnings while broader industrial capex remains subdued.
- **Action:** Bullish on Mega-Cap Technology Equities (QQQ), Bullish on Semiconductor Equities (SMH), Bullish on Data Center Real Estate Investment Trusts, and Bearish on Broad Industrial Small-Caps (IWM).

## Rule 6: Sluggish Real GDP Growth Amid Persistent Inflation (Late-Cycle Stagflation)
- **Condition:** Real GDP growth slows to sub-2.0% annualized rates while headline inflation remains stubbornly elevated above target, compressing real disposable income for consumers and tightening real corporate profit margins.
- **Action:** Bullish on Consumer Staples (XLP), Bullish on Quality Factor Equities (QUAL), Bearish on Lower-Tier Retail and Subprime Consumer Credit, and Bearish on Cyclical Consumer Discretionary Equities (XLY).

## Rule 7: Clean Energy Capital Retrenchment and Baseload Power Realignment
- **Condition:** Elevated financing costs, grid interconnection delays, and supply chain constraints suppress returns on utility-scale renewable installations, forcing capital reallocation toward conventional baseload power, nuclear generation, and midstream natural gas networks.
- **Action:** Bearish on Global Clean Energy Equities (ICLN), Bearish on Solar and Wind Component Manufacturers (TAN), Bullish on Midstream Energy Infrastructure (AMLP), and Bullish on Regulated Baseload Utilities (XLU).

## Rule 8: Cross-Border Industrial Tariffs and Automotive Supply Chain Friction
- **Condition:** The enactment or threat of punitive bilateral tariffs on imported steel, automotive platforms, and intermediate manufactured goods disrupts integrated North American supply chains and elevates import input costs.
- **Action:** Bullish on the US Dollar against the Canadian Dollar (USD/CAD) and Mexican Peso (USD/MXN), Bearish on Automotive Original Equipment Manufacturers (OEMs), and Bearish on Broad Cyclical Industrials (XLI).

## Rule 9: Clinical Wage Inflation and Healthcare Facility Margin Compression
- **Condition:** Structural shortages in specialized healthcare labor maintain elevated clinical wage growth, outpacing contracted insurer reimbursement rates and depressing operating margins for inpatient facilities.
- **Action:** Bullish on Large-Cap Pharmaceuticals and Medical Technology (XLV), Bearish on Healthcare Facility and Hospital Operators, and Bearish on Labor-Intensive Consumer Services (XLY).
