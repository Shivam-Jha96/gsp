# United States Macro Trading Rules

## Rule 1: Fed Rate Hike Resumption and Front-End Rate Repricing
- **Condition:** The Federal Reserve resumes monetary tightening or signals sequential benchmark rate hikes following pauses, driven by persistent headline inflation prints and upside labor surprises that reset terminal policy rate expectations higher.
- **Action:** Bullish on Front-End Treasury Yields (2Y Yield), Bullish on the US Dollar Index (DXY), Bearish on Short-Duration Fixed Income (SHY), and Bearish on Rate-Sensitive Small-Cap Equities (IWM).

## Rule 2: Energy-Driven CPI Acceleration and Real Yield Repricing
- **Condition:** Headline CPI accelerates toward 3.7% YoY driven by sudden spikes in retail gasoline and refined petroleum products, elevating short-term inflation expectations and forcing real policy discount rates higher.
- **Action:** Bullish on Treasury Inflation-Protected Securities (TIPS), Bullish on Ultra-Short Treasury Bills (BIL), Bearish on Spot Gold (XAU/USD), and Bearish on Spot Silver (XAG/USD).

## Rule 3: Sovereign Deficit Issuance and Term Premium Bear Steepening
- **Condition:** Structural federal budget deficit expansion forces the US Treasury to materially increase coupon auction sizes and long-dated supply, pushing sovereign term premiums wider across intermediate and long maturities.
- **Action:** Bullish on 10Y/2Y Yield Curve Steepener Trades, Bullish on Floating Rate Notes (USFR), Bearish on 30-Year Treasury Bonds, and Bearish on Broad Investment-Grade Bond Indices (AGG).

## Rule 4: Upstream Energy Cost-Push and Logistics Margin Compression
- **Condition:** Refined product and crude oil supply tightness drive wholesale fuel prices sharply higher, generating intense cost-push inflation across domestic transportation networks and industrial supply chains.
- **Action:** Bullish on WTI and Brent Crude Oil Futures, Bullish on Energy Exploration & Production Equities (XOP), Bearish on Airline Equities (JETS), and Bearish on Freight and Trucking Logistics Operators.

## Rule 5: Discount Rate Expansion and High-Multiple Valuation Compression
- **Condition:** Rising benchmark risk-free yields driven by hawkish Federal Reserve rate paths increase equity cost of capital, triggering severe multiple derating across long-duration, high-valuation technology and speculative growth assets.
- **Action:** Bearish on Unprofitable Tech and High-Multiple Software Equities, Bearish on Broad Mega-Cap Growth (QQQ), Bullish on Cash-Flow Yield and Low-Beta Value Factors (USMV), and Bullish on Ultra-Short Cash Equivalents.

## Rule 6: Resilient Labor Markets and Subprime Consumer Credit Deterioration
- **Condition:** Nonfarm payrolls and wage growth demonstrate persistent resilience while cumulative borrowing costs drain lower-tier household savings, driving severe credit performance divergence between prime and subprime borrowers.
- **Action:** Bullish on Large-Cap Quality Factor Equities (QUAL), Bullish on Consumer Staples Equities (XLP), Bearish on Subprime Auto Lenders and Consumer Finance Providers, and Bearish on Broad Discretionary Retailers (XRT).

## Rule 7: US-Mexico Cross-Border Supply Chain Integration
- **Condition:** Persistent nearshoring momentum and bilateral manufacturing alignment sustain elevated goods throughput along southern cross-border industrial and agricultural corridors.
- **Action:** Bullish on the Mexican Peso against G10 Crosses (MXN/EUR, MXN/JPY), Bullish on North American Cross-Border Class I Railroads, and Bearish on Trans-Pacific Container Freight Rates.

## Rule 8: Capital Cost Frictions in Renewables vs. Baseload Utility Expansion
- **Condition:** Elevated debt financing costs and interconnection bottlenecks delay utility-scale renewable buildouts, driving capital reallocations into regulated natural gas, midstream processing, and nuclear baseload power infrastructure.
- **Action:** Bullish on Regulated Electric and Nuclear Utilities (XLU), Bullish on Midstream Energy MLPs (AMLP), Bearish on Clean Energy ETFs (ICLN), and Bearish on Solar Equipment Manufacturers (TAN).

## Rule 9: Healthcare Clinical Wage Inflation and Hospital Operating Margin Squeeze
- **Condition:** Persistent shortages in specialized clinical and nursing staff sustain elevated hospital labor expenditures that outstrip fixed annual commercial and government reimbursement rate adjustments.
- **Action:** Bullish on Large-Cap Medical Device and Pharmaceutical Equities (XLV), Bearish on Acute Hospital Facility Operators, and Bearish on Inpatient Healthcare REITs.

## Rule 10: Policy Divergence and Broad Dollar Dominance
- **Condition:** Active Federal Reserve hawkishness and superior US economic growth diverge from foreign central banks confronting industrial stagnation or deflationary domestic demand, widening sovereign yield differentials in favor of the US.
- **Action:** Bullish on USD against the Euro (EUR/USD Short), Bullish on USD against the Japanese Yen (USD/JPY Long), and Bearish on Emerging Market Local Currency Debt (EMLC).

## Rule 11: Enterprise AI Capex Boom and Power Infrastructure Demand
- **Condition:** Accelerating enterprise and hyperscaler capital expenditure into artificial intelligence data centers cushions aggregate corporate investment and GDP growth despite elevated interest rates, creating unprecedented grid power and hardware demand.
- **Action:** Bullish on AI Semiconductor and Accelerator Manufacturers (SMH), Bullish on Electrical Grid Equipment and Industrial Power Providers, Bearish on Legacy Enterprise IT Hardware, and Bearish on Traditional Commercial Office REITs.
