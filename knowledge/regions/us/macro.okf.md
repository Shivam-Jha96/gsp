# United States Macro Trading Rules

## Rule 1: Hawkish Fed Rate Hike Reacceleration and Terminal Rate Extension
- **Condition:** Federal Reserve policymakers signal renewed rate hikes (such as potential December actions) and extended terminal policy rates to counter persistent inflation (with headline gauges climbing to 3.7%-3.8%), driving market repricing of tightening cycles back onto the table.
- **Action:** Bullish on Front-End Treasury Yields (2Y Yield), Bullish on the US Dollar Index (DXY), Bearish on Short-Duration Fixed Income (SHY), and Bearish on Rate-Sensitive Small-Cap Equities (IWM).

## Rule 2: Sequential CPI Acceleration and Real Rate Repricing
- **Condition:** Consumer price indices expand briskly driven by sticky components (such as food and rent costs rising, leading to a 0.4% monthly increase) and broadening price pressures, exceeding expectations and driving short-term inflation breakevens and real discount rates higher.
- **Action:** Bullish on Treasury Inflation-Protected Securities (TIPS), Bullish on Ultra-Short T-Bills and Cash (BIL), Bearish on Spot Gold (XAU/USD), and Bearish on Long-Duration Investment-Grade Corporate Debt (LQD).

## Rule 3: Acute Late-Cycle Stagflationary Decoupling
- **Condition:** Real GDP growth downshifts or shows divergence while inflation reaccelerates above target, raising concerns from market participants and economists that overtightening risks stalling the real economy while prices remain sticky.
- **Action:** Bullish on Large-Asset Defensive Quality Equities (QUAL), Bullish on Consumer Staples (XLP), Bearish on Cyclical Industrials (XLI), and Bearish on High-Yield Credit (HYG).

## Rule 4: Transatlantic Inflation Divergence and USD Dominance
- **Condition:** Persistent US inflation pressures and hawkish Federal Reserve forward guidance contrast sharply with disinflation and economic stagnation abroad, widening real sovereign yield differentials in favor of the United States.
- **Action:** Bullish on USD against the Euro (EUR/USD Short), Bullish on USD against the Japanese Yen (USD/JPY Long), Bearish on Emerging Market Local-Currency Debt (EMLC), and Bearish on European Cyclical Equities.

## Rule 5: Sovereign Fiscal Deficit Expansion and Term Premium Steepening
- **Condition:** Unanchored federal budget deficits and heavy Treasury coupon issuance schedules create structural supply indigestion across intermediate and long maturities, expanding the term premium.
- **Action:** Bullish on 10Y/2Y Yield Curve Steepener Structures, Bullish on Floating Rate Notes (USFR), Bearish on 30-Year Treasury Bonds (TLT), and Bearish on Aggregate US Bond Indices (AGG).

## Rule 6: Discount Rate Shock and Long-Duration Multiple Derating
- **Condition:** Renewed upward pressure on risk-free front-end yields and real discount rates drives valuation compression across non-profitable technology, high-multiple SaaS, and speculative growth assets as stocks tumble on safety bids.
- **Action:** Bullish on High-Free-Cash-Flow Yield Equities (COWZ), Bullish on Ultra-Short Cash Equivalents, Bearish on Non-Profitable Biotech (XBI), and Bearish on High-Multiple Cloud Software Equities.

## Rule 7: Clean Energy Retrenchment and Baseload Nuclear Repricing
- **Condition:** Widespread clean energy project cancellations and job losses collide with surging electrical load growth, accelerating commercial re-contracting for nuclear power, natural gas peakers, and traditional grid utilities.
- **Action:** Bullish on Regulated Electric and Nuclear Utilities (XLU), Bullish on Uranium and Nuclear Fuel Providers (URA), Bullish on Natural Gas Pipeline MLPs (AMLP), and Bearish on Clean Energy and Residential Solar Providers (TAN, ICLN).

## Rule 8: Enterprise AI Capex Supercycle and Grid Infrastructure Bottlenecks
- **Condition:** Multi-year hyperscaler capital investments into frontier artificial intelligence clusters continue to drive economic growth metrics despite broader macroeconomic headwinds, creating acute capacity deficits across high-voltage grid equipment, power transformers, and thermal management.
- **Action:** Bullish on AI Semiconductor Accelerators and Fab Equities (SMH), Bullish on Electrical Grid Hardware and Transformer Manufacturers, Bearish on Legacy Enterprise IT On-Premises Hardware, and Bearish on Commercial Office REITs (VNQ).

## Rule 9: Upstream Energy Cost-Push and Transportation Margin Compression
- **Condition:** Crude supply constraints and soaring energy prices elevate wholesale fuel and diesel crack spreads, transmitting operating cost increases directly into logistics and transportation networks.
- **Action:** Bullish on WTI and Brent Crude Oil Futures, Bullish on Energy Exploration & Production Equities (XOP), Bearish on Passenger Airlines (JETS), and Bearish on Freight and Trucking Logistics Equities.

## Rule 10: Household Savings Exhaustion and Consumer Credit Bifurcation
- **Condition:** Persistent cumulative inflation alongside softening aggregate growth drains lower- and middle-income liquidity buffers, impacting holiday spending and triggering elevated delinquency rates in non-prime credit while prime consumer balance sheets remain resilient.
- **Action:** Bullish on Low-Beta Consumer Staples (XLP), Bullish on Large-Cap Prime-Exposed Money-Center Banks, Bearish on Subprime Auto Lenders and Consumer Finance Equities, and Bearish on Broad Discretionary Retailers (XRT).

## Rule 11: Nearshoring Protectionism and North American Cross-Border Logistics
- **Condition:** Sustained tariff threats, protectionist trade realignments, and manufacturing relocation mandates preserve heavy commercial throughput across US-Mexico-Canada land transit corridors.
- **Action:** Bullish on North American Cross-Border Class I Railroads, Bullish on Mexican Peso against G10 Crosses (MXN/EUR), and Bearish on Trans-Pacific Spot Container Shipping Freight Rates.

## Rule 12: Healthcare Clinical Wage Inelasticity and Inpatient Margin Squeeze
- **Condition:** Structural shortages in specialized medical and clinical personnel sustain elevated operating wage expenses that exceed rigid multi-year reimbursement schedules set by commercial insurers and federal healthcare programs.
- **Action:** Bullish on Diversified Pharmaceutical and Medical Device Equities (XLV), Bearish on Acute-Care Hospital Operators, and Bearish on Skilled Nursing and Long-Term Care Healthcare REITs.

## Rule 13: Protectionist Tariff Escalation and Import-Sensitive Margin Compression
- **Condition:** Implementation and impending expansion of fresh US tariffs weigh heavily on market sentiment and supply chain inputs, increasing operational friction and cost bases for domestic entities reliant on foreign manufacturing components.
- **Action:** Bullish on Domestic-Centric Small-Cap Manufacturers with Protected Moats, Bullish on US Customs Compliance and Trade Advisory Services, Bearish on Consumer Electronics Importers, and Bearish on Import-Heavy Apparel Retailers.
