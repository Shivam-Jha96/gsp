# United States Macro Trading Rules

## Rule 1: Federal Reserve Hawkish Pause and Lingering Rate Hike Optionality
- **Condition:** Headline CPI accelerates to 3.7% YoY alongside resilient core inflation prints that top forecasts, compelling the Federal Reserve to implement a hawkish pause while explicitly preserving rate hike optionality and signaling an extended higher-for-longer policy trajectory.
- **Action:** Bullish on the US Dollar Index (DXY), Bullish on Front-End Treasury Yields (2Y Yield), Bearish on Long-Duration Treasuries (TLT), and Bearish on Rate-Sensitive Small-Cap Equities (IWM).

## Rule 2: Energy-Driven CPI Resurgence and Real Yield Headwinds on Precious Metals
- **Condition:** Surging retail gasoline prices and crude oil spikes lift headline inflation, elevating short-term inflation breakevens and nominal front-end yields ahead of monetary policy decisions.
- **Action:** Bullish on Treasury Inflation-Protected Securities (TIPS), Bullish on High-Yield Cash and Short-Term Treasury Bills (BIL), Bearish on Spot Gold (XAU/USD), and Bearish on Intermediate Treasuries (IEF).

## Rule 3: Divergent Yield Curve Dynamics and Sovereign Debt Term Premium Expansion
- **Condition:** Record sovereign coupon issuance required to fund persistent federal deficits collides with ongoing Federal Reserve balance sheet runoff, causing yields across the curve to diverge and driving term premium expansion on long-dated paper.
- **Action:** Bullish on 10Y/2Y Yield Curve Steepener Trades, Bullish on Ultra-Short Treasury Floating Rate Notes (USFR), Bearish on 30-Year US Treasuries, and Bearish on Broad Investment-Grade Fixed Income (AGG).

## Rule 4: Upstream Energy Supply Shocks and Downstream Margin Compression
- **Condition:** Tight crude supply and surging motor fuel costs create persistent cost-push inflation, squeezing operational margins across transport networks and non-essential consumer services.
- **Action:** Bullish on WTI and Brent Crude Oil Futures, Bullish on Energy Exploration & Production Equities (XOP), Bearish on Airlines and Freight Transport Equities (JETS), and Bearish on Consumer Discretionary Equities (XLY).

## Rule 5: Artificial Intelligence Hyperscaler Capex as Asymmetric Growth Engine
- **Condition:** Massive hyperscaler capex and corporate investment into artificial intelligence accelerators, data center real estate, and semiconductor capacity serve as the primary engine maintaining aggregate US economic growth amidst broader macro deceleration.
- **Action:** Bullish on Mega-Cap Technology Equities (QQQ), Bullish on Semiconductor Equities (SMH), Bullish on Data Center Real Estate and Infrastructure Providers, and Bearish on Broad Industrial Small-Caps (IWM).

## Rule 6: Stagflationary Macro Deceleration and Household Credit Deterioration
- **Condition:** Real GDP growth slows to a sluggish 1.5% annualized pace while inflation remains stubbornly above target, elevating borrowing costs and accelerating delinquency rates across revolving credit lines and auto loans.
- **Action:** Bullish on Consumer Staples Equities (XLP), Bullish on Large-Cap Quality Factor Equities (QUAL), Bearish on Subprime Consumer and Auto Lenders, and Bearish on Broad Discretionary Retailers (XRT).

## Rule 7: Clean Energy Capital Project Cancellations and Baseload Utility Realignment
- **Condition:** Elevated interest rates, grid interconnection backlogs, and cost overruns trigger widespread cancellations of utility-scale renewable wind and solar projects, redirecting capital expenditure toward reliable baseload electricity generation and midstream infrastructure.
- **Action:** Bearish on Global Clean Energy Equities (ICLN), Bearish on Solar Component Manufacturers (TAN), Bullish on Regulated Electric and Nuclear Utilities (XLU), and Bullish on Midstream Energy Infrastructure (AMLP).

## Rule 8: Clinical Labor Cost Inflation and Inpatient Healthcare Margin Compression
- **Condition:** Persistent shortages of specialized medical personnel and nursing staff drive clinical wage inflation that outpaces statutory insurer reimbursement schedules, impairing margins for inpatient facilities.
- **Action:** Bullish on Large-Cap Pharmaceuticals and Medical Devices (XLV), Bearish on Hospital Operators, and Bearish on Acute Inpatient Facility Networks.

## Rule 9: Cross-Border Nearshoring and North American Supply Chain Integration
- **Condition:** Continued restructuring of industrial manufacturing corridors favors localized North American sourcing and bilateral trade logistics over trans-oceanic freight routes.
- **Action:** Bullish on the Mexican Peso against G10 Currencies (MXN/EUR, MXN/JPY), Bullish on North American Cross-Border Class I Rail Equities, and Bearish on Trans-Pacific Container Shipping Carriers.
