# United States Macro Trading Rules

## Rule 1: Hawkish Fed Rate Hike Reacceleration and December Tightening Repricing
- **Condition:** Federal Reserve policymakers signal renewed benchmark rate hikes for the upcoming December meeting to restore inflation-fighting credibility despite equity market volatility, driving money markets to reprice higher terminal policy rates.
- **Action:** Bullish on Front-End Treasury Yields (2Y Yield), Bullish on the US Dollar Index (DXY), Bearish on Short-Duration Fixed Income (SHY), and Bearish on Interest-Rate-Sensitive Small Caps (IWM).

## Rule 2: Sequential CPI Acceleration and Real Rate Repricing
- **Condition:** Headline and core Consumer Price Indices accelerate sequentially for multiple consecutive months—driven by persistent shelter, rent, and food cost spikes (such as a 0.4% MoM print exceeding consensus)—forcing upward revisions to short-term inflation breakevens and real discount rates.
- **Action:** Bullish on Treasury Inflation-Protected Securities (TIPS), Bullish on Cash and Ultra-Short T-Bills (BIL), Bearish on Spot Gold (XAU/USD), and Bearish on Long-Duration Investment-Grade Corporate Debt (LQD).

## Rule 3: Late-Cycle Stagflationary Decoupling and Policy Overtightening Risk
- **Condition:** Persistent above-target inflation collides with softening underlying employment and volatile activity indicators, heightening macroeconomic risk that central bank overtightening will precipitate a growth contraction while price pressures remain rigid.
- **Action:** Bullish on Large-Cap Defensive Quality Equities (QUAL), Bullish on Consumer Staples (XLP), Bearish on Cyclical Industrials (XLI), and Bearish on High-Yield Credit (HYG).

## Rule 4: Transatlantic Monetary Divergence and Dollar Dominance
- **Condition:** Resilient US economic momentum and Federal Reserve rate-hiking bias contrast with acute economic stagnation and disinflationary easing cycles across European and Asian economies, expanding nominal and real sovereign yield differentials in favor of the US.
- **Action:** Bullish on USD against the Euro (EUR/USD Short), Bullish on USD against the Japanese Yen (USD/JPY Long), Bearish on Emerging Market Local-Currency Debt (EMLC), and Bearish on European Ex-US Equities.

## Rule 5: Sovereign Fiscal Deficit Expansion and Term Premium Steepening
- **Condition:** Structural multi-trillion-dollar federal deficits, heavy Treasury coupon auction schedules, and institutional debates surrounding official economic metrics drive supply indigestion and widen the term premium across the long end of the curve.
- **Action:** Bullish on 10Y/2Y Yield Curve Steepener Structures, Bullish on Floating Rate Notes (USFR), Bearish on 30-Year Treasury Bonds (TLT), and Bearish on Broad Aggregate US Fixed Income (AGG).

## Rule 6: Discount Rate Shock and Speculative Multiple Derating
- **Condition:** Rebounding short-term risk-free rates and widening discount factors trigger aggressive multiple derating across unprofitable growth companies, high-multiple cloud software, and speculative equities amid an escalating broad-market flight to safety.
- **Action:** Bullish on High-Free-Cash-Flow Yield Equities (COWZ), Bullish on Ultra-Short Fixed Income, Bearish on Non-Profitable Biotech (XBI), and Bearish on High-Multiple SaaS and Cloud Equities.

## Rule 7: Clean Energy Project Retrenchment and Baseload Power Repricing
- **Condition:** High capital costs and regulatory friction trigger widespread clean energy project cancellations and green-sector job losses, forcing utilities and industrial power consumers to pivot capital toward baseload nuclear generation, gas peakers, and traditional grid assets.
- **Action:** Bullish on Regulated Electric and Nuclear Utilities (XLU), Bullish on Uranium and Nuclear Fuel Cycle Providers (URA), Bullish on Midstream Natural Gas MLPs (AMLP), and Bearish on Clean Energy and Residential Solar Providers (TAN, ICLN).

## Rule 8: Enterprise AI Capex Supercycle and Power Infrastructure Scarcity
- **Condition:** Sustained enterprise and hyperscaler capital expenditure into frontier AI data centers acts as the primary driver of domestic private fixed investment, creating acute structural demand for electrical transmission, transformers, and specialized thermal components.
- **Action:** Bullish on Advanced Semiconductor and AI Accelerator Equities (SMH), Bullish on High-Voltage Electrical Grid Equipment Manufacturers, Bearish on Legacy On-Premises Enterprise IT Hardware, and Bearish on Commercial Office REITs (VNQ).

## Rule 9: Protectionist Tariff Escalation and Supply Chain Cost Friction
- **Condition:** Implementation or threat of aggressive, across-the-board import tariffs increases input costs and operational friction for domestic manufacturers, dampening trade-exposed market sentiment ahead of central bank rate determinations.
- **Action:** Bullish on Domestic-Centric Small-Cap Producers with Localized Supply Chains, Bullish on Customs and Trade Compliance Providers, Bearish on Import-Reliant Consumer Electronics, and Bearish on Foreign-Sourced Retail Apparel.

## Rule 10: Cumulative Inflation Drag and Holiday Discretionary Spending Bifurcation
- **Condition:** Multi-year cumulative price increases and elevated consumer borrowing costs erode lower- and middle-income household savings buffers heading into key retail periods, suppressing discretionary holiday spending while prime balance sheets hold firm.
- **Action:** Bullish on Discount and Off-Price Retailers (COST, ROST), Bullish on Prime Money-Center Banks, Bearish on Subprime Consumer Lenders and Buy-Now-Pay-Later Providers, and Bearish on Discretionary Specialty Retailers (XRT).

## Rule 11: Upstream Energy Cost-Push and Transportation Margin Compression
- **Condition:** Physical crude supply tightness and elevated refinery crack spreads drive wholesale fuel costs higher, generating secondary cost-push inflation and directly compressing margins for fuel-intensive transport networks.
- **Action:** Bullish on WTI and Brent Crude Oil Futures, Bullish on Energy Exploration & Production Equities (XOP), Bearish on Passenger Airlines (JETS), and Bearish on Long-Haul Trucking Logistics.

## Rule 12: Continental Nearshoring and North American Trade Logistics
- **Condition:** Persistent trade protectionism, geopolitical fragmentation, and nearshoring mandates redirect supply routes to North American land transit corridors, sustaining heavy commercial freight flows between the US, Mexico, and Canada.
- **Action:** Bullish on North American Cross-Border Class I Railroads, Bullish on Mexican Peso against G10 Crosses (MXN/EUR), and Bearish on Trans-Pacific Container Freight Rates.

## Rule 13: Clinical Labor Wage Inelasticity and Inpatient Healthcare Squeeze
- **Condition:** Structural shortages of specialized nursing and medical clinical staff maintain high wage inflation across acute-care facilities, outstripping the reimbursement adjustments offered by commercial insurers and government health programs.
- **Action:** Bullish on Large-Cap Pharmaceuticals and Diversified Medical Devices (XLV), Bearish on Acute-Care For-Profit Hospital Operators, and Bearish on Skilled Nursing and Senior Care REITs.
