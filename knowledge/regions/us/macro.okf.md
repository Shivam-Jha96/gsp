# United States Macro Trading Rules

## Rule 1: Federal Reserve Rate Lift-Off and Sustained Tightening Stance
- **Condition:** The Federal Reserve delivers an interest rate hike—marking its first increase in three years—defying political pressure for cuts as re-accelerating inflation forces policymakers to maintain an active tightening bias.
- **Action:** Bullish on the US Dollar (USD), Bullish on Front-End Treasury Yields (2Y Yield), Bearish on Long-Duration Treasuries (TLT), and Bearish on Rate-Sensitive Small-Cap Equities (IWM).

## Rule 2: Energy-Driven Headline CPI Re-Acceleration
- **Condition:** Headline CPI accelerates to 3.7% YoY or higher, propelled by soaring retail fuel and broad commodity price gains that threaten to unmoor medium-term inflation expectations and cement higher terminal rates.
- **Action:** Bullish on Treasury Inflation-Protected Securities (TIPS), Bullish on the US Dollar (USD), Bearish on Intermediate Treasuries (IEF), and Bearish on Gold (XAU/USD).

## Rule 3: Sovereign Deficit Financing and Term Premium Bear Steepening
- **Condition:** Expanding federal fiscal deficits and heavy Treasury coupon issuance coincide with ongoing central bank balance sheet runoff, driving structural term premium expansion across the long end of the curve.
- **Action:** Bearish on Core Aggregate Fixed Income (AGG), Bearish on 30-Year Treasuries, Bullish on 10Y/2Y Yield Curve Steepener Trades, and Bullish on Ultra-Short Treasury Bills (BIL).

## Rule 4: Geopolitical Escalation and Crude Oil Supply Shocks
- **Condition:** Military conflict in the Middle East directly disrupts regional energy logistics and drives sharp risk premiums into global crude benchmarks, elevating headline input costs across the economy.
- **Action:** Bullish on WTI/Brent Crude Oil Futures, Bullish on Energy Exploration & Production Equities (XOP), Bearish on Airlines and Transportation Equities (JETS), and Bearish on Consumer Discretionary Equities (XLY).

## Rule 5: Concentrated AI Infrastructure Capex Driving Asymmetric GDP Growth
- **Condition:** Private non-residential fixed investment disproportionately concentrates in artificial intelligence data centers, compute silicon, and hyperscale digital infrastructure, sustaining headline GDP while traditional industrial capex softens.
- **Action:** Bullish on Mega-Cap Technology Equities (QQQ), Bullish on Semiconductor Equities (SMH), and Bearish on Broad Industrial Small-Caps (IWM).

## Rule 6: Cross-Border Industrial Tariffs and Auto Supply Chain Friction
- **Condition:** The implementation or threat of punitive U.S. tariffs on cross-border automotive and industrial imports from North American trading partners impairs integrated supply chains, exposing manufacturing jobs and industrial output to contraction.
- **Action:** Bullish on the US Dollar against the Canadian Dollar (USD/CAD), Bearish on Automotive Original Equipment Manufacturers (OEMs), and Bearish on Cyclical Industrials (XLI).

## Rule 7: Clean Energy Capital Retrenchment and Grid Reliability Transition
- **Condition:** High financing costs, regulatory backlogs, and widespread project cancellations trigger retrenchment across utility-scale renewables, redirecting baseline capacity investment toward conventional thermal, midstream, and baseload power infrastructure.
- **Action:** Bearish on Global Clean Energy Equities (ICLN), Bearish on Solar and Wind Manufacturers, Bullish on Midstream Infrastructure (AMLP), and Bullish on Traditional Integrated Energy (XLE).

## Rule 8: Healthcare Labor Inflation and Hospital Margin Compression
- **Condition:** Persistent clinical wage inflation and rising medical delivery costs outpace institutional reimbursement rate adjustments, compressing operating margins for hospital systems while consuming household discretionary budgets.
- **Action:** Bullish on Large-Cap Pharmaceuticals and Medical Devices (XLV), Bearish on Healthcare Facility and Hospital Operators, and Bearish on Broad Consumer Discretionary Equities (XLY).
