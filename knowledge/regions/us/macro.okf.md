# United States Macro Trading Rules

## Rule 1: Federal Reserve Hawkish Hold and Extended Higher-for-Longer Regime
- **Condition:** US headline CPI accelerates to 3.7% YoY alongside sticky core inflation, forcing the Federal Reserve to maintain an aggressively hawkish pause, keep further rate hikes on the table, and actively push back against monetary easing expectations despite slowing GDP growth.
- **Action:** Bullish on the US Dollar Index (DXY), Bullish on Front-End Treasury Yields (2Y Yield), Bearish on Long-Duration Treasuries (TLT), and Bearish on Rate-Sensitive Small-Cap Equities (IWM).

## Rule 2: Upstream Oil-Fueled Inflation and Real Yield Compression on Non-Yielding Assets
- **Condition:** Skyrocketing retail gasoline and crude oil prices lift headline inflation expectations, driving nominal Treasury yields higher and triggering tactical pullbacks in non-yielding assets ahead of FOMC policy determinations.
- **Action:** Bullish on Treasury Inflation-Protected Securities (TIPS), Bullish on High-Yield Cash and Short-Term Treasury Bills (BIL), Bearish on Spot Gold (XAU/USD), and Bearish on Intermediate Treasuries (IEF).

## Rule 3: Sovereign Fiscal Deficits and Term Premium Expansion
- **Condition:** Elevated US Treasury coupon issuance to finance expanding federal deficits collides with ongoing Federal Reserve balance sheet runoff (Quantitative Tightening), driving upward term premium re-pricing and bear-steepening pressure on the yield curve.
- **Action:** Bearish on 30-Year US Treasuries, Bearish on Broad Investment-Grade Fixed Income (AGG), Bullish on 10Y/2Y Yield Curve Steepener Trades, and Bullish on Ultra-Short Treasury Floating Rate Notes (USFR).

## Rule 4: Upstream Energy Supply Shocks and Downstream Margin Compression
- **Condition:** Crude oil supply deficits and elevated refining margins drive surges in motor fuels and wholesale energy benchmarks, generating pervasive cost-push inflation across the economy.
- **Action:** Bullish on WTI and Brent Crude Oil Futures, Bullish on Energy Exploration & Production Equities (XOP), Bearish on Airlines and Freight Transport Equities (JETS), and Bearish on Consumer Discretionary Equities (XLY).

## Rule 5: Concentrated Artificial Intelligence Infrastructure and Hyperscaler Capex
- **Condition:** Hyperscaler and corporate capital expenditure heavily concentrates into artificial intelligence accelerators, specialized data centers, and advanced semiconductor packaging, maintaining economic momentum despite broader macroeconomic deceleration.
- **Action:** Bullish on Mega-Cap Technology Equities (QQQ), Bullish on Semiconductor Equities (SMH), Bullish on Data Center Real Estate and Infrastructure Providers, and Bearish on Broad Industrial Small-Caps (IWM).

## Rule 6: Stagflationary Headwinds and Household Credit Deterioration
- **Condition:** Real GDP growth slows toward a sluggish 1.5% pace while borrowing costs reach multi-decade highs, increasing delinquency rates across revolving credit lines and auto loans for lower- and middle-income consumer cohorts.
- **Action:** Bullish on Consumer Staples Equities (XLP), Bullish on Large-Cap Quality Factor Equities (QUAL), Bearish on Subprime Consumer and Auto Lenders, and Bearish on Broad Discretionary Retailers (XRT).

## Rule 7: Clean Energy Capital Project Cancellations and Baseload Utility Realignment
- **Condition:** Punitive financing costs, supply chain bottlenecks, and severe interconnection delays prompt large-scale write-downs and cancellations of utility-scale renewable wind and solar projects, redirecting capital toward reliable baseload electricity and midstream natural gas.
- **Action:** Bearish on Global Clean Energy Equities (ICLN), Bearish on Solar Component Manufacturers (TAN), Bullish on Regulated Electric and Nuclear Utilities (XLU), and Bullish on Midstream Energy Infrastructure (AMLP).

## Rule 8: Clinical Labor Cost Inflation and Inpatient Healthcare Margin Compression
- **Condition:** Persistent shortages of specialized medical personnel and nursing staff drive elevated wage inflation that outpaces statutory insurer reimbursement rates, squeezing hospital operating margins within the broader healthcare economy.
- **Action:** Bullish on Large-Cap Pharmaceuticals and Medical Devices (XLV), Bearish on Hospital Operators, and Bearish on Acute Inpatient Facility Networks.

## Rule 9: Cross-Border Nearshoring and North American Supply Chain Integration
- **Condition:** Regional supply chain re-alignment and bilateral trade integration accelerate across the North American corridor, supporting cross-border industrial freight corridors, bilateral agricultural trade, and border logistics infrastructure.
- **Action:** Bullish on the Mexican Peso against G10 Currencies (MXN/EUR, MXN/JPY), Bullish on North American Cross-Border Class I Rail Equities, and Bearish on Trans-Pacific Container Shipping Carriers.
