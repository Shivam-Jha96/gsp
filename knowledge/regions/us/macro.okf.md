# United States Macro Trading Rules

## Rule 1: Federal Reserve Rate Lift-Off and Sustained Tightening Stance
- **Condition:** The Federal Reserve delivers an interest rate hike—marking its first increase in three years—defying executive and political pressure for monetary easing as re-accelerating inflation forces policymakers to maintain an active tightening posture.
- **Action:** Bullish on the US Dollar (USD), Bullish on Front-End Treasury Yields (2Y Yield), Bearish on Long-Duration Treasuries (TLT), and Bearish on Rate-Sensitive Small-Cap Equities (IWM).

## Rule 2: Energy-Driven Headline CPI Re-Acceleration
- **Condition:** Headline Consumer Price Index (CPI) reaches or exceeds 3.7% YoY, driven by surging retail gasoline and broader commodity costs that elevate short-to-medium term inflation expectations and push back market expectations for policy easing.
- **Action:** Bullish on Treasury Inflation-Protected Securities (TIPS), Bullish on the US Dollar (USD), Bearish on Intermediate Treasuries (IEF), and Bearish on Gold (XAU/USD).

## Rule 3: Sovereign Deficit Financing and Term Premium Bear Steepening
- **Condition:** Massive federal fiscal deficits and heavy multi-maturity Treasury coupon auctions collide with ongoing Federal Reserve balance sheet runoff (QT), driving sustained expansion in structural term premiums across the long end of the yield curve.
- **Action:** Bearish on Core Aggregate Fixed Income (AGG), Bearish on 30-Year Treasuries, Bullish on 10Y/2Y Yield Curve Steepener Trades, and Bullish on Ultra-Short Treasury Bills (BIL).

## Rule 4: Geopolitical Escalation and Crude Oil Supply Shocks
- **Condition:** Escalating geopolitical tensions and military conflict in key oil-producing corridors disrupt maritime transit routes and drive risk premia into global crude benchmarks, elevating input costs across transport and manufacturing sectors.
- **Action:** Bullish on WTI and Brent Crude Oil Futures, Bullish on Energy Exploration & Production Equities (XOP), Bearish on Passenger Airlines and Transportation Equities (JETS), and Bearish on Consumer Discretionary Equities (XLY).

## Rule 5: Concentrated AI Infrastructure Capex Driving Asymmetric GDP Growth
- **Condition:** Private non-residential fixed investment concentrates heavily in artificial intelligence compute hardware, data center construction, and hyperscale digital infrastructure, generating asymmetric headline GDP growth while traditional industrial capex softens.
- **Action:** Bullish on Mega-Cap Technology Equities (QQQ), Bullish on Semiconductor Equities (SMH), Bullish on Data Center Real Estate Investment Trusts, and Bearish on Broad Industrial Small-Caps (IWM).

## Rule 6: Clean Energy Capital Retrenchment and Grid Baseload Realignment
- **Condition:** Sustained high financing rates, supply chain bottlenecks, and grid interconnection delays trigger widespread cancellations of utility-scale renewables, redirecting critical power generation capital back toward conventional baseload power and midstream infrastructure.
- **Action:** Bearish on Global Clean Energy Equities (ICLN), Bearish on Solar and Wind Component Manufacturers (TAN), Bullish on Midstream Energy Infrastructure (AMLP), and Bullish on Traditional Integrated Power & Utilities (XLU).

## Rule 7: Cross-Border Industrial Tariffs and Auto Supply Chain Friction
- **Condition:** Imposition or escalation of punitive tariffs on cross-border automotive, steel, and industrial components from key trading partners impairs integrated supply chains, contracting corporate operating margins in heavy manufacturing.
- **Action:** Bullish on the US Dollar against the Canadian Dollar (USD/CAD) and Mexican Peso (USD/MXN), Bearish on Automotive Original Equipment Manufacturers (OEMs), and Bearish on Cyclical Industrials (XLI).

## Rule 8: Healthcare Clinical Wage Inflation and Hospital Operating Margin Compression
- **Condition:** Persistent clinical labor shortages and institutional wage growth outpace managed-care and statutory reimbursement adjustments, compressing operating margins for hospital operators and health systems.
- **Action:** Bullish on Large-Cap Pharmaceuticals and Medical Devices (XLV), Bearish on Healthcare Facility and Hospital Operators, and Bearish on Broad Consumer Discretionary Equities (XLY).
