# United States Macro Trading Rules

## Rule 1: Federal Reserve Rate Lift-Off and Sustained Tightening Guidance
- **Condition:** The Federal Reserve initiates an interest rate hike cycle—delivering its first hike in three years—and signals further tightening within the year despite political resistance, driven by reignited inflation expectations.
- **Action:** Bullish on the US Dollar (USD), Bullish on Front-End Treasury Yields (2Y Yield), Bearish on Long-Duration Treasuries (TLT), and Bearish on Rate-Sensitive Small-Cap Equities (IWM).

## Rule 2: Energy-Driven Headline CPI Re-Acceleration and Sticky Services Inflation
- **Condition:** Headline CPI accelerates to 3.7% or higher driven by sharp increases in retail fuel and energy commodities, while core services inflation remains rigid, cementing hawkish terminal rate pricing.
- **Action:** Bullish on Treasury Inflation-Protected Securities (TIPS), Bullish on the US Dollar (USD), Bearish on Intermediate Treasuries (IEF), and Bearish on Gold (XAU/USD).

## Rule 3: Sovereign Deficit Financing and Term Premium Bear Steepening
- **Condition:** Expanding federal fiscal deficits and heavy coupon issuance coincide with central bank balance sheet runoff, driving structural term premium expansion across the long end of the curve.
- **Action:** Bearish on Core Aggregate Fixed Income (AGG), Bearish on 30-Year Treasuries, Bullish on 10Y/2Y Yield Curve Steepener Trades, and Bullish on Ultra-Short Treasury Bills (BIL).

## Rule 4: Concentrated AI Infrastructure Capex Driving Asymmetric GDP Growth
- **Condition:** Private non-residential fixed investment disproportionately concentrates in artificial intelligence data centers, compute silicon, and hyperscale digital infrastructure, sustaining headline GDP while traditional corporate capex softens.
- **Action:** Bullish on Mega-Cap Technology Equities (QQQ), Bullish on Semiconductor Equities (SMH), and Bearish on Broad Industrial Small-Caps (IWM).

## Rule 5: Cross-Border Industrial Tariffs and Auto Supply Chain Friction
- **Condition:** The implementation or threat of punitive U.S. tariffs on cross-border automotive and industrial imports from North American trading partners impairs supply chain integration and elevates manufactured input costs.
- **Action:** Bullish on the US Dollar against the Canadian Dollar (USD/CAD), Bearish on Automotive Original Equipment Manufacturers (OEMs), and Bearish on Cyclical Industrials (XLI).

## Rule 6: Clean Energy Capital Retrenchment and Hydrocarbon Grid Reliance
- **Condition:** High hurdle rates, project cancellations, and regulatory interconnection backlogs trigger widespread capital retrenchment across utility-scale renewables, redirecting baseline capacity investment toward conventional thermal and midstream energy.
- **Action:** Bearish on Global Clean Energy Equities (ICLN), Bearish on Solar and Wind Component Manufacturers, and Bullish on Integrated Oil, Gas, and Midstream Infrastructure (XLE/AMLP).

## Rule 7: Healthcare Labor Inflation and Operating Margin Compression
- **Condition:** Persistent clinical wage inflation and rising medical delivery costs outpace reimbursement adjustments, eroding operational margins for care facilities while absorbing household discretionary budgets.
- **Action:** Bullish on Large-Cap Pharmaceuticals and Medical Devices (XLV), Bearish on Healthcare Facility and Hospital Operators, and Bearish on Broad Consumer Discretionary Equities (XLY).
