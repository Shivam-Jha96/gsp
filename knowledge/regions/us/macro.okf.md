# United States Macro Trading Rules

## Rule 1: Federal Reserve Rate Lift-Off and Sustained Tightening Guidance
- **Condition:** The Federal Reserve delivers an interest rate hike—its first in three years—and explicitly signals an additional hike within the calendar year, defying executive and political pressure for monetary easing amid persistent inflation.
- **Action:** Bullish on the US Dollar (USD), Bullish on Front-End Treasury Yields (2Y Yield), Bearish on Long-Duration Fixed Income (TLT), and Bearish on Rate-Sensitive Small-Cap Equities (IWM).

## Rule 2: Energy-Driven CPI Re-Acceleration and Sticky Services Inflation
- **Condition:** Headline CPI accelerates to 3.7% or higher driven by surging retail energy and fuel costs, while core services inflation remains stubbornly elevated, keeping terminal policy rate expectations high and forcing additional rate hikes onto the table.
- **Action:** Bullish on Treasury Inflation-Protected Securities (TIPS), Bullish on the US Dollar (USD), Bearish on Intermediate and Long-Duration Treasuries (IEF/TLT), and Bearish on Gold (XAU/USD).

## Rule 3: Sovereign Deficit Financing and Term Premium Expansion
- **Condition:** Persistent federal fiscal deficit expansion and heavy Treasury coupon issuance collide with quantitative tightening, expanding term premiums and forcing a bear steepening of the sovereign yield curve.
- **Action:** Bearish on Core Aggregate Fixed Income (AGG), Bearish on 30-Year Treasuries, Bullish on 10Y/2Y Yield Curve Steepeners, and Bullish on Ultra-Short T-Bills (BIL) and Cash Equivalents.

## Rule 4: Concentrated AI Infrastructure Capex Driving Asymmetric GDP Growth
- **Condition:** Private fixed investment disproportionately concentrates in artificial intelligence compute hardware, datacenters, and semiconductor supply chains, supporting headline GDP expansion while broader non-tech corporate capex remains subdued.
- **Action:** Bullish on Mega-Cap Technology Equities (QQQ), Bullish on Semiconductor Equities (SMH), and Bearish on Broad Industrial Small-Caps (IWM).

## Rule 5: Cross-Border Industrial Tariffs and Supply Chain Frictions
- **Condition:** Escalation or imposition of U.S. tariffs targeting integrated North American trading partners raises intermediate automotive and industrial input costs and disrupts cross-border manufacturing flows.
- **Action:** Bullish on the US Dollar against the Canadian Dollar (USD/CAD), Bearish on Cyclical Industrials (XLI), and Bearish on Automotive Original Equipment Manufacturers (OEMs).

## Rule 6: Clean Energy Capital Retrenchment and Traditional Energy Resilience
- **Condition:** Elevated financing costs, policy bottlenecks, and interconnection delays trigger large-scale project cancellations and employment losses across utility-scale renewables, redirecting private capital back toward conventional hydrocarbons and thermal generation.
- **Action:** Bearish on Clean Energy Equities (ICLN/QCLN), Bearish on Solar and Wind Equipment Manufacturers, and Bullish on Traditional Integrated Oil, Gas, and Midstream Infrastructure (XLE/AMLP).

## Rule 7: Healthcare Cost Inflation and Structural Labor Pressures
- **Condition:** Sustained healthcare wage pressures and rising clinical delivery costs capture a growing share of aggregate GDP, compressing operating margins for hospital systems while draining household discretionary spending capacity.
- **Action:** Bullish on Large-Cap Pharmaceuticals and Defensive Healthcare (XLV), Bearish on Margin-Compressed Healthcare Facility Operators, and Bearish on Broad Consumer Discretionary Equities (XLY).
