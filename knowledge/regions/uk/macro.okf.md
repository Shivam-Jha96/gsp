# United Kingdom Macro Trading Rules

## Rule 1: Imminent Monetary Tightening and Front-End Rate Repricing
- **Condition:** Headline UK CPI prints at 3.5% alongside above-consensus monthly GDP expansion (0.1%), compelling the Bank of England MPC to signal an active Q4 interest rate hike and completely extinguish remaining domestic rate-cut expectations.
- **Action:** Bearish on short-dated (2Y) UK Gilts and front-quarter SONIA futures; bullish on front-end SONIA implied policy rates and 1Y Forward Rate Agreements (FRAs).

## Rule 2: Fuel Surge Cost-Push Inflation and Breakeven Widening
- **Condition:** Escalating automotive fuel and wholesale energy costs drive UK headline inflation higher than forecast to 3.5%, re-anchoring medium-term inflation expectations well above the BoE’s 2.0% target.
- **Action:** Bullish on UK 5Y and 10Y inflation-linked Gilts (breakeven inflation rates) and FTSE 100 integrated energy producers; bearish on intermediate nominal Gilts and consumer discretionary equities.

## Rule 3: Autumn Budget Deficit Expansion and Fiscal Supply Steepening
- **Condition:** Escalating public sector net borrowing ahead of Chancellor John Healey’s first Autumn Budget forces the UK Debt Management Office (DMO) to expand gilt issuance quotas, lifting sovereign term premia.
- **Action:** Bearish on 10Y and 30Y nominal UK Gilts; bullish on UK Gilt 2s10s and 5s30s yield curve steepeners.

## Rule 4: Monthly GDP Resilience and Real Rate Expansion
- **Condition:** Monthly UK GDP expands by 0.1% in August, beating forecasts and confirming underlying macroeconomic resilience in the face of restrictive policy settings.
- **Action:** Bullish on front-end UK real yields and UK clearing bank net interest margins; bearish on long-duration sovereign fixed income and defensive government bond proxies.

## Rule 5: Policy Divergence and Sterling FX Outperformance
- **Condition:** Hawkish BoE rate hike pricing against an easing Federal Reserve and European Central Bank expands the UK nominal yield advantage, driving sterling appreciation.
- **Action:** Bullish on spot GBP/USD, GBP/EUR, and sterling call options; bearish on EUR/GBP.

## Rule 6: Disappearing Sub-5% Mortgages and Real Estate Contraction
- **Condition:** Rising benchmark swap rates eliminate sub-5% fixed-rate mortgage products across high-street lenders, triggering a refinancing shock, dampening housing turnover, and constricting household discretionary cash flow.
- **Action:** Bearish on UK residential homebuilders, domestic retail mortgage originators, and commercial property REITs; bullish on defensive consumer staples and high-yielding utilities.

## Rule 7: Active Quantitative Tightening and Gilt Swap Spread Cheapening
- **Condition:** The Bank of England sustains active gilt sales alongside run-off during heavy Autumn DMO issuance, congesting primary dealer balance sheets with sovereign paper.
- **Action:** Bearish on 10Y and 30Y nominal Gilts; bullish on 10Y and 30Y SONIA swap spreads (Gilts cheapening relative to swaps) and sterling repo rates.

## Rule 8: Multinational Large-Cap Outperformance vs. Domestic Mid-Caps
- **Condition:** The combination of 3.5% headline inflation, escalating mortgage borrowing costs, and impending Autumn Budget tax burdens depresses domestic UK aggregate demand while sterling-adjusted foreign revenue buffers multinational blue chips.
- **Action:** Bullish on the FTSE 100 / FTSE 250 equity spread (long FTSE 100, short FTSE 250); bearish on domestically oriented UK mid-cap retailers, leisure operators, and industrial cyclicals.
