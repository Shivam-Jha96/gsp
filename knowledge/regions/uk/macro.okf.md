# United Kingdom Macro Trading Rules

## Rule 1: Bank Rate Stabilization and Front-End Rate Re-pricing
- **Condition:** The Bank of England stabilizes the base rate at 4% with City economists pushing back against aggressive peak pricing expectations of 4.75% by next summer, signaling a pause in rapid tightening.
- **Action:** Bullish on front-end (2Y) nominal Gilts and 3M SONIA interest rate futures; bearish on 1Y and 2Y SONIA swap yields and aggressive upward rate re-pricing positions.

## Rule 2: Energy-Led CPI Resurgence and Inflation Breakeven Widening
- **Condition:** UK headline Consumer Prices Index (CPI) inflation ticks higher as high global oil prices haunt fuel pumps and industrial input costs, prompting Bank of England warnings that inflation will rise further.
- **Action:** Bullish on UK 5Y and 10Y Gilt breakeven inflation rates and short-dated index-linked Gilts (linkers); bearish on intermediate nominal Gilts and sterling investment-grade corporate credit.

## Rule 3: Sovereign Debt Overhang, Issuance Shifts, and Term Premium Steepening
- **Condition:** The Office for Budget Responsibility (OBR) outlines ongoing structural public deficit challenges and fiscal issuance profiles in its Economic and Fiscal Outlook, interacting with evolving Gilt market risk premia.
- **Action:** Bullish on UK Gilt 10Y-30Y term premia, 5s30s curve steepener trades, and 10Y Gilt-Bund yield spreads; bearish on outright 10Y and 30Y nominal Gilt prices.

## Rule 4: Terms-of-Trade Deterioration and Sterling Depreciation Pressure
- **Condition:** Spikes in global crude oil and imported commodity costs degrade the UK's current account balance and terms of trade, maintaining external valuation pressure on sterling.
- **Action:** Bullish on USD/GBP (bearish spot GBP/USD) and EUR/GBP; bearish on sterling cross-currency basis swaps.

## Rule 5: Sustained Mortgage Rate Pressure and Housing Sector Contraction
- **Condition:** The establishment of a 4% base rate environment and elevated borrowing costs impact consumer mortgage repayments and loan pricing, cooling transaction volumes and property market breadth.
- **Action:** Bullish on UK private residential rental yields and prime money market fund assets; bearish on UK residential housebuilder equities, domestic mortgage lenders, and residential REITs.

## Rule 6: Stagflationary Wedge and FTSE 100 vs. FTSE 250 Divergence
- **Condition:** Monthly GDP data prints (such as marginal 0.1% growth in August and flat-to-anemic historical readings) combine with elevated consumer price inflation and baseline borrowing costs to create a restrictive domestic economic drag.
- **Action:** Bullish on FTSE 100 multi-national energy, commodity, and foreign-earning defensive shares; bearish on domestically focused FTSE 250 consumer cyclicals, retail banking, and commercial property stocks.
