# United Kingdom Macro Trading Rules

## Rule 1: Hawkish MPC Guidance and Front-End Rate Repricing
- **Condition:** The Bank of England signals incremental policy rate hikes to counter persistent inflation, reinforced by hawkish rhetoric from the MPC Chief Economist indicating monetary tightening is not yet complete.
- **Action:** Bullish on 1Y and 2Y SONIA swap yields and clearing bank net interest margins; bearish on 3M SONIA interest rate futures and front-end (2Y) nominal Gilts.

## Rule 2: Inflation Resurgence and Breakeven Widening
- **Condition:** Services inflation and baseline price persistence trigger upside surprises in UK headline and core CPI prints, elevating short-to-medium inflation expectations.
- **Action:** Bullish on UK 5Y and 10Y Gilt breakeven inflation rates and short-dated index-linked Gilts; bearish on intermediate nominal Gilts and fixed-rate sterling investment-grade corporate credit.

## Rule 3: Sovereign Debt Overhang and Term Premium Steepening
- **Condition:** Expanding UK public debt issuance and structural deficit projections from the Office for Budget Responsibility (OBR) raise fiscal sustainability concerns and elevate the sovereign term premium.
- **Action:** Bullish on UK Gilt 2s10s and 5s30s curve steepeners and 10Y Gilt-Bund yield spreads; bearish on outright 10Y and 30Y nominal Gilt prices.

## Rule 4: Hawkish Policy Divergence and Sterling Strength
- **Condition:** The Bank of England maintains an active rate-hiking bias while peer central banks (Federal Reserve, ECB) pause or prepare rate cuts, widening front-end nominal rate differentials in favor of sterling.
- **Action:** Bullish on spot GBP/USD and GBP/EUR; bearish on EUR/GBP forwards and cross-currency basis swaps.

## Rule 5: Mortgage Rate Shock and Housing Market Contraction
- **Condition:** Rising policy rates flow through to standard variable and fixed-rate mortgage products, depressing first-time buyer enquiries, house prices, and residential transaction volumes.
- **Action:** Bullish on sterling money market funds, short-term cash instruments, and residential rental yields; bearish on UK residential housebuilders, domestic mortgage lenders, and retail/residential REITs.

## Rule 6: Monthly Output Resilience and Cyclical Sector Rebound
- **Condition:** Monthly UK GDP prints beat consensus expectations and avoid technical contraction, dampening immediate stagflation risks despite restrictive monetary conditions.
- **Action:** Bullish on domestic cyclical equities (FTSE 250 industrials and consumer cyclicals) and 5Y UK real yields; bearish on defensive bond-proxy equities (regulated utilities and telecoms).
