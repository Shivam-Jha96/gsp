# United Kingdom Macro Trading Rules

## Rule 1: Conditional Hawkish Hold and Front-End Rate Repricing
- **Condition:** The Bank of England holds the Bank Rate steady while the MPC and Chief Economist push back against market rate-cut pricing, signaling a potential Q4 rate hike if energy prices remain elevated.
- **Action:** Bearish on 2Y UK Gilts and short-dated SONIA futures; bullish on front-month SONIA implied volatility and 1Y forward rate agreements (FRAs).

## Rule 2: Energy-Driven CPI Rebound and Gilt Breakeven Widening
- **Condition:** Global oil and gas prices push headline UK CPI back up toward 3.1%, reigniting inflation persistence concerns despite restrictive base monetary conditions.
- **Action:** Bullish on UK 5Y and 10Y inflation-linked Gilts (breakevens) and FTSE 100 integrated energy producers; bearish on short-dated nominal Gilts.

## Rule 3: Sovereign Borrowing Headwinds and Gilt Curve Steepening
- **Condition:** Elevated public sector borrowing warnings from fiscal watchdogs ahead of the Budget coincide with surging DMO gilt issuance, lifting long-end sovereign term premia.
- **Action:** Bearish on 10Y and 30Y nominal UK Gilts; bullish on UK Gilt 2s10s and 5s30s yield curve steepener trades.

## Rule 4: Cyclical Growth Beats and Easing Cycle Pricing Purge
- **Condition:** Monthly UK GDP prints beat consensus expectations (e.g., expanding 0.1% or higher), demonstrating underlying economic resilience and forcing rate markets to price out near-term monetary easing.
- **Action:** Bullish on front-end UK real yields and commercial bank net interest margins; bearish on duration-heavy sovereign debt.

## Rule 5: Sterling Yield Divergence and Bullish FX Breakout
- **Condition:** Hawkish MPC forward guidance contrasts with rapid policy easing by the Federal Reserve and ECB, driving UK yield differentials wider and pushing GBP/USD decisively above key resistance at 1.3250.
- **Action:** Bullish on spot GBP/USD, GBP/EUR, and sterling call options; bearish on EUR/GBP.

## Rule 6: Mortgage Refinancing Friction and Domestic Credit Strain
- **Condition:** Elevated gilt yields and delayed policy rate cuts keep fixed-rate mortgage refinancing costs high, reducing household discretionary cash flows and increasing debt-service ratios.
- **Action:** Bearish on UK homebuilders, domestic retail banks, and commercial property REITs; bullish on defensive consumer staples and utilities.

## Rule 7: Quantitative Tightening Gilt Runoff and Swap Spread Compression
- **Condition:** The Bank of England sustains active gilt sales alongside balance sheet runoff amid heavy primary sovereign issuance, straining primary dealer absorption capacity.
- **Action:** Bearish on long-dated Gilts and UK sovereign repo liquidity; bullish on 10Y and 30Y SONIA swap spreads (Gilts cheapening relative to swaps).

## Rule 8: Large-Cap Multinational Equity Outperformance vs. Domestic Mid-Caps
- **Condition:** Resilient global demand, sterling strength, and commodity price firming drive the FTSE 100 toward new highs (e.g., above 10,800), while domestic monetary tightness constrains FTSE 250 performance.
- **Action:** Bullish on the FTSE 100 / FTSE 250 equity spread (long FTSE 100, short FTSE 250); bearish on domestically oriented small-cap industrials.
