# United Kingdom Macro Trading Rules

## Rule 1: BoE Hawkish Pivot and Front-End Rate Repricing
- **Condition:** The Bank of England shifts its tone toward rate hikes as inflation pressures mount and are projected to top 4%, prompting market re-pricing of terminal rates above prior stable expectations.
- **Action:** Bullish on short-term interest rate futures and front-end SONIA rate-hike strips; bearish on short-dated (2Y) UK Gilts.

## Rule 2: Geopolitical Energy Shocks and Breakeven Widening
- **Condition:** Escalating geopolitical tensions threaten renewed energy price spikes, sustaining high cost-of-living impacts and raising risks of inflation topping 3.5% to 4%.
- **Action:** Bullish on UK 5Y and 10Y inflation-linked Gilts (breakeven inflation rates); bearish on intermediate nominal Gilts and fixed-rate sterling corporate credit.

## Rule 3: Sovereign Issuance Burden and Term Premium Compression
- **Condition:** Persistent DMO sovereign issuance supply and active Bank of England Quantitative Tightening maintain heavy absorption requirements, driving structural upward pressure on Gilt yields.
- **Action:** Bullish on UK Gilt 2s10s yield curve steepener trades; bearish on 10Y and 30Y nominal Gilt outright prices.

## Rule 4: Resilient Economic Growth and Real Yield Support
- **Condition:** Monthly UK GDP prints beat consensus expectations (e.g., 0.1% MoM expansion), confirming cyclical resilience, dispelling near-term recession fears, and validating higher-for-longer real interest rates.
- **Action:** Bullish on UK 5Y real yields and major UK clearing bank net interest margins; bearish on interest-rate-sensitive utilities and long-duration sovereign debt.

## Rule 5: Growth Beat and Relative Monetary Stance Driving Sterling Strength
- **Condition:** Resilient domestic GDP growth alongside the Bank of England's hawkish policy shift outpaces softening foreign growth environments and relative rate trajectories.
- **Action:** Bullish on spot GBP/EUR and GBP/USD; bearish on EUR/GBP forward curves.

## Rule 6: High Policy Plateau and Mortgage Refinancing Strain
- **Condition:** With policy rates adjusting upward or staying at elevated plateaus, structural swap rates perpetuate household mortgage affordability headwinds and depress housing turnover.
- **Action:** Bearish on UK residential homebuilders, specialist mortgage originators, and commercial property REITs; bullish on defensive consumer staples.

## Rule 7: Quantitative Tightening and Swap Spread Cheapening
- **Condition:** The Bank of England sustains active Gilt divestment from the Asset Purchase Facility in tandem with substantial HM Treasury debt issuance, placing heavy absorption requirements on primary dealers.
- **Action:** Bullish on 10Y and 30Y SONIA swap spreads (Gilts cheapening relative to swaps) and sterling repo rates; bearish on benchmark 10Y nominal Gilts.

## Rule 8: Exporter Resilience vs. Domestic Demand Compression
- **Condition:** Elevated financing costs and persistent cost-of-living pressures constrain domestic discretionary spending, while international diversification protects global mega-cap balance sheets.
- **Action:** Bullish on the FTSE 100 / FTSE 250 ratio (long FTSE 100, short FTSE 250); bearish on domestically focused UK consumer discretionary retailers and leisure operators.
