# United Kingdom Macro Trading Rules

## Rule 1: Hawkish MPC Guidance and Front-End Rate Repricing
- **Condition:** The Bank of England signals near-term policy rate hikes beyond 4.0% toward 5.0% amid persistent underlying price pressures and a commitment to higher-for-longer monetary policy.
- **Action:** Bullish on 1Y SONIA swap yields and clearing bank net interest margins; bearish on 3M SONIA interest rate futures and front-end (2Y) nominal Gilts.

## Rule 2: Inflation Resurgence and Breakeven Widening
- **Condition:** Base effects and persistent service and energy costs reverse temporary disinflation dips, driving headline CPI prints back toward 3.5% and unanchoring short-to-medium inflation expectations.
- **Action:** Bullish on UK 5Y and 10Y Gilt breakeven inflation rates and short-dated index-linked Gilts; bearish on intermediate nominal Gilts and fixed-rate sterling investment-grade corporate bonds.

## Rule 3: Shorter-Tenor Gilt Issuance and Curve Steepening
- **Condition:** The Debt Management Office shifts funding toward shorter-dated maturities while persistent fiscal deficits and heavy sovereign debt supply expand term premiums across long-dated paper.
- **Action:** Bullish on UK Gilt 2s10s and 5s30s yield curve steepener trades; bearish on 10Y and 30Y nominal Gilt outright prices.

## Rule 4: Hawkish Policy Divergence and Sterling Outperformance
- **Condition:** Monetary policy expectations diverge as the Bank of England guides toward additional rate hikes while peer central banks pause or execute easing cycles, widening short-term nominal yield differentials in favor of the UK.
- **Action:** Bullish on spot GBP/USD and GBP/EUR; bearish on EUR/GBP forward contracts and unhedged foreign fixed-income assets.

## Rule 5: Mortgage Rate Transmission and Property Market Contraction
- **Condition:** Bank of England base rate increases immediately pass through to variable-rate borrowing and elevated fixed-rate refinancing hurdles, dampening household discretionary purchasing power and housing turnover.
- **Action:** Bullish on residential private rental operators and high-yield sterling liquid cash funds; bearish on UK residential homebuilders, specialist mortgage finance providers, and domestic retail REITs.

## Rule 6: Positive Monthly Growth Surprises and Cyclical Equity Support
- **Condition:** Monthly UK GDP prints beat consensus forecasts with positive expansion, easing immediate stagflation and recession fears despite restrictive monetary conditions.
- **Action:** Bullish on UK domestic cyclical equities (FTSE 250 value and industrials) and 5Y UK real yields; bearish on long-duration defensive utilities and sovereign safe-haven proxy assets.
