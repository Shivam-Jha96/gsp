# United Kingdom Macro Trading Rules

## Rule 1: Higher-for-Longer Policy Stance via Geopolitical and Energy Shocks
- **Condition:** The Bank of England signals a prolonged restrictive policy rate plateau, citing geopolitical conflict in energy-producing regions and warnings that consumer price inflation will accelerate further.
- **Action:** Bullish on 1Y and 2Y SONIA swap yields and short-sterling rate expectations; bearish on 3M SONIA interest rate futures and front-end (2Y) nominal Gilts.

## Rule 2: Energy-Led CPI Resurgence and Breakeven Widening
- **Condition:** Headline UK CPI accelerates above forecasts driven by escalating fuel, power, and supply-chain energy costs, lifting medium-term retail and consumer inflation expectations.
- **Action:** Bullish on UK 5Y and 10Y Gilt breakeven inflation rates and short-dated index-linked Gilts (linkers); bearish on intermediate nominal Gilts and sterling investment-grade corporate credit.

## Rule 3: Sovereign Debt Overhang and Term Premium Steepening
- **Condition:** Long-term fiscal sustainability warnings, structural deficit projections by the Office for Budget Responsibility (OBR), and heavy debt issuance clash with ongoing Bank of England quantitative tightening (QT).
- **Action:** Bullish on UK Gilt 10Y-30Y term premia, 5s30s curve steepener trades, and 10Y Gilt-Bund yield spreads; bearish on outright 10Y and 30Y nominal Gilt prices.

## Rule 4: Terms-of-Trade Deterioration and Sterling Depreciation
- **Condition:** Sharp increases in global crude oil and energy import prices degrade the UK's current account balance and terms of trade, outstripping nominal interest rate differentials against reserve currencies.
- **Action:** Bullish on USD/GBP (bearish spot GBP/USD) and EUR/GBP; bearish on sterling cross-currency basis swaps.

## Rule 5: Sustained Mortgage Rate Pressure and Housing Sector Contraction
- **Condition:** Front-end swap rates remain elevated, preventing relief in standard fixed-rate mortgage pricing and depressing mortgage approvals, transaction volumes, and builder sentiment.
- **Action:** Bullish on UK private residential rental yields and prime money market fund assets; bearish on UK residential housebuilder equities, domestic mortgage lenders, and residential REITs.

## Rule 6: Stagflationary Wedge and FTSE 100 vs. FTSE 250 Divergence
- **Condition:** Monthly GDP displays flat-to-anemic growth alongside elevated inflation prints and high domestic borrowing costs, creating a stagflationary drag on the UK domestic economy.
- **Action:** Bullish on FTSE 100 multi-national energy, commodity, and foreign-earning defensive shares; bearish on domestically focused FTSE 250 consumer cyclicals, retail banking, and commercial property stocks.
