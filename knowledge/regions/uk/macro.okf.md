# United Kingdom Macro Trading Rules

## Rule 1: Conditional Hawkish Hold and Front-End Rate Repricing
- **Condition:** The Bank of England holds the Bank Rate steady while the MPC signals potential Q4 rate hikes or extended high-for-longer policy if energy-driven price pressures persist, aggressively pushing back against market rate-cut pricing.
- **Action:** Bearish on 2Y UK Gilts and short-dated SONIA futures; bullish on front-month SONIA implied volatility and 1Y forward rate agreements (FRAs).

## Rule 2: Energy-Driven CPI Rebound and Gilt Breakeven Widening
- **Condition:** Global oil and gas price shocks push headline UK CPI back up toward 3.1%, reigniting inflation persistence risks despite restrictive baseline policy.
- **Action:** Bullish on UK 5Y and 10Y inflation-linked Gilts (breakevens) and FTSE 100 integrated energy producers; bearish on short-dated nominal Gilts.

## Rule 3: Fiscal Deficit Headwinds and Gilt Curve Steepening
- **Condition:** Fiscal watchdog warnings from the IFS and OBR highlight surging public sector borrowing requirements ahead of the Budget, lifting long-end sovereign debt issuance and expanding term premia.
- **Action:** Bearish on 10Y and 30Y nominal UK Gilts; bullish on UK Gilt 2s10s and 5s30s yield curve steepener trades.

## Rule 4: Cyclical Growth Beats and Easing Cycle Pricing Purge
- **Condition:** Monthly UK GDP prints beat consensus expectations (e.g., expanding 0.1% or higher month-over-month), demonstrating underlying economic resilience and forcing swap markets to price out near-term monetary easing.
- **Action:** Bullish on front-end UK real yields and commercial bank net interest margins; bearish on duration-heavy sovereign debt.

## Rule 5: Sterling Yield Divergence and Bullish FX Breakout
- **Condition:** Hawkish MPC forward guidance contrasts with aggressive policy easing by the Federal Reserve and ECB, widening UK yield differentials and propelling spot GBP/USD above key technical resistance at 1.3250.
- **Action:** Bullish on spot GBP/USD, GBP/EUR, and sterling call options; bearish on EUR/GBP.

## Rule 6: Mortgage Refinancing Friction and Domestic Credit Strain
- **Condition:** High front-end Gilt yields and delayed rate cuts drive 2-year and 5-year fixed mortgage rates higher, tightening household discretionary cash flows and elevating debt-service ratios.
- **Action:** Bearish on UK homebuilders, domestic retail banks, and commercial property REITs; bullish on defensive consumer staples and utilities.

## Rule 7: Quantitative Tightening Runoff and Swap Spread Compression
- **Condition:** The Bank of England sustains active gilt sales alongside balance sheet runoff amid heavy DMO primary issuance, straining dealer balance sheets and sovereign liquidity.
- **Action:** Bearish on long-dated Gilts and UK sovereign repo liquidity; bullish on 10Y and 30Y SONIA swap spreads (Gilts cheapening relative to swaps).

## Rule 8: Multinational Large-Cap Outperformance vs. Domestic Mid-Caps
- **Condition:** Resilient global demand, firming commodity prices, and high domestic borrowing costs widen the performance wedge between international earners and the UK domestic economy.
- **Action:** Bullish on the FTSE 100 / FTSE 250 equity spread (long FTSE 100, short FTSE 250); bearish on domestically focused small-cap industrials and discretionary retailers.
