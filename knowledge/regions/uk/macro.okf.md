# United Kingdom Macro Trading Rules

## Rule 1: BoE Rate Hike Cycle and Front-End Repricing
- **Condition:** The Bank of England executes an interest rate hike to 4.0%—marking its first rate increase in over three years—and signals that further policy tightening may follow to counter persistent price pressures.
- **Action:** Bullish on clearing bank net interest margins and 1Y SONIA swap yields; bearish on 3M SONIA interest rate futures and front-end (2Y) nominal Gilts.

## Rule 2: Headline CPI Resurgence and Inflation Breakevens
- **Condition:** Rising energy inputs and stubborn cost-of-living components push headline UK CPI toward 3.5%, reigniting near-term inflation expectations and eroding real cash yields.
- **Action:** Bullish on UK 5Y and 10Y inflation-linked Gilts (breakeven inflation rates); bearish on intermediate nominal Gilts and sterling fixed-rate investment-grade corporate credit.

## Rule 3: Debt Issuance Shortening and Curve Steepening
- **Condition:** Office for Budget Responsibility borrowing projections remain high while the Debt Management Office relies on shorter-dated Gilt issuance to finance public sector deficits, shifting duration risk premiums outward.
- **Action:** Bullish on UK Gilt 2s10s and 5s30s yield curve steepener trades; bearish on 10Y and 30Y nominal Gilt outright prices.

## Rule 4: Hawkish Policy Divergence and Sterling Strength
- **Condition:** The Bank of England accelerates policy tightening to 4.0% with open-ended guidance while peer central banks maintain monetary easing or hold rates steady, widening nominal yield differentials in favor of the UK.
- **Action:** Bullish on spot GBP/USD and GBP/EUR; bearish on EUR/GBP forward contracts and unhedged foreign bond holdings.

## Rule 5: Household Mortgage Pass-Through and Housing Market Strain
- **Condition:** The BoE base rate increase to 4.0% mechanically lifts tracker mortgage servicing costs and drives higher fixed-rate refinancing hurdles, curbing disposable household income and residential property transaction volumes.
- **Action:** Bullish on residential private rental operators and high-yield cash deposits; bearish on UK residential homebuilders, specialist mortgage lenders, and retail/commercial property REITs.

## Rule 6: Growth Beat Dispelling Stagflation Fears
- **Condition:** Monthly GDP prints consistently beat consensus expectations (registering positive month-on-month growth), confirming domestic macro resilience and pricing out immediate recessionary risk premiums.
- **Action:** Bullish on UK domestic cyclical equities (FTSE 250 value segment) and 5Y UK real yields; bearish on long-duration defensive utilities and sovereign safe-haven proxy debt.
