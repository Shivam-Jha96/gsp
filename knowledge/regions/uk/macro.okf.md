# United Kingdom Macro Trading Rules

## Rule 1: Conditional Hawkish Hold and Front-End Rate Repricing
- **Condition:** The Bank of England holds the Bank Rate steady while the MPC explicitly signals potential Q4 rate hikes or an extended high-for-longer stance if energy-driven price pressures persist, dismantling market pricing for near-term rate cuts.
- **Action:** Bearish on 2Y UK Gilts and short-dated SONIA futures; bullish on front-month SONIA implied volatility and 1Y forward rate agreements (FRAs).

## Rule 2: Energy-Driven Headline CPI Rebound and Breakeven Widening
- **Condition:** Oil and gas price spikes push headline UK CPI back up toward 3.1% even as domestic food inflation remains benign, widening the wedge between headline inflation risk and core goods disinflation.
- **Action:** Bullish on UK 5Y and 10Y inflation-linked Gilts (breakevens) and FTSE 100 integrated energy producers; bearish on short-to-intermediate nominal Gilts.

## Rule 3: Autumn Budget Deficit Concerns and Gilt Curve Steepening
- **Condition:** Fiscal watchdog warnings from the OBR and IFS highlight surging public sector borrowing requirements ahead of the Budget, forcing the DMO to lift long-end gilt issuance and inflating term premia.
- **Action:** Bearish on 10Y and 30Y nominal UK Gilts; bullish on UK Gilt 2s10s and 5s30s yield curve steepener trades.

## Rule 4: Monthly GDP Expansion and Easing Cycle Purge
- **Condition:** Monthly UK GDP prints beat consensus expectations (e.g., expanding 0.1% or higher month-over-month), demonstrating underlying economic resilience and forcing interest rate swap markets to defer rate cut expectations.
- **Action:** Bullish on front-end UK real yields and commercial bank net interest margins; bearish on duration-heavy sovereign debt.

## Rule 5: Sterling Yield Divergence and Bullish FX Breakout
- **Condition:** Hawkish MPC forward guidance contrasts with easing cycles at the Federal Reserve and ECB, driving UK-US and UK-EZ policy rate differentials wider and propelling spot GBP/USD sustainably above key resistance at 1.3250.
- **Action:** Bullish on spot GBP/USD, GBP/EUR, and sterling call options; bearish on EUR/GBP.

## Rule 6: Mortgage Refinancing Friction and Domestic Credit Tightening
- **Condition:** Elevated front-end Gilt yields and central bank policy inertia push 2-year and 5-year fixed mortgage rates higher, tightening household discretionary cash flows and increasing debt-service burdens.
- **Action:** Bearish on UK residential homebuilders, domestic retail banks, and commercial property REITs; bullish on defensive consumer staples and regulated utilities.

## Rule 7: Quantitative Tightening Acceleration and Swap Spread Widening
- **Condition:** The Bank of England maintains or accelerates its pace of active gilt sales alongside redemption runoff during its annual QT review, compounding DMO primary supply and straining gilt market absorption.
- **Action:** Bearish on long-dated Gilts and UK sovereign repo liquidity; bullish on 10Y and 30Y SONIA swap spreads (Gilts cheapening relative to swaps).

## Rule 8: Multinational Large-Cap Outperformance vs. Domestic Mid-Caps
- **Condition:** Global commodity firming, international revenue exposure, and elevated domestic financing costs expand the corporate profitability spread between global multinationals and the UK domestic economy.
- **Action:** Bullish on the FTSE 100 / FTSE 250 equity spread (long FTSE 100, short FTSE 250); bearish on domestically focused UK small-cap industrials and consumer discretionary retailers.
