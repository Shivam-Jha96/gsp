# United Kingdom Macro Trading Rules

## Rule 1: Conditional Hawkish Hold and Front-End Rate Repricing
- **Condition:** The Bank of England maintains the Bank Rate at 3.75% while the MPC and Chief Economist push back against market rate-cut pricing, signaling a potential Q4 hike contingent on persistent energy inflation.
- **Action:** Bearish on 2Y UK Gilts and short-dated SONIA futures; bullish on front-month SONIA implied volatility and 1Y forward rate agreements (FRAs).

## Rule 2: Disinflationary Headline Print and Front-End Relief
- **Condition:** Headline CPI decelerates toward 3.1%, delivering temporary relief to monetary policymakers despite persistent services inflation and elevated household cost-of-living metrics.
- **Action:** Bullish on short-dated UK Gilts and front-end SONIA swap spreads; bearish on GBP/USD and short-dated UK-US government bond yield spreads.

## Rule 3: Energy Supply Shock and Gilt Breakeven Widening
- **Condition:** Geopolitical escalation in the Middle East disrupts global crude and natural gas supplies, elevating UK retail energy caps and long-run market inflation expectations.
- **Action:** Bullish on UK 5Y and 10Y inflation-linked Gilts (breakevens) and FTSE 100 integrated energy equities; bearish on FTSE 250 consumer discretionary shares.

## Rule 4: Sovereign Borrowing Warnings and Gilt Curve Steepening
- **Condition:** High public sector net borrowing (PSNB) figures and elevated UK Debt Management Office (DMO) bond issuance compress sovereign fiscal headroom and revive gilt term premia.
- **Action:** Bearish on 10Y and 30Y nominal UK Gilts; bullish on UK Gilt 2s10s and 5s30s yield curve steepeners and 10Y asset swap spreads.

## Rule 5: Cyclical Growth Resilience and Easing Pushback
- **Condition:** Quarterly UK GDP delivers an upside surprise (expanding 0.6%), confirming domestic cyclical resilience and forcing money markets to price out policy easing across the forecast horizon.
- **Action:** Bullish on front-end UK real yields and GBP/EUR; bearish on duration-sensitive long-dated sovereign debt.

## Rule 6: Mortgage Refinancing Cliff and Retail Credit Stress
- **Condition:** Higher terminal policy rate expectations and sustained gilt yields transmit into sharp resets for fixed-rate mortgages, depressing household disposable income and elevating loan delinquency risks.
- **Action:** Bearish on UK residential homebuilders, domestic mortgage banks, and commercial property REITs; bullish on UK non-discretionary consumer staples and defensive utilities.

## Rule 7: Sterling Yield Divergence and Cross-Currency Breakout
- **Condition:** Hawkish MPC rhetoric contrasts with accelerating policy easing by the Federal Reserve and ECB, driving sterling yield differentials wider and propelling GBP/USD above key technical resistance (e.g., 1.3250).
- **Action:** Bullish on spot GBP/USD and GBP/EUR; bearish on foreign currency-denominated debt hedging costs for UK corporate issuers.

## Rule 8: Quantitative Tightening Gilt Disposal and Swap Spread Compression
- **Condition:** The Bank of England accelerates active gilt sales (QT) in its annual target review, flooding primary dealer balance sheets with long-end supply amid reduced market liquidity.
- **Action:** Bearish on long-dated nominal Gilts and Gilt repo liquidity; bullish on UK 30Y SONIA swap spreads (Gilts cheapening relative to swaps).

## Rule 9: Exporter Multinationals vs. Domestic Mid-Cap Divergence
- **Condition:** Elevated commodity prices and global demand support multinational corporate earnings, while domestic monetary restriction and high borrowing costs suppress UK domestic demand.
- **Action:** Bullish on the FTSE 100 / FTSE 250 equity spread (long FTSE 100, short FTSE 250); bearish on UK domestically focused small-cap industrials.
