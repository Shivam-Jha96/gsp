# United Kingdom Macro Trading Rules

## Rule 1: BoE Hawkish Pivot and Front-End Rate Repricing
- **Condition:** The Bank of England pivots toward imminent policy rate increases—marking the first rate hike cycle since 2023—as inflation re-accelerates past 3.5% toward 4%, forcing markets to price out rate cuts and reprice higher terminal rates.
- **Action:** Bullish on 2Y UK Gilt yields and clearing bank net interest margins; bearish on 3M SONIA interest rate futures and front-end (2Y) nominal Gilts.

## Rule 2: Energy-Driven CPI Resurgence and Breakeven Widening
- **Condition:** Volatile retail energy prices and cost-of-living pressures push headline CPI above 3.5% with upside risks beyond 4.0%, structurally elevating near-term and medium-term inflation expectations.
- **Action:** Bullish on UK 5Y and 10Y inflation-linked Gilts (breakeven inflation rates); bearish on intermediate nominal Gilts and fixed-rate sterling investment-grade corporate bonds.

## Rule 3: Pre-Budget Borrowing Expansion and Yield Curve Steepening
- **Condition:** Public sector net borrowing expands ahead of HM Treasury's autumn fiscal budgets, compounding heavy Debt Management Office (DMO) sovereign gilt supply and driving up term premium.
- **Action:** Bullish on UK Gilt 2s10s and 5s30s yield curve steepener trades; bearish on 10Y and 30Y nominal Gilt outright prices.

## Rule 4: Domestic Growth Beats and Real Yield Realignment
- **Condition:** Monthly UK GDP prints beat consensus expectations (e.g., MoM expansion prints), dispelling stagflationary recession scenarios and supporting the transmission of higher-for-longer real interest rates.
- **Action:** Bullish on UK 5Y real yields and the British Pound; bearish on long-duration UK infrastructure debt and interest-rate-sensitive utilities.

## Rule 5: Hawkish Policy Divergence Driving Sterling Outperformance
- **Condition:** The Bank of England maintains a hawkish tightening bias in response to persistent inflation while major international central banks (such as the Federal Reserve and ECB) pause or pursue rate-cutting cycles.
- **Action:** Bullish on spot GBP/USD and GBP/EUR; bearish on EUR/GBP forward contracts and foreign currency sovereign debt unhedged against sterling.

## Rule 6: Mortgage Refinancing Squeeze and Housing Market Deterioration
- **Condition:** Persistent terminal rate expectations and elevated multi-year swap rates lift residential mortgage borrowing costs, dampening housing turnover, buyer affordability, and loan origination volumes.
- **Action:** Bullish on residential private rental yield operators and defensive consumer staples; bearish on UK residential homebuilders, specialist mortgage lenders, and commercial property REITs.

## Rule 7: Active Quantitative Tightening and Asset Purchase Facility Absorption
- **Condition:** The Bank of England accelerates or sustains active Gilt sales via the Asset Purchase Facility (APF) concurrently with high HM Treasury debt issuance, straining primary dealer balance sheets.
- **Action:** Bullish on sterling general collateral repo rates and SONIA swap spreads (Gilts cheapening vs. swaps); bearish on benchmark 10Y nominal Gilts.

## Rule 8: Stagflationary Squeeze and Equity Factor Divergence
- **Condition:** Domestic cost-of-living constraints and elevated corporate funding costs depress domestic retail demand, while global macroeconomic demand supports multinational revenue streams.
- **Action:** Bullish on the FTSE 100 / FTSE 250 equity pairs trade (long FTSE 100, short FTSE 250); bearish on domestically focused UK mid-cap consumer discretionary retailers and leisure operators.
