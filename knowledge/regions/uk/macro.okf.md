# United Kingdom Macro Trading Rules

## Rule 1: Conditional Hawkish Hold and Front-End Rate Repricing
- **Condition:** The Bank of England holds the Bank Rate at 3.75% while explicitly signalling a potential Q4 rate hike contingent on Middle East conflict escalation and sustained energy import inflation.
- **Action:** Bearish on 2Y UK Gilts and short-dated SONIA futures; bullish on front-month SONIA implied volatility and 1Y forward rate agreements (FRAs).

## Rule 2: Disinflationary Print Relief and Dovish Re-anchoring
- **Condition:** Headline CPI decelerates to 3.1%, weakening the MPC's immediate rate hike impetus despite elevated underlying cost-of-living metrics and services persistence.
- **Action:** Bullish on short-dated UK Gilts and SONIA futures; bearish on GBP/USD and GBP short-term yield spreads against EUR and USD.

## Rule 3: Energy Pass-Through and Gilt Breakeven Widening
- **Condition:** Geopolitical friction in the Middle East sustains an imported crude and natural gas supply shock, driving market-based inflation expectations higher while squeezing domestic disposable income.
- **Action:** Bullish on UK 5Y and 10Y inflation-linked Gilts (breakevens) and FTSE 100 integrated energy producers; bearish on FTSE 250 Consumer Discretionary equities.

## Rule 4: Sovereign Debt Supply and Gilt Curve Steepening
- **Condition:** Escalating public sector net borrowing warnings and heavy UK Debt Management Office (DMO) bond issuance put fiscal growth plans at risk and revive sovereign term premia.
- **Action:** Bearish on 10Y and 30Y nominal Gilts; bullish on UK Gilt yield curve steepeners (2s10s and 5s30s) and long-end asset swap spreads.

## Rule 5: Cyclical Growth Resilience and Easing Pushback
- **Condition:** Quarterly UK GDP delivers an upside surprise (e.g., 0.6% expansion), demonstrating cyclical resilience that forces money markets to price out monetary policy easing through late-cycle horizons.
- **Action:** Bullish on UK front-end real yields and GBP/EUR; bearish on duration-sensitive long-dated sovereign bonds.

## Rule 6: Mortgage Refinancing Cliff and Domestic Credit Squeeze
- **Condition:** Transmission of the 3.75% Bank Rate into reset dates for fixed-rate mortgages drives household borrowing costs higher and accelerates retail credit delinquency risks.
- **Action:** Bearish on UK residential homebuilders, domestic retail banks, and commercial property REITs; bullish on defensive UK regulated utilities and non-discretionary consumer staples.

## Rule 7: Fiscal-Monetary Friction and Sovereign Risk Premia
- **Condition:** Rising UK Gilt yields constrain government growth agendas and revive scrutiny over UK fiscal credibility and debt sustainability.
- **Action:** Bearish on domestic UK corporate credit spreads and 10Y Gilt swap spreads; bullish on USD-earning multinational FTSE 100 exporters relative to the broader domestic market.

## Rule 8: Exporter Multinationals vs. Domestic Mid-Cap Divergence
- **Condition:** Global commodity strength driven by geopolitical supply shocks pairs with domestic growth headwinds from restrictive monetary policy and heavy tax burdens.
- **Action:** Bullish on the FTSE 100 / FTSE 250 equity spread (long FTSE 100, short FTSE 250); bearish on UK domestically focused small-cap industrials.
