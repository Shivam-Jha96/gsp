# United Kingdom Macro Trading Rules

## Rule 1: BoE Precautionary Tightening Cycle and Front-End Yield Surge
- **Condition:** Headline inflation jumping to 3.5% alongside resilient monthly GDP growth (0.1% MoM) triggers expectations of an impending Bank of England rate hike cycle kicking off in November.
- **Action:** Bearish on front-quarter SONIA futures and 2Y UK Gilts; bullish on 1Y forward rates and front-end SONIA implied yields.

## Rule 2: Inflation Acceleration and Breakeven Widening
- **Condition:** Headline CPI accelerates to 3.5%, exceeding forecasts and demonstrating persistent price pressures across energy, administered services, and core components.
- **Action:** Bullish on UK 5Y and 10Y inflation-linked Gilts (breakeven inflation rates); bearish on intermediate nominal Gilts and fixed-rate sterling corporate debt.

## Rule 3: Autumn Budget Borrowing Climb and Fiscal Supply Steepening
- **Condition:** Surging public sector borrowing ahead of Chancellor John Healey's first Autumn Budget intensifies Gilt issuance supply pressure and revives sovereign term premia concerns.
- **Action:** Bearish on 10Y and 30Y nominal UK Gilts; bullish on UK Gilt 2s10s and 5s30s yield curve steepeners.

## Rule 4: Macroeconomic Stamina and Real Yield Expansion
- **Condition:** Successive positive GDP prints (0.1% MoM in August) beat consensus expectations, dispelling imminent recession risks and validating higher neutral policy rate assumptions.
- **Action:** Bullish on UK 5Y real yields and major UK clearing bank net interest margins; bearish on long-duration sovereign fixed income and interest-rate-sensitive utilities.

## Rule 5: Sterling Fiscal Discounting vs. Cross-Currency Divergence
- **Condition:** Pre-Budget fiscal borrowing anxieties and sovereign risk discount drive spot GBP toward 1.3300 against the USD, even as hawkish BoE hike expectations support sterling against a loosening ECB.
- **Action:** Bullish on GBP/EUR and GBP/USD short-dated implied volatility; bearish on spot GBP/USD during pre-Budget fiscal risk pricing windows.

## Rule 6: Mortgage Refinancing Cliff and Property Sector Compression
- **Condition:** Gilt yield spikes and pricing of BoE rate hikes elevate prevailing mortgage swap rates, exacerbating refinancing distress for borrowers rolling off fixed rates and stalling housing turnover.
- **Action:** Bearish on UK residential homebuilders, specialist mortgage originators, and commercial property REITs; bullish on defensive consumer staples.

## Rule 7: Quantitative Tightening and Swap Spread Cheapening
- **Condition:** The Bank of England maintains active Gilt sales alongside expanded DMO sovereign issuance, overwhelming primary dealer balance sheets and cheapening sovereign cash bonds relative to derivatives.
- **Action:** Bullish on 10Y and 30Y SONIA swap spreads (Gilts cheapening relative to swaps) and sterling repo rates; bearish on benchmark 10Y nominal Gilts.

## Rule 8: Multinational Mega-Caps vs. Domestic Mid-Cap Divergence
- **Condition:** Persistent inflation at 3.5%, rising borrowing costs, and fiscal tightening dampen domestic household demand, while global earnings exposure insulates top-tier exporters.
- **Action:** Bullish on the FTSE 100 / FTSE 250 ratio (long FTSE 100, short FTSE 250); bearish on domestically focused UK discretionary retailers and leisure operators.
