# United Kingdom Macro Trading Rules

## Rule 1: Imminent Monetary Tightening and Front-End Rate Repricing
- **Condition:** Headline UK CPI jumps to 3.5% alongside resilient monthly growth, prompting the Bank of England MPC to prepare an active Q4 interest rate hike and unprice remaining monetary easing expectations.
- **Action:** Bearish on short-dated (2Y) UK Gilts and front-quarter SONIA futures; bullish on front-end SONIA implied rate hike pricing and 1Y Forward Rate Agreements (FRAs).

## Rule 2: Inflationary Upside Shocks and Breakeven Widening
- **Condition:** UK headline CPI accelerates above consensus to 3.5% driven by persistent services inflation and elevated energy costs, expanding the wedge between market inflation expectations and the BoE’s 2.0% target.
- **Action:** Bullish on UK 5Y and 10Y inflation-linked Gilts (breakeven inflation rates) and FTSE 100 integrated energy producers; bearish on intermediate nominal Gilts and consumer discretionary equities.

## Rule 3: Autumn Budget Deficit Expansion and Fiscal Term Premium Steepening
- **Condition:** Escalating public sector borrowing figures ahead of Chancellor Healey’s Autumn Budget compound fiscal deficit concerns, forcing the UK Debt Management Office (DMO) to scale up gilt auction supply and elevating sovereign term premia.
- **Action:** Bearish on 10Y and 30Y nominal UK Gilts; bullish on UK Gilt 2s10s and 5s30s yield curve steepeners.

## Rule 4: Monthly GDP Resilience and Rate-Cut Extinction
- **Condition:** Monthly UK GDP prints beat expectations (expanding 0.1% in August), confirming underlying economic resilience and removing residual domestic justification for policy accommodation.
- **Action:** Bullish on front-end UK real yields and clearing bank net interest margins; bearish on long-duration sovereign fixed income.

## Rule 5: Sterling Yield Advantage and Divergent FX Appreciation
- **Condition:** Front-end rate hike repricing pushes GBP/USD above 1.3400, capitalizing on diverging monetary policy paths between an actively hawkish BoE and easing cycles at the Federal Reserve and ECB.
- **Action:** Bullish on spot GBP/USD, GBP/EUR, and sterling call options; bearish on EUR/GBP.

## Rule 6: Disappearing Sub-5% Mortgages and Credit Contraction
- **Condition:** Rising benchmark swap rates cause sub-5% fixed-rate mortgage products to disappear across high-street lenders, increasing debt-servicing burdens on refinancing households and constricting private-sector credit growth.
- **Action:** Bearish on UK residential homebuilders, domestic mortgage lenders, and commercial property REITs; bullish on defensive consumer staples and high-yielding utilities.

## Rule 7: Active Quantitative Tightening and Gilt Swap Spread Cheapening
- **Condition:** The Bank of England sustains active gilt portfolio sales alongside balance sheet runoff during heavy DMO supply absorption, congesting primary dealer balance sheets with sovereign issuance.
- **Action:** Bearish on 10Y and 30Y nominal Gilts; bullish on 10Y and 30Y SONIA swap spreads (Gilts cheapening relative to swaps) and sterling repo rates.

## Rule 8: Multinational Large-Cap Outperformance vs. Domestic Cyclicals
- **Condition:** Accelerating headline inflation (3.5%), rising mortgage borrowing costs, and forthcoming budget tax burdens suppress domestic UK consumer demand, while foreign-revenue exposure protects global blue-chips.
- **Action:** Bullish on the FTSE 100 / FTSE 250 equity spread (long FTSE 100, short FTSE 250); bearish on domestically oriented UK mid-cap retailers, leisure operators, and industrial cyclicals.
