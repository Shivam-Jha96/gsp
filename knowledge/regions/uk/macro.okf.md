# United Kingdom Macro Trading Rules

## Rule 1: Higher-for-Longer Policy Stance and Upward Rate Re-pricing
- **Condition:** The Bank of England, via guidance from the Chief Economist and official monetary policy reports, signals ongoing interest rate hikes and maintains an aggressive tightening stance to combat persistent inflation pressures.
- **Action:** Bullish on 1Y and 2Y SONIA swap yields and short-sterling rate expectations; bearish on 3M SONIA interest rate futures and front-end (2Y) nominal Gilts.

## Rule 2: Energy-Led CPI Resurgence and Inflation Breakeven Widening
- **Condition:** UK headline CPI inflation accelerates to 3.6% YoY, driven by soaring global oil prices, fuel pump pressures, and industrial input costs, lifting medium-term consumer and market inflation expectations.
- **Action:** Bullish on UK 5Y and 10Y Gilt breakeven inflation rates and short-dated index-linked Gilts (linkers); bearish on intermediate nominal Gilts and sterling investment-grade corporate credit.

## Rule 3: Sovereign Debt Overhang, Issuance Shifts, and Term Premium Steepening
- **Condition:** The Office for Budget Responsibility (OBR) outlines structural public deficit challenges alongside heavy sovereign debt issuance and changing Gilt maturity profiles, clashing with ongoing fiscal financing needs.
- **Action:** Bullish on UK Gilt 10Y-30Y term premia, 5s30s curve steepener trades, and 10Y Gilt-Bund yield spreads; bearish on outright 10Y and 30Y nominal Gilt prices.

## Rule 4: Terms-of-Trade Deterioration and Sterling Depreciation Pressure
- **Condition:** Sharp increases in global crude oil and commodity import prices degrade the UK's current account balance and terms of trade, challenging domestic currency stability despite rate hikes.
- **Action:** Bullish on USD/GBP (bearish spot GBP/USD) and EUR/GBP; bearish on sterling cross-currency basis swaps.

## Rule 5: Sustained Mortgage Rate Pressure and Housing Sector Contraction
- **Condition:** Base rate hikes to 4% and elevated front-end swap rates push fixed-rate mortgage pricing higher into the winter, depressing property transaction volumes and consumer mortgage approvals.
- **Action:** Bullish on UK private residential rental yields and prime money market fund assets; bearish on UK residential housebuilder equities, domestic mortgage lenders, and residential REITs.

## Rule 6: Stagflationary Wedge and FTSE 100 vs. FTSE 250 Divergence
- **Condition:** Monthly GDP data reveals flat-to-anemic domestic growth alongside accelerating headline inflation prints and high borrowing costs, creating a stagflationary drag on the UK domestic economy.
- **Action:** Bullish on FTSE 100 multi-national energy, commodity, and foreign-earning defensive shares; bearish on domestically focused FTSE 250 consumer cyclicals, retail banking, and commercial property stocks.
