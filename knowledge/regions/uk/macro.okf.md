# United Kingdom Macro Trading Rules

## Rule 1: Conditional Hawkish Hold and Front-End Rate Repricing
- **Condition:** The Bank of England holds the Bank Rate steady while the MPC explicitly signals potential Q4 rate hikes or an extended high-for-longer stance driven by persistent energy-led price pressures, wiping out market pricing for near-term easing.
- **Action:** Bearish on 2Y UK Gilts and short-dated SONIA futures; bullish on front-month SONIA implied volatility and 1Y forward rate agreements (FRAs).

## Rule 2: Energy-Driven Headline CPI Surge and Breakeven Widening
- **Condition:** Crude oil and wholesale natural gas price spikes push headline UK CPI back toward 3.1%–3.5% even as domestic consumption slows, driving a wedge between headline inflation and underlying disinflation trends.
- **Action:** Bullish on UK 5Y and 10Y inflation-linked Gilts (breakevens) and FTSE 100 integrated oil and gas producers; bearish on intermediate nominal Gilts and consumer discretionary equities.

## Rule 3: Autumn Budget Deficit Concerns and Gilt Curve Steepening
- **Condition:** Fiscal watchdog warnings from the OBR and IFS highlight surging public sector borrowing requirements ahead of the Autumn Budget, forcing the DMO to increase long-end gilt issuance and pushing up fiscal term premia.
- **Action:** Bearish on 10Y and 30Y nominal UK Gilts; bullish on UK Gilt 2s10s and 5s30s yield curve steepeners.

## Rule 4: Monthly GDP Resilience and Easing Cycle Deferral
- **Condition:** Monthly UK GDP prints beat consensus expectations (e.g., expanding 0.1% or higher month-over-month), demonstrating underlying economic resilience and forcing swap markets to eliminate remaining rate-cut projections for the calendar year.
- **Action:** Bullish on front-end UK real yields and commercial bank net interest margins; bearish on duration-sensitive sovereign bonds.

## Rule 5: Sterling Yield Advantage and Bullish FX Continuation
- **Condition:** Hawkish MPC forward guidance contrasts with active monetary easing cycles at the Federal Reserve and ECB, widening UK-US and UK-EZ front-end rate differentials and pushing spot GBP/USD above key technical resistance levels.
- **Action:** Bullish on spot GBP/USD, GBP/EUR, and sterling call options; bearish on EUR/GBP.

## Rule 6: Mortgage Refinancing Friction and Domestic Credit Contraction
- **Condition:** Elevated front-end Gilt yields and sustained restrictive policy rates keep 2-year and 5-year fixed mortgage rates elevated, accelerating household debt-servicing costs as fixed-term borrowing transitions to prevailing market rates.
- **Action:** Bearish on UK residential homebuilders, domestic retail lenders, and commercial property REITs; bullish on defensive consumer staples and high-yielding utilities.

## Rule 7: Active Quantitative Tightening and Gilt Swap Spread Cheapening
- **Condition:** The Bank of England maintains active gilt sales alongside reinvestment runoff during its annual QT framework review, compounding primary issuance from the DMO and saturating private balance sheet capacity.
- **Action:** Bearish on 10Y and 30Y nominal Gilts; bullish on 10Y and 30Y SONIA swap spreads (Gilts cheapening relative to swaps) and sterling repo rates.

## Rule 8: Multinational Large-Cap Outperformance vs. Domestic Cyclicals
- **Condition:** Elevated domestic borrowing costs and high energy input costs depress UK domestic demand while global commodity prices and weaker non-sterling currency impacts support overseas revenues for large-cap multinationals.
- **Action:** Bullish on the FTSE 100 / FTSE 250 equity spread (long FTSE 100, short FTSE 250); bearish on domestically focused UK mid-cap industrials and retail operators.
