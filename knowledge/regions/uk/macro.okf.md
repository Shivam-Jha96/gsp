# United Kingdom Macro Trading Rules

## Rule 1: Bank Rate Hike Re-Pricing and Front-End Curve Bear Flattening
- **Condition:** Bank of England rate-setters leave December rate hikes on the table and warn that higher prices will filter through, forcing markets to price out earlier easing expectations and re-price front-end rate risk.
- **Action:** Bullish on 3M SONIA interest rate futures and front-end (2Y) swap yields; bearish on short-term nominal Gilts and aggressive rate-cut positioning.

## Rule 2: Sticky CPI Resurgence and Inflation Breakeven Widening
- **Condition:** UK headline Consumer Prices Index (CPI) inflation ticks higher to 3.6% alongside official Bank of England warnings that inflation will rise further in upcoming prints, maintaining structural cost-push pressures.
- **Action:** Bullish on UK 5Y and 10Y Gilt breakeven inflation rates and short-dated index-linked Gilts (linkers); bearish on intermediate nominal Gilts and sterling investment-grade corporate credit.

## Rule 3: Sovereign Fiscal Deficit Pressures and Term Premium Steepening
- **Condition:** The Office for Budget Responsibility (OBR) Economic and Fiscal Outlook outlines ongoing structural public sector net borrowing challenges and heavy Gilt issuance profiles interacting with market risk premia.
- **Action:** Bullish on UK Gilt 10Y-30Y term premia, 5s30s curve steepener trades, and 10Y Gilt-Bund yield spreads; bearish on outright 10Y and 30Y nominal Gilt prices.

## Rule 4: Terms-of-Trade Deterioration and Sterling Depreciation Pressure
- **Condition:** Stubborn domestic inflation differentials relative to global counterparts and persistent external deficit vulnerabilities maintain downward valuation pressure on sterling as rate expectations shift.
- **Action:** Bullish on USD/GBP (bearish spot GBP/USD) and EUR/GBP; bearish on sterling cross-currency basis swaps.

## Rule 5: Remortgage Cliff and Consumer Housing Sector Contraction
- **Condition:** Millions in the UK brace to remortgage on significantly worse deals as elevated base rates filter through to consumer lending, compressing household disposable income and cooling transaction volumes.
- **Action:** Bullish on UK private residential rental yields and prime money market fund assets; bearish on UK residential housebuilder equities, domestic mortgage lenders, and residential REITs.

## Rule 6: Stagflationary Wedge and FTSE 100 vs. FTSE 250 Divergence
- **Condition:** Anemic monthly GDP growth prints combine with elevated consumer price inflation and restrictive baseline borrowing costs to create a severe domestic economic drag.
- **Action:** Bullish on FTSE 100 multi-national energy, commodity, and foreign-earning defensive shares; bearish on domestically focused FTSE 250 consumer cyclicals, retail banking, and commercial property stocks.
