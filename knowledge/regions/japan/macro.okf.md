# Japan Macro Trading Rules

## Rule 1: Sovereign Debt Friction, Twist-Steepening, and BOJ Rinban Purchase Overhang
- **Condition:** The Bank of Japan weighs tapering bond purchases alongside normalization while political friction over debt-servicing costs and fiscal borrowing pressure push 20Y-40Y JGB yields to record levels, raising the threat of emergency, unscheduled Rinban market operations.
- **Action:** Bullish on 10Y-30Y JGB curve twist-steepeners, front-end JGB discount bills, and Japanese money center banks; Bearish on ultra-long JGB paper (30Y-40Y) and high-leverage J-REITs.

## Rule 2: Persistent FX Carry Disconnect and Ministry of Finance Intervention Asymmetry
- **Condition:** USD/JPY and cross-yen pairs remain elevated despite firm domestic inflation metrics and hawkish central bank commentary, driven by wide nominal yield differentials and unhedged foreign carry trades, escalating unilateral MoF currency intervention risks.
- **Action:** Bullish on tactical spot JPY, long JPY implied volatility (gamma), and short USD/JPY risk reversals; Bearish on unhedged JPY carry-funded positions and export-dependent equities vulnerable to rapid yen appreciation.

## Rule 3: Fiscal Subsidy Distortions vs. Imported Energy Cost-Push Inflation
- **Condition:** Headline consumer price inflation is artificially suppressed by temporary government utility and fuel subsidies, while geopolitical tensions and rising oil prices reignite pipeline inflation and upstream import costs.
- **Action:** Bullish on general trading companies (*sogo shosha*), domestic inflation-linked bonds (JGBi), and upstream commodity importers; Bearish on unhedged electric utilities with rigid retail rate caps and margin-squeezed food and staple processors.

## Rule 4: Capex-Led GDP Resilience Diverging from Household Consumption Stagnation
- **Condition:** Headline real GDP displays moderate growth driven by corporate investment in automation and slight capex improvements, while household consumption remains constrained under sluggish real wage growth.
- **Action:** Bullish on factory automation equipment makers, specialized industrial machinery, and advanced non-residential engineering contractors; Bearish on domestic mass-market retail chains, department store operators, and consumer discretionary cyclicals.

## Rule 5: TSE Governance Mandates, Strategic AI Capex, and Inflow Momentum
- **Condition:** Tokyo Stock Exchange enforcement of capital efficiency (mandatory PBR remediation, dividend payout hikes, and cross-shareholding unwinds) coincides with sustained investment into Japan's domestic semiconductor ecosystem and AI infrastructure.
- **Action:** Bullish on semiconductor manufacturing equipment (SME) champions, specialized silicon materials suppliers, and cash-rich corporate restructuring targets; Bearish on governance holdouts with entrenched cross-holdings and capital-destructive balance sheets.

## Rule 6: Hawkish Policy Repricing, Growth Factor De-Rating, and Value Rotation
- **Condition:** Rising front-end JGB yields and accelerated policy normalization expectations prompt risk-off valuation compressions in high-multiple growth equities, triggering aggressive capital rotation toward domestic asset-sensitive value sectors.
- **Action:** Bullish on TOPIX Value relative to TOPIX Growth, major non-life insurers, and ultra-short corporate commercial paper; Bearish on high-P/E speculative technology equities and long-duration growth indices.
