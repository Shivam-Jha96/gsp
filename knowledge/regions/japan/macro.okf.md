# Japan Macro Trading Rules

## Rule 1: Ultra-Long Sovereign Debt Stress, Fiscal Deficit Concerns, and Discretionary Rinban Interventions
- **Condition:** Persistent BOJ policy normalization bets alongside fiscal expansion push yields on 20Y–40Y Japanese Government Bonds (JGBs) to multi-decade highs, steepening the sovereign curve and stoking market anticipation of emergency BOJ bond-buying operations (*Rinban*) to suppress disorderly yield spikes.
- **Action:** Bullish on front-end JGB discount bills, domestic money center commercial banks, and tactical 10Y-30Y curve flattener unwinds; Bearish on ultra-long sovereign paper (30Y–40Y JGBs), long-duration domestic bond funds, and interest rate-sensitive J-REITs.

## Rule 2: Domestic CPI Overshoots, Hawkish BOJ Re-Pricing, and Systematic FX Carry Unwinds
- **Condition:** Core CPI prints consistently exceed the 2% target driven by imported commodity costs and service sector price pass-through, narrowing cross-border yield differentials and triggering rapid short-covering rallies in the Japanese Yen and violent unwinds of short-JPY carry trades.
- **Action:** Bullish on spot JPY, long JPY implied volatility (gamma), and short USD/JPY risk reversals; Bearish on high-beta cross-yen carry pairs (AUD/JPY, NZD/JPY, MXN/JPY) and unhedged export-heavy automotive manufacturers.

## Rule 3: Imported Energy Cost-Push Shocks vs. Government Utility Subsidy Distortions
- **Condition:** Resurgent international crude oil and LNG prices amplify import bills via residual currency depreciation, accelerating headline inflation while volatile municipal and national utility tariff subsidies distort underlying consumer price trajectories.
- **Action:** Bullish on diversified Japanese trading conglomerates (*sogo shosha*), domestic inflation-linked bonds (JGBi), and upstream petroleum refiners; Bearish on regulated electric and gas utilities facing cost-adjustment lags and domestic food processors unable to pass on wholesale input costs.

## Rule 4: Domestic Private Consumption Weakness vs. Upgraded Enterprise Capex Divergence
- **Condition:** Quarterly real GDP growth prints miss consensus due to sustained contraction in real wages and sluggish private household consumption, while corporate capital expenditure is revised upward on robust semiconductor manufacturing capacity, factory automation, and enterprise software investment.
- **Action:** Bullish on domestic semiconductor equipment manufacturers (SPE), precision industrial automation producers, and enterprise digital transformation integrators; Bearish on mass-market apparel retailers, department store operators, and domestic consumer discretionary cyclicals.

## Rule 5: Tokyo Stock Exchange Governance Mandates and Balance-Sheet Optimization
- **Condition:** Tokyo Stock Exchange compliance pressures targeting companies trading below book value (PBR < 1.0x) drive corporate boards to actively dismantle strategic cross-shareholdings, institute aggressive return-on-equity (ROE) targets, and announce record share repurchases.
- **Action:** Bullish on cash-generative low-PBR heavy industrials, capital goods firms with accelerating net buyback yields, and financial holding companies divesting legacy corporate equity stakes; Bearish on corporate governance laggards with persistent sub-5% ROE and defensive antitakeover provisions.

## Rule 6: Hawkish Policy Rate Re-anchoring, Equity Discount Rate Shocks, and Value-over-Growth Rotation
- **Condition:** Hawkish BOJ forward guidance and ascending terminal policy rate expectations trigger upward revisions to domestic risk-free discount rates, sparking multiple compression in long-duration growth equities and prompting systematic sector rotation into asset-sensitive value stocks.
- **Action:** Bullish on TOPIX Value relative to TOPIX Growth, multi-line property and casualty insurers, and regional banking groups; Bearish on high-multiple growth equities, the Nikkei/TOPIX (NT) ratio, and domestic high-valuation SaaS providers.

## Rule 7: Institutional Asset Repatriation and FX-Hedged Sovereign Disinvestment
- **Condition:** Structural yield increases in 10Y–30Y JGBs, coupled with prohibitively elevated foreign exchange hedging costs on US Dollar and Euro assets, invert the hedged yield premium of overseas fixed income and compel Japanese life insurers and pension funds to repatriate capital into domestic credit and debt instruments.
- **Action:** Bullish on high-grade yen-denominated corporate credit spreads, primary 10Y–20Y domestic debt absorption, and domestic fixed-income mutual fund inflows; Bearish on FX-hedged US Treasuries, European core sovereign paper, and offshore fixed-income investment trusts (*toshin*).

## Rule 8: Regional Asian Intermediate Export Impulses vs. Domestic Margin Compression
- **Condition:** Resilient manufacturing and export momentum across East Asia bolsters cross-border order books for Japanese specialized chemicals, high-precision industrial inputs, and advanced components, while domestic-bound intermediate suppliers suffer severe margin erosion from unhedged raw material imports.
- **Action:** Bullish on high-spec electronic materials producers, specialized fine chemical exporters, and intra-Asia freight shipping lines; Bearish on domestic low-margin intermediate fabricators and unhedged raw-materials-dependent domestic suppliers.
