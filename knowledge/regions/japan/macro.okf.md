# Japan Macro Trading Rules

## Rule 1: Ultra-Long Sovereign Debt Friction, Political Fiscal Resistance, and Rinban Interventions
- **Condition:** Bank of Japan policy normalization and quantitative tightening collide with political resistance over escalating debt-servicing burdens as 20Y–40Y JGB yields breach multi-year and record highs, heightening market expectations for unscheduled Rinban liquidity operations to contain tail-risk yield curve steepening.
- **Action:** Bullish on 10Y-30Y JGB curve steepeners, front-end JGB discount bills, and domestic money center banks; Bearish on ultra-long sovereign paper (30Y–40Y JGBs) and rate-sensitive J-REITs.

## Rule 2: FX Carry Unwind Asymmetry and Central Bank Divergence
- **Condition:** Persistent domestic core inflation and steady BOJ normalization signals compress sovereign yield differentials against global central banks, catalyzing sharp, asymmetric unwinds of unhedged cross-yen carry positions alongside elevated Ministry of Finance currency defense vigilance.
- **Action:** Bullish on tactical spot JPY, long JPY implied volatility (gamma), and short USD/JPY risk reversals; Bearish on high-beta cross-yen carry pairs (AUD/JPY, NZD/JPY) and export-dependent automotive manufacturers.

## Rule 3: Fiscal Subsidy Disinflation Distortions vs. Upstream Energy Cost-Push Pressures
- **Condition:** Headline CPI experiences artificial deceleration due to reinstated government utility and fuel subsidies, while geopolitical tensions and elevated crude benchmarks sustain pipeline import costs and corporate goods price pressures.
- **Action:** Bullish on Japanese general trading companies (*sogo shosha*), domestic inflation-linked bonds (JGBi), and upstream energy/commodity distributors; Bearish on regulated electric utilities constrained by retail tariff caps and margin-squeezed food processors.

## Rule 4: Capex-Led Output Resilience Diverging from Household Consumption Fragility
- **Condition:** Real GDP exhibits uneven quarterly expansion driven primarily by upgraded corporate capital expenditures in automation, semiconductor capacity onshoring, and digital transformation, while private household consumption remains suppressed by sluggish real wage gains.
- **Action:** Bullish on factory automation equipment makers, specialized industrial machinery, and domestic non-residential engineering contractors; Bearish on domestic mass-market retail chains, department store operators, and consumer discretionary cyclicals.

## Rule 5: TSE Governance Mandates, Semiconductor Onshoring, and Strategic AI Capex
- **Condition:** Tokyo Stock Exchange balance-sheet reform directives (mandated PBR remediation, accelerated cross-shareholding unwinds, and record share buybacks) converge with state-subsidized semiconductor fabrication initiatives and enterprise AI infrastructure buildouts.
- **Action:** Bullish on semiconductor manufacturing equipment (SME) champions, advanced electronic materials suppliers, and undervalued cash-rich corporate restructuring targets; Bearish on corporate governance holdouts with low return on equity (ROE) and entrenched cross-holdings.

## Rule 6: Hawkish Policy Acceleration, Equity Duration De-Rating, and Factor Rotation
- **Condition:** Upside surprises in domestic inflation indicators and normalization momentum firm BOJ terminal rate pricing, forcing broad equity valuation multiple compression and driving institutional capital rotation out of long-duration growth assets into rate-sensitive domestic value.
- **Action:** Bullish on TOPIX Value relative to TOPIX Growth, major non-life insurance groups, and mega-bank financial holding companies; Bearish on high-P/E speculative technology equities and high-duration growth benchmarks (Nikkei 225 relative to TOPIX).

## Rule 7: Institutional Portfolio Repatriation and Foreign Fixed Income Disinvestment
- **Condition:** Ultra-long JGB yields reaching structural multi-decade highs alter liability-matching economics for domestic life insurers and pension funds (GPIF), prompting accelerated capital repatriation away from currency-hedged foreign sovereign debt back into domestic fixed income.
- **Action:** Bullish on domestic long-duration corporate credit spreads, primary corporate issuance clearance rates, and medium-term JPY base liquidity; Bearish on currency-hedged US Treasuries, European sovereign paper, and foreign fixed income investment trusts.
