# Japan Macro Trading Rules

## Rule 1: Ultra-Long Sovereign Debt Friction, Political Fiscal Resistance, and Rinban Interventions
- **Condition:** The Bank of Japan's rate-hike and quantitative tightening trajectory collides with political resistance over escalating debt-servicing costs as 20Y–40Y JGB yields breach multi-year highs, intensifying market expectations for unscheduled Rinban bond-buying operations to curb tail-risk yield spikes.
- **Action:** Bullish on 10Y-30Y JGB curve twist-steepeners, Japanese money center banks, and front-end JGB discount bills; Bearish on ultra-long sovereign paper (30Y–40Y JGBs) and high-leverage J-REITs.

## Rule 2: FX Carry Unwind Asymmetry and MoF Intervention Defense
- **Condition:** Persistent domestic inflation and hawkish BOJ normalization signaling elevate the probability of narrowing yield differentials and unilateral Ministry of Finance currency defense, catalyzing violent, asymmetric unwinds of unhedged cross-yen carry trades.
- **Action:** Bullish on tactical spot JPY, long JPY implied volatility (gamma), and short USD/JPY risk reversals; Bearish on high-beta cross-yen carry pairs (AUD/JPY, NZD/JPY) and export-dependent automakers vulnerable to rapid yen appreciation.

## Rule 3: Fiscal Subsidy Disinflation Distortions vs. Upstream Energy Cost-Push Pressures
- **Condition:** Headline consumer inflation slows artificially due to re-instituted government utility and fuel subsidies, while geopolitical tensions and rising global crude prices sustain pipeline imported inflation and corporate goods pricing pressures.
- **Action:** Bullish on Japanese general trading companies (*sogo shosha*), domestic inflation-linked bonds (JGBi), and upstream commodity distributors; Bearish on regulated electric utilities constrained by retail tariff caps and margin-squeezed food processors.

## Rule 4: Capex-Led Output Resilience Diverging from Household Consumption Fragility
- **Condition:** Real GDP prints positive quarterly momentum driven primarily by upgraded business capital expenditures in digital transformation, factory automation, and capacity onshoring, while household consumption remains constrained by lagging real wage growth.
- **Action:** Bullish on factory automation equipment makers, specialized industrial machinery, and non-residential engineering contractors; Bearish on domestic mass-market retail chains, department store operators, and consumer discretionary cyclicals.

## Rule 5: TSE Governance Mandates, Semiconductor Onshoring, and Strategic AI Capex
- **Condition:** Tokyo Stock Exchange balance-sheet remediation pressures (mandated PBR elevation, accelerated cross-shareholding unwinds, and record share buybacks) converge with sovereign-backed semiconductor fabrication and AI infrastructure investments.
- **Action:** Bullish on semiconductor manufacturing equipment (SME) champions, advanced electronic materials suppliers, and undervalued cash-rich restructuring targets; Bearish on corporate governance holdouts with low return on equity (ROE) and entrenched cross-holdings.

## Rule 6: Hawkish Policy Acceleration, Equity Duration De-Rating, and Factor Rotation
- **Condition:** Upside surprises in economic normalization indicators and resilient GDP growth accelerate BOJ terminal rate pricing, forcing broad equity valuation multiple compression and triggering aggressive institutional rotation out of long-duration growth assets into rate-sensitive domestic value.
- **Action:** Bullish on TOPIX Value relative to TOPIX Growth, major non-life insurance groups, and domestic financial holding companies; Bearish on high-P/E speculative technology equities and high-duration growth benchmarks (Nikkei 225 relative to TOPIX).
