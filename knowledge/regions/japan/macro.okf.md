# Japan Macro Trading Rules

## Rule 1: Sovereign Debt Friction, Twist-Steepening, and BOJ Rinban Purchase Overhang
- **Condition:** The Bank of Japan pursues policy rate hikes and quantitative tapering while political resistance to rising debt-servicing costs and fiscal deficit concerns drive 20Y-40Y JGB yields to multi-year highs, raising the probability of emergency, unscheduled Rinban bond-buying operations.
- **Action:** Bullish on 10Y-30Y JGB curve twist-steepeners, front-end JGB discount bills, and Japanese money center banks; Bearish on ultra-long JGB paper (30Y-40Y) and high-leverage J-REITs.

## Rule 2: Persistent FX Carry Disconnect and Ministry of Finance Intervention Asymmetry
- **Condition:** USD/JPY and high-beta cross-yen pairs trade at elevated levels driven by structural nominal yield differentials and unhedged foreign carry trades, while domestic inflation pressures amplify the threat of unilateral MoF currency intervention.
- **Action:** Bullish on tactical spot JPY, long JPY implied volatility (gamma), and short USD/JPY risk reversals; Bearish on unhedged JPY carry-funded positions and export-dependent equities vulnerable to rapid yen appreciation.

## Rule 3: Fiscal Subsidy Distortions vs. Imported Energy Cost-Push Inflation
- **Condition:** Headline consumer price inflation is artificially suppressed by temporary government utility and fuel subsidies, while geopolitical tensions and rising global crude prices reignite pipeline imported inflation and upstream wholesale costs.
- **Action:** Bullish on general trading companies (*sogo shosha*), domestic inflation-linked bonds (JGBi), and upstream commodity importers; Bearish on unhedged electric utilities with rigid retail rate caps and margin-squeezed food and staple processors.

## Rule 4: Capex-Led Growth Divergence from Sluggish Private Consumption
- **Condition:** Headline real GDP prints moderate expansion sustained primarily by corporate upgrades in automation, digital transformation, and industrial capex, while real wage stagnation dampens private household consumption.
- **Action:** Bullish on factory automation equipment makers, specialized industrial machinery, and advanced non-residential engineering contractors; Bearish on domestic mass-market retail chains, department store operators, and consumer discretionary cyclicals.

## Rule 5: TSE Governance Mandates, Strategic AI Capex, and Inflow Momentum
- **Condition:** Tokyo Stock Exchange compliance pressures (mandated PBR remediation, dividend expansion, and cross-shareholding unwinds) converge with multi-billion-dollar domestic semiconductor fabrication and AI infrastructure capital expenditures.
- **Action:** Bullish on semiconductor manufacturing equipment (SME) champions, specialized silicon materials suppliers, and cash-rich corporate restructuring targets; Bearish on governance holdouts with entrenched cross-holdings and low ROIC balance sheets.

## Rule 6: Hawkish Policy Repricing, Growth Factor De-Rating, and Value Rotation
- **Condition:** Accelerating central bank normalization bets and climbing front-end yields trigger equity de-rating across high-valuation equities, forcing institutional rotation out of long-duration growth into rate-sensitive domestic value sectors.
- **Action:** Bullish on TOPIX Value relative to TOPIX Growth, major non-life insurers, and ultra-short corporate commercial paper; Bearish on high-P/E speculative technology equities and long-duration growth indices.
