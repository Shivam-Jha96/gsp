# Japan Macro Trading Rules

## Rule 1: Ultra-Long Sovereign Debt Friction, Political Fiscal Pushback, and Rinban Interventions
- **Condition:** Bank of Japan policy normalization and quantitative tightening collide with political resistance over mounting debt-servicing burdens as 20Y–40Y JGB yields breach multi-decade or record highs, triggering market expectations for discretionary Rinban bond-purchase operations to curb disorderly tail-risk curve steepening.
- **Action:** Bullish on 10Y-30Y JGB curve steepeners, front-end JGB discount bills, and domestic money center banks; Bearish on ultra-long sovereign paper (30Y–40Y JGBs) and rate-sensitive J-REITs.

## Rule 2: FX Carry Unwind Asymmetry, Rate Repricing, and Currency Vigilance
- **Condition:** Persistent Japanese core-core inflation and hawkish BOJ terminal rate repricing compress sovereign yield spreads against major central banks, generating asymmetric unwind pressure on unhedged short-JPY carry trades amid heightened Ministry of Finance intervention risk.
- **Action:** Bullish on tactical spot JPY, long JPY implied volatility (gamma), and short USD/JPY risk reversals; Bearish on high-beta cross-yen carry pairs (AUD/JPY, NZD/JPY) and export-dependent automotive manufacturers.

## Rule 3: Geopolitical Energy Cost-Push Pressures vs. Government Utility Subsidy Distortions
- **Condition:** Escalating Middle Eastern or global supply-chain tensions drive crude oil benchmarks and pipeline import prices higher, while headline CPI print volatility is artificially dampened by the cyclical reinstatement and expiration of state utility and fuel subsidies.
- **Action:** Bullish on Japanese general trading houses (*sogo shosha*), domestic inflation-linked bonds (JGBi), and upstream commodity/refining distributors; Bearish on regulated electric utilities subject to retail rate caps and margin-compressed consumer packaged food producers.

## Rule 4: Structural Capex Resilience Diverging from Household Real Consumption Stagnation
- **Condition:** Headline real GDP displays sluggish, below-potential quarterly growth constrained by stagnant household real wages and depressed consumer sentiment, even as business gross fixed capital formation expands robustly in domestic semiconductor fab construction, factory automation, and digital software infrastructure.
- **Action:** Bullish on industrial robotics manufacturers, specialized semiconductor fabrication equipment (SME) producers, and non-residential engineering contractors; Bearish on domestic mass-market retail chains, regional railway operators, and consumer discretionary cyclicals.

## Rule 5: TSE Balance-Sheet Mandates, Share Repurchases, and Corporate Governance Reform
- **Condition:** Tokyo Stock Exchange compliance deadlines enforce capital efficiency remediation for entities trading persistently below book value (PBR < 1.0x), prompting record corporate equity retirements, strategic dividend hikes, and the accelerated dissolution of cross-shareholding structures.
- **Action:** Bullish on undervalued cash-rich corporate restructuring targets, capital goods manufacturers with high buyback yields, and financial holding companies unwinding cross-holdings; Bearish on corporate governance holdouts exhibiting sub-cost-of-capital ROE and entrenched cross-shareholder networks.

## Rule 6: Hawkish Terminal Rate Re-anchoring, Duration Compression, and Value Rotation
- **Condition:** Consecutive above-forecast wage growth metrics and resilient service sector inflation prompt the BOJ to front-load policy rate hikes, triggering broad equity valuation multiple compression and a decisive institutional rotation out of high-duration growth assets into balance-sheet asset-sensitive value sectors.
- **Action:** Bullish on TOPIX Value relative to TOPIX Growth, multi-line domestic casualty/property insurers, and regional banking indexes; Bearish on high-P/E speculative technology software, high-duration Nikkei 225 growth constituents, and benchmark Nikkei/TOPIX (NT) ratios.

## Rule 7: Institutional Asset Repatriation and Foreign Sovereign Debt Disinvestment
- **Condition:** Elevated ultra-long domestic risk-free yields alter liability-matching solvency economics for institutional life insurers and public pensions (GPIF), prompting systematic disinvestment from foreign currency-hedged sovereign bonds in favor of higher-yielding domestic paper.
- **Action:** Bullish on domestic long-duration corporate credit spreads, primary domestic corporate bond clearing rates, and medium-term JPY base liquidity; Bearish on currency-hedged US Treasuries, European core sovereign debt, and foreign fixed income investment trusts (*toshin*).

## Rule 8: Regional Asian Supply-Chain Reorientation and Export Demand Impulses
- **Condition:** Shifting cyclical demand from Mainland China and regional Asian trading partners interacts with Japanese advanced industrial export cycles, creating divergent performance between global supply-chain suppliers and domestic-facing manufacturing.
- **Action:** Bullish on advanced electronic materials suppliers, specialized chemical exporters, and maritime container carriers; Bearish on low-margin domestic industrial component makers lacking pricing power over imported raw inputs.
