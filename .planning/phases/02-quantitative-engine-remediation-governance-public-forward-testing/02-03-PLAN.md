# Plan 02-03: Public Forward-Testing Ledger & Central Bank Feed Ingestion

## Objective
Launch an append-only forward-test ledger for live out-of-sample paper trading telemetry, ingest direct official Central Bank RSS feeds across all four hubs, and integrate forward-test metrics into the UI dashboard.

## Tasks
1. Integrate direct official Central Bank RSS feeds (Federal Reserve, RBI, Bank of England, Bank of Japan) into `src/config/market_registry.py`.
2. Update unit test `tests/unit/test_classifier_and_registry.py` to validate both Google News and Central Bank feed structure.
3. Build `src/signal_engine/forward_tester.py` supporting ledger initialization, execution signal recording, and summary metrics calculation.
4. Initialize `reports/forward_test_ledger.csv` with standard telemetry schema.
5. Add unit test suite `tests/unit/test_forward_tester.py` covering recording, deduplication, and metrics generation.
6. Integrate collapsible Forward-Testing Ledger card in `src/ui/app.py` displaying live out-of-sample performance telemetry.
